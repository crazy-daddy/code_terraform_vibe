## Where phase 12 left things

- Power `trim` `stopMargin` takes `step` besides a number: in `#trimReeval` the second stop of a candidate rated r needs `max(hist) × margin + r` (`needK(r)`, a function of the candidate now), a numeric `stopMargin` keeps `max(hist) × stopMargin`. `trimMargins(p, quietWindows)` lets the string `"step"` through the numeric check; the `needs quietWindows > 0` rule applies to it too. `--standin power=trim,stopMargin=step` reaches the constructor as the string (the `k=v` parser keeps a non-numeric value). `report()` does not emit `stopMargin`.
- A/B on 1220, 1 h, three trimmed rows one after another against the phase 11 real run (`scripts/` unchanged between 6a601b3 and b354494, `latest_scripts` identical). `stopMargin=step`: `restarted 19, restopped 14, evals 58`, the floor holds at 4,900 W (`oil_generator_7` is never cut), no restart in the window after a cut. `stopMargin=1.25`: `15 / 10 / 58`, same floor, the night after the first day peak cuts all five at once. `reevalTicks=300,quietWindows=6,stopMargin=step`: `10 / 10 / 117`, the day restart lands before the peak (6,168 W against 7,272 W), the first-night battery low is 8,856 Wh against 7,711 Wh, the 0.75 h low is the same. TP within 0.01 % in every row, no brownout, no warning.
- Credits: the `spire_25` sale (525,000; glass 4,000 and silicon 20,000) lands at 0.417 h real, 0.75 h in the 300-tick row and not within the hour in the two 600-tick rows (silicon 19,964 shipped at the end). Silicon is mined ore, so it is the vehicle timing drift (open item 5 of the previous handoff), not the power served. The two 600-tick rows are metric-identical up to 0.5 h and diverge after the first night's cut.
- Tests: `test/standins.test.mjs` +1 (`step`: a cut the numeric rule would make stays, a lower peak cuts, a peak inside the step band does not restart, a higher one does) and two lines in the `stopMargin` validation test (`step` without `quietWindows` throws, with it `trim.stopMargin === "step"`); 67 green with `node --test "devtools/simulation/headless/test/*.test.mjs"`. The reviewer's one finding: the refused-stop and dead-script branches of `#trimReeval` are not exercised under `step` (they are unchanged by the phase and covered by the earlier tests with a numeric `stopMargin`).

Pitfalls that still apply: `pass(t)` counts from the run's start, archive ages use `state.tickCount`; `--only-scripts` pins `keep` and a stand-in skips kept slots; validate params for value before any side effect (the constructor, not `#setup`, for anything that must precede the archive seeds); the watcher does not cover `devtools/`; a group that is off or stood in has a boundary (`GROUPS[g].produces` / `consumes`) and the A/B gaps come from there first, name them in ab_results.md rather than tuning the stand-in; `final_save.json` is the raw state (no `state` wrapper) and is UTF-8; `run.mjs --hours` and `summary` hours are clock hours (36,000 ticks), planet-hours (250 ticks) are the construction and day-cycle unit; a generator's `generated` share is demand, not capacity, and `subnet.consumed` is demand, not served load (the re-evaluation relies on that); the stand-in's `standins:` lines go to the run's stdout, not `console.log`; a run deploys the repo's working-tree `scripts/`, so uncommitted script edits from a parallel session end up in the run (keep `latest_scripts` identical across a pair: `diff -rq --strip-trailing-cr out/<a>/latest_scripts out/<b>/latest_scripts`); the `k=v` parser makes `null` the string `"null"`, leave a key out for its default.
