## Where phase 14 left things

- **Left-over sweep** (`standins.mjs` `sweepValue`, `#sweep`, `#deadIds`): a stopped slot, the machine that owns it and every machine `scale` undeployed are dead ids; their archive records go by shape (whole key `<prefix>.<id>` / `<prefix>:<id>`; id-keyed entries at depth 1 and 2; records whose owner field, `sweep.fields`, names a dead id, at depth 0 to 2). Arrays untouched, emptied parents kept. Runs at the end of setup, after a trim stop or re-stop, every 100-tick flow pass, after a revival. A key hit on three passes in a row is `sweepLive` (left alone, warned once). Skipped: `sweep.skipKeys`, `standin.terraforming`, the stubs, seeded `archive` keys. `summary.standins.leftovers`, `sweepLive`.
- **Revival guard** (`#revivePass`): every pass, a slot in a stopped group that counts for the allowance again is stopped again (`revived`), a refused re-stop counted apart (`reviveRefused`), three warnings per slot. `trim` exempt. Not one revival in the five A/B rows.
- **Profile** (`profiles/code_terraform.json`, `scenario.mjs` `loadProfile` / `validateProfile`, `--script-set PATH|none`, scenario field `profile`): the `off` stubs, the orchestrator-off budget refresh, the orchestrator role markers, the mover's request protocol (keys and stale ticks) and the sweep defaults moved out of code. `MODES` stopping modes take `sweep` (default true); `mover` has `needs: "requests"` and fails validation under `none`. `summary.standins.profile`. What still encodes this repo and why: the "Profile" section's second table.
- **A/B on 1631** (five rows): the sweep acts (keys named per row), no revival, no error, no wall-time cost. The O2 −14 % gap is `scale`'s own (identical with and without the sweep). The fabricator's units-sent-per-hour swing 30 to 57 across configurations in both directions around real (43); the `machine_frame` demand is present in every run, so the phase 14 field report's "lost demand" was timing drift, not a left-over. Sweeping `script.parked` turned the stopped mixers into "stray dark" for the parking manager (80 warnings); the profile now skips that key (the rows ran before the change).
- Tests: 80 green with `node --test "devtools/simulation/headless/test/*.test.mjs"` (+10: `sweepValue` rules, setup sweep and report, `sweep: false` per group and global with `skipKeys` / `extraKeys`, the periodic sweep, the live-writer streak, the revival guard and its refused branch, the trim sweep, the `scale` sweep, the profile). Reviewed with `cavecrew-reviewer` (9 risks, 4 questions, 6 nits; all fixed or documented: per-group `sweep` applies to listed groups only, documented in scenarios.md).
- Docs: standins.md "Left-overs" and "Profile", scenarios.md (`profile`, `sweep`, `--script-set`, Output rows), README (profiles row, `--script-set none` example), dev_workflow §10b, TODO phase 14 line, ab_results "Phase 14".
