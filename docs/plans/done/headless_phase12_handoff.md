## Where phase 11 left things

- Power `trim` takes `stopMargin` (default null = `margin`): `#trimReeval`'s second stop needs `max(hist) × stopMargin`, the restart keeps `peak × margin`. Validated in the constructor by `trimMargins(p, quietWindows)` after `quietWindows` (`margin` finite > 0; `stopMargin` finite and ≥ `margin`, a lower one would stop a generator the next window restarts; an explicit `stopMargin` with `quietWindows` 0 throws, as `floor` `keepMin` does). `--standin power=trim,stopMargin=null` is rejected: the `k=v` parser makes the string `"null"` (same for `reevalTicks=null`); leave the key out for the default.
- A/B on 1220, 1 h (two more load cycles than phases 7 to 10), each run alone, both on the 6a601b3 scripts: `--standin power=trim,quietWindows=3,floor=keepMin,margin=1.05,stopMargin=1.1`. The floor fires every night (oil generators cut one by one, 7 of 12 kept after the warm-up), every day peak restarts all: `restarted 19, restopped 14, evals 58`, no brownout, ghosts 0 at the end. TP 673,838 against 674,812 real (0.14 %). Credits lag by one sale (sale timing). The 5 % band (about 215 W at 4.3 kW) is under the 700 W oil-generator step, so `oil_generator_8` flapped three times on sub-day dips and `oil_generator_7` once: the band must satisfy `stopMargin − margin ≥ rating / peak` (0.16 here) to hold a step of that size.
- The phase 10 rows ran on c652412; this real run (6a601b3) shows 670,749 at 0.667 h against 669,990 there, so TP across phases is not comparable, only within a pair on identical `latest_scripts`.
- Tests: `test/standins.test.mjs` +2 (band: a cut at `margin` but not at `stopMargin` stays, inside the band no restart; default equals `margin`, below `margin`, NaN / Infinity / `"abc"` / `""`, and `stopMargin` without `quietWindows` throw before any side effect), `scenario.test.mjs` default params and the `--standin` parse; 66 green with `node --test "devtools/simulation/headless/test/*.test.mjs"`.
- Docs: `standins.md` (`trim` row, phase table row 11, design note), `ab_results.md` "Phase 11" and the open rows. TODO's headless block has the phase 11 line.

Pitfalls that still apply: `pass(t)` counts from the run's start, archive ages use `state.tickCount`; `--only-scripts` pins `keep` and a stand-in skips kept slots; validate params for value before any side effect (the constructor, not `#setup`, for anything that must precede the archive seeds); the watcher does not cover `devtools/`; a group that is off or stood in has a boundary (`GROUPS[g].produces` / `consumes`) and the A/B gaps come from there first, name them in ab_results.md rather than tuning the stand-in; `final_save.json` is the raw state (no `state` wrapper) and is UTF-8; `run.mjs --hours` and `summary` hours are clock hours (36,000 ticks), planet-hours (250 ticks) are the construction and day-cycle unit; a generator's `generated` share is demand, not capacity, and `subnet.consumed` is demand, not served load (the re-evaluation relies on that); the stand-in's `standins:` lines go to the run's stdout, not `console.log`; a run deploys the repo's working-tree `scripts/`, so uncommitted script edits from a parallel session end up in the run (keep `latest_scripts` identical across a pair: `diff -rq --strip-trailing-cr out/<a>/latest_scripts out/<b>/latest_scripts`).
