**Early Harvester** (`scripts/0_cold_boot/harvesting/harvester.py`, no `lib/`): collects the surface field for credits per tick, tuned offline with `devtools/simulation/headless/harvest_policies.mjs` ("hybrid"; results in [`docs/plans/scoring_map_seeds.md`](../plans/scoring_map_seeds.md)). Re-plans before every hop and every collect except on arrival at its target. Times in ticks at 1× (1 world hour = 250).
- Routes: cheapest monotone route (steps toward the cell only) to every cell, by DP. Hop cost `MOVE_TICKS = 125` + heat price × hop heat (item cell or base pad +1, else +7). Heat price from `LAM_COLD = 20` ticks per heat unit at heat 0, linear to `LAM_HOT = 100` at the 97 limit, rounded.
- Target: the `PAIR_TOP = 6` items with the best `value / (route + COLLECT_TICKS = 63)`, re-rated by the best two-item trip `(v1 + v2) / (T1 + T2)`, second leg by Manhattan distance × (125 + heat price × `PAIR_HEAT = 4`).
- Collect gate: an item it stands on is collected only if worth ≥ `K_COLLECT = 0.5` × 63 × the best single-item rate; cheaper items stay as +1 stepping stones until the rate drops.
- Rest just before a hop would pass 97 heat (`HEAT_MAX − HEAT_SAFETY`), for exactly as long as needed. Starts once `min_scanned_sectors = 10` sectors are scanned. Sells every item at once.
- Paving stock: the `PAVE_KEEP_ITEMS = 4` items nearest the starter path cells (`PAVE_PATH` = A2, A4, B3, C2, `field_layout.path_cells()` of the starter at A1, a constant since this tier has no `lib/`; Manhattan hops), then the lowest-value (ties: lower cell; never the base pad or an item worth more than `PAVE_MAX_VALUE = 250` Scanner value), are never targets and stay as +1 stepping stones. The Field Keeper later carries them onto its path cells ([bio_seeds_planting.md §1k](bio_seeds_planting.md), Paving). Not moved by this script (no drop step in its router).
- Step budget: planning runs as `map()` callbacks (one tick each, uncharged, 10,000-step cap, §1d-1): route rows in chunks of `ROUTE_ROWS = 3` (~1,000 steps per row), the ranking, then pair candidates in chunks of `PAIR_WORK = 150` candidate × item pairs. Largest chunk ~4,200 steps on a full field; a plan takes ~6–7 ticks (up to ~13 on a full field).

### §2a Vehicle Energy Budgeting Detail (`lib/vehicle_energy.py` `VehicleEnergyMixin`; station discovery `lib/charging_stations.py`; charging and station-routed driving `lib/vehicle_charging.py` `VehicleChargingMixin`)

- Module split: `charging_stations.py` holds the station functions (`all_stations()`, `nearest()`, `outpost_ref()`, `station_at()`, `home_slot_coords()`, `current()`, `station_load()`) and the `STATION_DISCOVERY_TTL_TICKS` cache; `VehicleEnergyMixin` keeps one-line method wrappers (`get_all_charging_stations()`, `get_nearest_charging_station()`, ...). `VehicleChargingMixin` owns `recharge_at_station()`, `balance_dock()`, `drive_with_recharge()`, `return_to_base()` and `recharge_at_nearest()`; energy never drives.

- **Travel energy = developer-confirmed exact model, no calibration — Pioneer and
  Rover use two DIFFERENT models.** Construction *progress* energy still calibrated
  (`wh_per_progress`, no confirmed formula).

  **Pioneer** (`VehicleEnergyMixin`, used as-is by `PioneerController`):
  ```
  power (W)   = (BASE_TRAVEL_POWER_W=3.0 + MODULE_TRAVEL_POWER_W=8.0 × active_modules
                 + CARGO_UNIT_TRAVEL_POWER_W=0.04 × cargo_units) × throttle^1.5 × nav_power_multiplier
  speed (m/h) = DRIVE_SPEED_M_PER_HOUR_PER_THROTTLE=100.0 × throttle × nav_speed_multiplier
  ```
  - `active_modules` — mounted functional modules drawing power while driving: Nav/Drill/Sonar/
    Constructor family, including upgraded variants (`ACTIVE_MODULE_ID_PREFIXES`). Passive
    containers (Battery Holder, Cargo Rack) don't count. `active_modules_count()`.
  - `cargo_units` — live `self.vehicle.cargo.count()`. `calculate_trip_energy()` computes outbound
    and return legs with *different* cargo loads (return = outbound + `planned_drill_units`).
    `cargo_units_count()`.
  - `nav_speed_multiplier` / `nav_power_multiplier` — from mounted Sport Nav modules. 1 Sport Nav =
    exactly 2x speed / 2.6x power (`docs/components/nav_module.md`). n Sport Navs = `1 + n` speed,
    `1 + 1.25n + 0.35n²` power (game formula; 2 -> 4.9x). Sport Nav replaces Basic Nav, so
    `active_modules` is unchanged. No Sport Nav gives
    both multipliers `1.0`.

  **Rover** (`RoverController` override in `lib/rover.py`, not in base mixin):
  ```
  Wh/meter = ROVER_WH_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE=0.2 × throttle
  ```
  Developer-confirmed (Spyros - CT Dev, in-game Discord #playtest-chat, 2026-08-28): "the rover is
  very simple wh = distance x throttle x 0.2" — flat, **no** `active_modules`, `cargo_units`, or
  Sport Nav terms, linear in throttle (vs. Pioneer `throttle^1.5`).
  `RoverController.wh_per_meter_at_throttle()` overrides only that method —
  `minimum_wh_per_meter()`, `calculate_trip_energy()`, `energy_needed_to_return_now()`/
  `_comfortably()`, `energy_wh_for_leg()` all call through it. `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()` solves
  directly (linear): `t <= available_for_leg / (distance * ROVER_WH_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE *
  SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER)`.
  - Module-level standalone `_for`-suffixed versions (`travel_wh_per_meter_for(vehicle, throttle,
    cargo_units=None)`, etc.) let non-`VehicleController` callers (e.g. a charging station's own
    script — no cross-script instance calls exist, only `get_component(id)`/Archive/Signal Bus)
    use the same formula without a live instance, dispatching to the Rover model via
    `is_rover_chassis_for(vehicle)` (probes `vehicle.id`/`.name` for a `"rover"` prefix).
    `lib/charging.py`'s `rescue_wh_per_meter_for(vehicle)` collapses this into one entry point for
    rescue sizing (rates the leg at `MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE`, the cheapest Wh/m; falls back to a
    bare-module Pioneer shape if unreachable).
  - **Station pacing and arbitration** (`lib/charging.py` and `lib/drone_service.py`, shared base `lib/station_controller.py` `StationController`): `step()` returns busy (bays active/queued, a rescue in progress, or an entity this station owns that is stranded, below floor or below its rescue target); `run()` sleeps `ACTIVE_POLL_SECONDS = 1.5` while busy, else `IDLE_POLL_SECONDS = 5.0` (unpowered included). Per entity, `assess_stations()` does one pass over the per-poll station list and returns (is this station nearest, nearest distance, nearest coords); a station does floor/target work only for entities it is nearest to (tie or own position unknown → mine; a dry service is never nearest for a heli drone). `return_floor_wh()`/`rescue_target_level()` take the already-computed distance/floor. Parked stations (`lib/script_parking.py`, dev_workflow.md §1d-2) are left out of the nearest check; an entity needing attention that is nearest to a parked station gets that station woken (`wake_for_visit()`) instead, and the woken station handles it. `step()` feeds a `ParkRequester` (idle = powered and not busy).
  - **`lib/charging.py` fleet rescue** (`manage_fleet_rescues()`): dispatch triggers on engine
    `"stranded"`/`"stalled_no_battery"` status, OR `return_floor_wh()` (the Wh needed on board
    *right now* to self-navigate to the nearest station, via `rescue_wh_per_meter_for()` at
    `MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE` — same formula the vehicle's own hard-abort uses).
    `RESCUE_EXTRA_RESERVE_WH = 8.0` is added on top only when sizing `rescue_target_level()` (how
    much to charge *during* rescue); the trigger comparison uses the bare floor.
  - **Multi-station arbitration**: every deployed station gates dispatch on
    `assess_stations()` (see station pacing above), so only one rescues a given stranded vehicle. Ties default
    to allowing dispatch.
  - **Station choice when parked** (`balance_dock()`, called by `recharge_at_station()` after it parks): every station inside an outpost covers the whole outpost. With 2+ stations in the outpost of `current_station()`, the vehicle calls `dock()` on the one with the fewest other vehicles queued or active per bay (`get_queue()` ∪ `get_active()` / `get_bay_count()`; ties: current station, then id), then charges and wakes that station. Docked = `current_station()` non-empty.
- Round-trip budget = outbound drive + sonar/scan budget + mining/drill budget + drive from target
  to *nearest* charging station, all × `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER = 1.05` (5%), plus hard
  `MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH = 8.0` floor on top. See `calculate_trip_energy()`.
- **Mining/drill budget also developer-confirmed exact model, not flat average.**
  `mine_wh_per_unit(item_id, purity)` (and standalone `mine_wh_per_unit_for(...)`):
  ```
  time (h) = (ORE_DIG_MINUTES[item_id] / 60) × drill.speed_multiplier() / PURITY_DIVISOR[purity]
  Wh       = time (h) × DRILL_POWER_W_BY_HARDNESS_LIMIT[drill.hardness_limit()]
  ```
  Per-tier Watts: basic=10W/industrial=20W/heavy=30W (keyed by `drill.hardness_limit()`, no
  `.power_draw()` method exists). Per-ore dig minutes (`docs/database/items_minerals.md`):
  iron_ore/silicon=15, lead_ore=18, titanium/cobalt=20, rare_earth=25, neutronium=30. Purity yield
  multiplier: `standard`=1×/`rich`=2×/`pure`=3× (same divisor as game's own time formula).
  `calculate_trip_energy()`'s `mine_item_id`/`mine_purity` params feed this; every real mining
  call site passes candidate's own `harvest_item`/`purity`. `MINE_WH_PER_UNIT = 2.5` (flat) =
  only fallback for candidate with no `mine_item_id` — equals cheapest real case (basic drill,
  iron ore, standard) but under-reserves by up to **~3.6x** for Heavy/Neutronium
  (`9.0` Wh vs. flat `2.5` Wh).
- Throttle clamped to `[MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE=0.10, MAX_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE=1.0]`, picked
  per-leg by `select_cruise_throttle()` / `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()`. Pioneer power scales with
  `throttle^1.5`, speed with `throttle`, so leg Wh/m scales with `sqrt(throttle)` —
  `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()` solves via `t <= (available_Wh / (distance *
  coeff * SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER)) ** 2`.
- **Cruise throttle = one numeric default, not binary conserve/highspeed flag.**
  `select_cruise_throttle()` picks `min(self.cruise_throttle, MAX_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE)` as
  baseline, then caps DOWN (never up) to what `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()` allows.
  `self.cruise_throttle` = `default_cruise_throttle()`, read once at script start (no per-slot
  override; vehicle and drone scripts have no throttle parameter) — fleet-wide
  `vehicle.default_cruise_throttle` archive value (clamped to `[MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE,
  MAX_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE]`, fallback `DEFAULT_CRUISE_THROTTLE_FALLBACK = 0.6`), settable via
  Data Archive Notebook or live from `vehicles_panel.py`'s FLEET card slider. `lib/drone_energy.py`
  mirrors exactly for drones — own `drone.default_cruise_throttle` archive key (distinct from
  vehicle key so fleets tune independently), same clamp, fallback `DEFAULT_CRUISE_THROTTLE_FALLBACK = 1.0`, settable from the FLEET card's
  Drones-tab slider (`vehicles_panel.py`, §7).
- `minimum_wh_per_meter()` gives best-case Wh/m at throttle floor. Any check claiming target/job
  *permanently* unreachable (not just "not right now") must budget against this, not typical
  cruise-throttle rate.
- Two reserve checks, deliberately separate:
  - `energy_needed_to_return_now()` — true floor, at `minimum_wh_per_meter()`. Used only for
    the **hard mid-drive abort** inside `drive_to()`'s tick loop.
  - `energy_needed_to_return_comfortably()` — rated at `self.cruise_throttle`. Used for every
    **proactive** "keep working or head back" decision (mining stop check, construction Field
    Battery Floor, survey per-POI/per-waypoint reserve checks). A simple heuristic, not an exact
    time/output optimum.
- `drive_to()` computes `is_driving_to_station` once before polling loop, skips return-reserve
  abort check entirely when destination is charging station/base slot (or already within 3m) —
  arriving there *is* recovery.
- Construction (Pioneer only): `calculate_trip_energy()`'s `planned_construction_progress` param
  adds `progress * self.wh_per_progress`. `wh_per_progress` calibrated per-vehicle from real
  `constructor.execute()` calls (`calibrate_wh_per_progress()`), fallback
  `CONSTRUCTION_WH_PER_PROGRESS_DEFAULT = 40.0` Wh (0%→100%) until enough samples.
  `construction_lots.planned_progress_for_job()` caps planned progress at
  `TARGET_CONSTRUCTION_PROGRESS_PER_TRIP = 0.25` (or less if further along) — floor on whether to
  depart, not on-site cap.
- Construction trip throttle (Pioneer only, `BuildJob._plan_trip_throttle()`, every step with
  cargo for pending jobs, from the current position and battery): `construction_plan.trip_route()`
  chains the first `TRIP_PLAN_JOBS = 8` served jobs nearest-next, and `trip_throttle()` tries
  throttles from the base cruise down to `MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE` in `TRIP_THROTTLE_STEP = 0.05`
  steps. Per throttle: longest chain prefix whose drive out + drive to nearest station (× margin) +
  capped progress Wh + 2 × emergency reserve fits the battery; pick max jobs / cycle hours, where
  cycle hours = driving hours + hours to recharge the battery's deficit plus the trip's Wh at
  `TRIP_CHARGE_W = 60.0` W. Sets the vehicle's `cruise_throttle`, so the field reserve checks above
  use it too. No fitting plan: at a station, reset to base (`BuildJob.base_cruise_throttle`, the
  throttle before the first plan); in the field, keep the throttle and recharge at the nearest
  station first, so the trip out is planned there (not driven at cruise after a
  `drive_with_recharge()` top-off). A far chain gets driven slower and several jobs per charge
  instead of one.
- **Construction job claims (Pioneer only, `BuildJob` in `lib/job_build.py`, exclusive)**: construction
  job NOT shareable (two Constructor Pioneers on same blueprint would double-load materials).
  Uses `vehicle_claims.py`'s exclusive claim mechanism, key `f"build_{job_id}"`
  (`PioneerController.construction_claim_key()`), prefix distinct from mining `"site_"`/survey
  `"poi_"`. `claim_target()` called at each of three commit points (resuming paused job,
  executing cargo-matching pending job, committing before round trip home).
  `execute_construction()` heartbeats via `refresh_claim()` every attempt (no-op if not owner).
  Released on completion (`last_build_progress >= 1.0`, the progress read after the last `execute()` of `execute_construction()`) or genuine failure, held across
  incomplete "still paused" outcome, released wholesale on unhandled exception (`BuildJob.on_error()`,
  called by `run_job_loop()`).
  **Blueprint missing from every list (pending/active/paused) counts as complete (1.0)** — game
  drops finished blueprints from all lists.
  `construction_lots.release_finished_claims()` also sweeps this Pioneer's own `build_*` claims on
  no-longer-live blueprints each `BuildJob.step()` (one transaction per claims key, only when something
  needs releasing, skipped if any list read failed).
- **Construction job scan (`lib/construction_plan.py`, pure, via `lib/atomic.py`)**: each
  `BuildJob.step()` scans the paused/pending/active blueprint lists in atomic
  slices (`scan_jobs()`, `JOB_CHUNK = 15` jobs per call): job ids for the claim sweep, open rows
  (id, not in `BuildJob.failed_jobs`, not in an active peer's lot, no fresh peer claim per
  `claim_free()`), and cargo-matching rows (deconstruction, or materials aboard per one
  `cargo.stacks()` read, `ConstructionStockMixin.cargo_counts()`) sorted by priority, then own lot first, then nearest first. Step 5's "permanently out of range" check runs as `station_trip_wh()`
  (`TRIP_CHUNK = 12` rows per call, nearest of the memoized station list).
  `get_construction_progress()` finds the job via `job_progress()` (`PROGRESS_CHUNK = 256`).
  Worst slice per function asserted below `ATOMIC_STEP_BUDGET = 4000` operations in
  `tests/test_construction_plan.py`. Only the scan is atomic: claims, archive writes, trip
  budgeting (logs) and driving stay outside.
- **Construction restock batch (`BuildJob` restock step, `ConstructionStockMixin.stock_materials()` in `lib/pioneer_stock.py`)**: loads toward the summed need
  of open jobs using the target job's material (`batch_count()`), capped by free cargo. With
  active same-home Constructor peers (`peer_builders()`: `fleet.status` entries with
  `role == "constructor"`, same `home`, heartbeat younger than `PEER_BUILDER_ACTIVE_TICKS = 6000`
  ticks (10 sim min), state not idle (`fleet_status.is_idle()`: any `IDLE*`, `READY_AT_*`,
  off duty)), `fair_share_batch()` caps it further at
  ceil((home takeable stock + units aboard) / builders) (`fair_share()`,
  `storage.takeable_stock()`). A lone builder is uncapped, so a long remote chain with field
  recharges still runs on one load. **Top-up** (`_top_up_materials()`): after that load, the
  cargo room left is filled with the other materials the open jobs at the working priority need
  (`construction_plan.other_materials()`: the material whose job is nearest the target job
  first), each loaded only when its nearest job's whole need fits the bins (`bin_space()`: room
  in bins holding it plus empty bins, a Portable Bin holds one item type) and home takeable
  stock, capped by `batch_count()` and `fair_share_batch()`; skipped while a deconstruction is
  open (it needs room for reclaimed parts). So one trip home stocks for several materials.
  Heartbeat: `publish_telemetry("BUILDING")` at every
  `execute_construction()`, `"RESTOCKING"` at every restock. A failed restock defers every open
  job needing that material for the pass (`ids_needing()`), not just the target job.
- **Construction lots (`construction.lots`, restock step, `BuildJob._reserve_lot()`; archive I/O in `lib/construction_lots.py`)**: after loading a
  batch, a Constructor reserves the jobs it covers as its lot: the claimed seed job plus a
  nearest-neighbour chain of open same-material jobs (deconstructions with deconstructions,
  `DECONSTRUCT_LOT_JOBS = 10`) grown from it (`construction_plan.grow_lot()`, atomic
  `grow_lot_step()` chunks of `LOT_STEP_VISITS = 80` candidate visits, an append counting as
  `LOT_ADD_VISITS = 4`), cut to the units aboard (`lot_prefix()`); each topped-up material adds
  its own chain, grown from its nearest job, to the same lot. The chain skips jobs a peer
  claimed since the pre-trip scan. One transaction replaces the builder's entry, keeps ids a peer
  reserved meanwhile, and prunes entries of builders no longer active. A seed job that landed in
  a peer's lot meanwhile has its claim released, so the lot owner can build it. Peers drop every job in the lot of an active Constructor of any home
  (`peer_builders()` with `home=None`) from their scan; a lot of a quiet or inactive owner stops
  counting without a write. The owner sorts its own-lot jobs first when building from cargo and
  restocks its own unbuilt lot jobs first. Released when the builder idles without jobs, defers a
  material, or its step raises. Builders so work separate stretches of a line or separate sites
  instead of chasing each other to the same nearest job.
- **Construction hold (`construction.hold`, `construction_lots.read_hold()`, `BuildJob`)**: `{"by", "tick", "kinds"}`; while fresh (`construction_plan.held_kinds()`, younger than `HOLD_STALE_TICKS = 600`), jobs of a held kind are dropped from the open rows and cargo matches after the scan. They still count as live for claim and priority pruning. The autoplay power survey holds `deconstruct` while it probes.
- **Power-line ledger (`construction.power_tiles`, `execute_construction()`)**: a finished job (`res.status == "ok"`) calls `construction_lots.note_finished_power_job()` → `construction_plan.note_power_job()` in one transaction: a `power_line` piece adds its two tiles (`power_job_tiles()` from the job position); a `deconstruct` or `power_bridge` job marks its tiles dirty for re-probing. See [autoplay.md §11d](autoplay.md).
- **Construction job priority (`construction.priority`, `BuildJob`)**: each row
  carries `prio` from the archive dict (`construction_plan.PRIORITY_KEY`, `{blueprint_id: int}`,
  read once per pass before the job lists, cleaned by `clean_priorities()`); a blueprint without
  an entry is `DEFAULT_PRIORITY = 0`, lower runs first, so a negative value raises a job and the
  autoplay planner's plan-ahead jobs use 1. Each pass works only the lowest prio among open
  paused + pending rows (`top_priority()`/`at_priority()`): lower-priority jobs are not built,
  matched from cargo, or counted in the restock batch/`fair_share_batch()` while a higher one is
  open. A job that failed this pass or is claimed by a peer is not open, so a peer or a stock
  shortage lets the next priority through; when every pending job at the top priority was just
  marked out of range, the job rescans at once instead of idling. Entries of blueprints no longer
  in any list are pruned each pass (`construction_lots.prune_priorities()`, one transaction, only when
  something is stale, skipped if a list read failed). With the key absent every job is prio 0 and
  the job behaves as without priorities.
- Fleet coordination (`lib/vehicle_claims.py`): atomic `archive.transaction()` claims (mirrored to
  `rover.claims` / `survey.claims`), heartbeat-renewed via `refresh_claim()`, expire after
  `fleet_claims_common.CLAIM_STALE_TICKS = 36000` ticks (1 real hour; drone claims and the archive cleaner use the same value). **Mineral mining sites not exclusive**
  (several Pioneers/Rovers may mine same site): a mine claim is per vehicle, key
  `"<target_key>@<vehicle>"` (`mine_claim_key()`, `VehicleClaimsMixin.claim_key()`), so it never
  loses to a peer; it only serves bookkeeping (the mission, resume via
  `_mission_claims()`). Survey/POI targets still exclusive via same mechanism.
  The current job target is `VehicleController.mission` (`lib/mission.py` `Mission`), written only by
  `VehicleClaimsMixin`: `begin_mission(m)` = claim (skipped with `claimed=True`) + yield reservation when
  `m.reserved_yield` + `vehicle.mission` record + set; `end_mission()` = release claim, yield reservation
  and record, clear. `current_target`, `current_target_key`, `current_target_reserved` are read-only
  views of it (a write raises `AttributeError`). `release_target_claim()` on the current target (or with
  no key) calls `end_mission()`. After a reload `load_mission()` returns `Mission.from_record(...)` and
  `adopt_mission()` sets it (a mine mission then re-arms `reserved_yield` from the live reservation).
  Drones keep the dict path (`current_target*` attributes).
- **In-flight mining yield reservation** (`lib/mining_reservations.py`, `mining.reserved_yield`
  archive key): non-exclusive, additive bookkeeping, so vehicles converging on one deficit don't
  all see the same undiminished demand. One entry per vehicle (its current trip).
  `VehicleMiningMixin.select_best_mining_target(candidates)` reserves every mine trip's planned
  yield: `max_mineable_units()` capped by candidate `"max_units"` (stockpile headroom), tagged with
  the delivery `outpost_id`. `logistics_requests.outpost_tier_deficits()` counts reservations
  for that outpost as in-flight (`exclude_vehicle=` leaves out a miner's own), so pull haulers and
  `home_ore_demand()` never chase ore a miner already fetches; stockpile path's
  `stockpile_headroom(outpost_id, item_id)` = `ore_stock_target()` − stock − peers' reservations −
  hauler pickups bound there (`in_flight()`). A second vehicle joins a site only while demand is left
  after every reservation. Heartbeat-renewed/released (`refresh_yield()`/`release_yield()`),
  same expiry (`RESERVATION_STALE_TICKS = 36000`).
- **Energy-based mining trip sizing** (`VehicleEnergyMixin.max_mineable_units()`): default
  yield estimate (not `cargo.capacity()`). Solves trip-energy budget directly for units
  (outbound + base return Wh fixed, mined-unit Wh and marginal return-drive Wh linear in unit
  count) after subtracting `MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH` and applying `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER`,
  clamped to free cargo (`cargo.capacity()` − `count()`). `mine_until_full_or_exhausted()` stays safety net for estimate
  drift (e.g. richer-than-expected purity).
- Recall (flag in `lib/vehicle_claims.py`, `handle_recall_if_active()` in `lib/vehicle_recall.py`): one shared `vehicle.recall` dict `{vehicle_name: True}` (not
  one archive key per vehicle). `is_vehicle_recalled()`/`set_vehicle_recalled()` module-level
  read/write. Toggled via `vehicles_panel.py`'s Fleet card switch: on → abandon current target, drive to
  base now; off → resume. Checked inside `drive_to()`'s tick loop and mining loops, same
  `is_driving_to_station` exemption as return-reserve check.
- Navigation timeout (`drive_timeout_ticks()`): defaults `None`, computed per-leg from expected
  travel time, converted via `Clock.real_seconds_per_hour()` (not flat constant — compressed
  day/night cycle stretches game-hours relative to world-clock hours). `safety_multiplier = 2.0`,
  `min_ticks = 3000` floor.
- Navigation safety: stall detection re-issues drive command after repeated stuck cycles,
  dropping to `MIN_SPEEDMODE_THROTTLE` each retry, gives up after 3 failed recoveries — applies
  regardless of `is_driving_to_station`. Base staging slots staggered per vehicle index.
- `is_at_base(threshold=3.0)`: `self.distance_to_home() <= threshold`. Gates one-time-per-visit
  actions (top-off charging, restocking) so they fire only when parked at base.
- **Loop-top "cargo aboard but not at base" safety net vs. mission resume**:
  `MineJob.prepare_cargo()` (`lib/job_mine.py`) keeps cargo aboard while `host.mission` is resumable
  (target key + coords), so save reload mid-trip doesn't undo already-resumed drive.
  `cargo_matches_target(target)` (cargo holds *different* material than resumed target's
  `harvest_item`; cargo not material-locked) or `cargo_full_for_resume()` sends it home to unload
  first, mission kept; the step ends after the unload (`blocked_delay` 10 s while cargo is still aboard) and the next one resumes. Target with no
  `harvest_item` (survey POI) always matches.

### §2b Mining (`lib/vehicle_mining.py` `VehicleMiningMixin`, `lib/job_mine.py` `MineJob`)

Mineral-site discovery + drill execution in one place, shared by Rover and Pioneer (mixed into `VehicleController`). No loop: the mining role is `MineJob(host, targets)` run by `run_job_loop()`.

- **`MineJob` step** (`lib/job_base.py` contract): `prepare()` at base tops off below 95% (Rover at any station; `OutpostStockpile` only with a station at its outpost, §2e) and runs `handle_upgrade_cycle_if_idle()` when the host has it; `prepare_cargo()` handles cargo aboard (§2a resume net) and `targets.transfer()`. `pick()` = resumable `host.mission` (fresh trip budget, copy of the mission) else `targets.select()` (claims through `select_best_mining_target()` → `begin_mission`). `execute()` = log block, `OUTBOUND`, `drive_with_recharge()`, `scan_and_survey(mission)` for a `poi` target or `mine_until_full_or_exhausted(coords, targets.mine_units())` for `mine`, `return_to_base()` (incomplete: mission kept, 5 s), `release_target_claim()`, `unload_cargo()` (blocked: `WAITING_INVENTORY_SPACE`, 10 s), recharge (same station rule as the top-off), ready telemetry. `on_error()` = `release_target_claim()` (every claim, yield reservation, saved mission).
- **`RoverTargets`** (`RoverController.run()` = `run_job_loop(MineJob(self, RoverTargets()))`): `cleanup_stale_claims()`, unscanned POIs (priority 1) + `build_mineral_site_candidates()`; mines the reserved `estimated_units`; idle = warn with the reason (no home demand / no candidate / none in budget / claims), `IDLE_AT_BASE`, 10 s; no transfer step (plans from where it stands).
- **`OutpostStockpile(outpost_id)`** (Miner Pioneer, §2e): `build_local_stockpile_candidates(outpost_id)`; transfers to the base slot first when away; mines max(1, stock headroom, NEED request) live at the site; idle = recharge when `charge_would_reach()` (0 s), else the stand-by reason (`stockpile_empty_reason` or `reject_reason()`), `IDLE_AT_OUTPOST`, 30 s.

- Capability always read live via `self.vehicle.drill.hardness_limit()` — never assumed from vehicle type. Rover fixed Drill only carries basic drill (`hardness_limit = 1`, iron_ore/silicon); Industrial (`hardness_limit = 3`) and Heavy (`hardness_limit = 4`) Drills = Pioneer-universal-slot items for higher-hardness sites.
- `home_ore_bands()`: `{band: {ore: units}}` the vehicle's `home_outpost` still requests (`logistics_requests.outpost_tier_deficits()`, raw ores only via `ore_bands()`; a smelting site's ore requests, dock-owed ore included, come from §2i-1) net of stock, in-flight pickups and the yield that in-flight mining trips there already promised (`mining.reserved_yield`). `home_ore_demand()` = every band summed (`sum_bands()`). `build_mineral_site_candidates(deprioritize_hardness_at_or_below=None)`: candidate sites matching it + vehicle hardness limit (Rover); each gets `"band"` = `top_band(bands, ore)`, the highest band in which home still misses that ore (`STOCK` when only stock headroom is left). Passing `ROVER_PREFERRED_MAX_HARDNESS = 1.0` sets `priority=3` instead of `2` on hardness ≤ 1 sites — **soft** preference.
- Neither candidate builder filters peer-claimed sites (mineral sites not exclusive) — see `lib/mining_reservations.py` for overmining guard.
- `select_best_mining_target(candidates)`: sorts by `(priority, -band, -mining_score(),
  distance)` — priority first; then ore missing in a higher band at the delivery outpost beats lower-band ore (same rule as haulers' `haul_rank()`; the fine prio plays no part); then the highest score; distance only breaks ties. Score = candidate `"urgency"` / `mining_hours_per_unit()`. Hours per unit = `mine_hours_per_unit()` (dig time: mounted drill, site purity) + round trip at `leg_speed_m_per_hour()` (`select_cruise_throttle()` incl. battery cap, mounted nav) / units per trip (`max_mineable_units()` on the battery on board, capped by `"max_units"`). All read live per pick, so battery, throttle and loadout changes (outfitter) re-rank. `"urgency"`: stationed `STOCK`-band candidates carry `stock_urgency(item, room, band)` = missing share of `ore_stock_target()` (0..1), so a nearly empty slow ore beats a half-full fast one; ore missing in a higher band and home-demand candidates use 1.0 (the band already puts it first; pure speed). POI candidates score 0. Soft preference — `calculate_trip_energy()` achievability check + `claim_target()` still run after sort. Both builders attach site `"purity"` from `getattr(site, "purity", None)`.
  Achievability checked at full 10-unit haul first; if not fitting round-trip budget, retries once at whatever `max_mineable_units()` says affordable instead of rejecting (only 0 affordable = real rejection) — else vehicle whose battery never fits full 10-unit trip (undersized battery, or expensive-per-unit ore like Neutronium) idles at base forever despite reachable demand. Logged at `print()` level (`"battery can't afford a full load -- heading out for a partial ~N-unit load"`) when fallback fires — normal-operation outcome worth surfacing, not debug detail.
- `mine_current_site(max_units=None)` defaults to `self.vehicle.cargo.capacity()` (live) when no `max_units`. Home-demand mining loops always pass explicit `max_units` from `select_best_mining_target()`'s `estimated_units` (energy-based, `max_mineable_units()`).
  `mine_until_full_or_exhausted(target_coords, max_units)` wraps it with recharge-and-resume-in-place loop; `max_units` bounds the whole job (each resume gets what is left). Battery-interruption recharge stop landing at *home base* station itself with cargo loaded unloads via `unload_cargo()` **before** `recharge_at_station()` (ore not stranded in cargo during a possibly multi-minute recharge), then ends the job (`"load delivered at base"`) after `run_module_upgrades_mid_job()`: the next cycle picks its target from current demand. Resuming with an empty hold instead let a battery-bound stationed miner stockpile one ore for hours past its target while the others ran dry.
- Pioneer mining role (`MineJob` with `OutpostStockpile`) requires operator already mounted drill — `detect_role()` picks it by the mounted drill; under `role_override` without one, `mine_current_site()` logs "No DrillModule mounted!" and mines nothing; never auto-mounts.
- **Unified role entrypoint (`PioneerController.run()`/`detect_role()`, `lib/pioneer.py`)**: every `pioneer_N.py` script constructs `PioneerController`, calls `run()`. `detect_role()` maps `ROLE_MODULES` (`{"constructor": "constructor", "scout": "sonar", "miner": "drill"}`) via `hasattr(self.vehicle, <attr>)` — exactly one mounted module picks role; none → `"hauler"`; more than one = misconfigured loadout (`TreeConsole` warn + no-op) unless `role_override=` passed. `run()` dispatches: constructor → `run_job_loop(BuildJob(self))` (`lib/job_build.py`; loop plumbing in `VehicleController.run_job_loop()`, `lib/job_base.py` contract), scout → `run_job_loop(SurveyJob(self))` (`lib/job_survey.py`; skipped with a warning when Sonar or Nav Module missing, `SurveyJob.can_run()`), miner → `run_job_loop(MineJob(self, OutpostStockpile(self.home_base)))` (`lib/job_mine.py`), hauler → `run_job_loop(PullJob(self))` for `HOME_BASE` (`lib/job_pull.py`, §2f). Equipment swappable at runtime, so re-probes every script run, no role caching.

### §2b-1 Pioneer Auto-Upgrade (`lib/pioneer_upgrade.py` `PioneerUpgradeMixin`)

Auto hardware tier upgrades for Pioneer, checked once per idle-at-base cycle (`handle_upgrade_cycle_if_idle()`, called from same "parked at base, checking readiness" checkpoint every role loop has: `BuildJob._ready_at_base()` in `lib/job_build.py`, `SurveyJob.prepare()` in `lib/job_survey.py`, `MineJob.prepare()` in `lib/job_mine.py`, `PullJob.prepare()` in `lib/job_pull.py`; `Job.prepare()`/`SurveyJob.prepare()`/`MineJob.prepare()`/`PullJob.prepare()` gate with `getattr(host, ...)`).

- **Idle gate**: at base, empty cargo, no `mission`. `MineJob.prepare_cargo()` ends the step after a return-and-unload in both mining roles (Rover and stationed Miner), so the next cycle passes the gate before picking a target. At a recharge-and-resume base stop (`_recharge_and_resume_mining()`) `run_module_upgrades_mid_job()` swaps Sonar/Drill and runs the holder/rack split (§2b-2) (at base, empty cargo, claim still held) before that stop ends the job. Container tier steps wait for the idle gate.
- **Pioneer-only.** `docs/database/equipment_modules.md` documents Sport Nav, Wide/Deep Sonar, Industrial/Heavy Drill as Pioneer-universal-slot items — Rover's 3 fixed slots only accept basic `nav_module`/`sonar_module`/`drill_module` (§2b), so never better tier for it. `PioneerUpgradeMixin` mixed into `PioneerController` only, never shared `VehicleController` base.
- **Tier tables** (worst → best, all in `lib/item_tiers.py`, the one home of every hardware ladder; `tests/test_item_tiers.py` checks each against `docs/database/`, so a new game tier fails the suite until listed). `best_unlocked_tier()` only steps to immediate next tier present in fresh `shop.get_catalogue()` read, never straight to top, so one swap = one affordable purchase:
  - `SONAR_TIERS = ["sonar_module", "sonar_module_wide", "sonar_module_deep", "sonar_module_seismic"]`. Commission and the default auto ladder use `pioneer_upgrade.SONAR_UPGRADE_TIERS` (stops at Deep). `_sonar_ladder()` allows the Deep → Seismic step (7,500 cr) only while `_seismic_wanted()`: Petroleum Survey + Advanced Oil Extraction unlocked (`deep_oil.SEISMIC_RESEARCH_IDS`), `deep_oil.open_work()`, and no other Pioneer has Seismic mounted (`fleet.vehicles()` → each live `modules()`). A Seismic Pioneer keeps it (no swap back, so newly found formations never flip it).
- **Deep oil (`lib/deep_oil.py`, `vehicle_survey.deep_oil_targets()`)**: `RESERVOIRS_PER_MAP = 3` reservoirs hide under inert formations (`GeologicalAnomaly`, `kind() == "inert"`, ids `geological_anomaly_N`). Only a Seismic scan (both researches) sets `seismic_status` `"potential"` (a reservoir, certain) / `"dry"`; other tiers leave `"unscanned"`, so ordinary scouts never route there. A Seismic survey of a non-dry formation (0.5 h, 3 Wh) turns a reservoir into a rich 16 t/h Oil Well with the same id; a dry one is free. `prospect()` reads it all from journal sites: `found` = oil sites with a formation id, `remaining = 3 − found − potential`, `odds` = remaining / unscanned. `targets()` = potential + unscanned while `remaining > 0`. With Seismic mounted, `survey_known_pois()` adds these as a third candidate pool (key `site_<id>`); `scan_and_survey()` surveys every inert site in the sweep not known dry (the scan's site objects may predate the sweep's own status write).
  - `DRILL_TIERS = ["drill_module", "drill_module_industrial", "drill_module_heavy"]`
  - `BATTERY_HOLDER_TIERS = ["battery_holder_small", "battery_holder_medium", "battery_holder_large"]`
    (`_BAY_COUNTS`: 1/2/3 bays)
  - `CARGO_RACK_TIERS = ["cargo_rack_small", "cargo_rack_medium", "cargo_rack_large"]` (1/2/3 bays)
  - `PORTABLE_BATTERY_TIERS = ["portable_battery", "heavy_portable_battery"]` (50Wh/100Wh)
  - `PORTABLE_BIN_TIERS = ["portable_bin", "heavy_portable_bin"]` (25u/50u)
- **Credits**: every auto swap (module, holder/rack + bay fill, Heavy density top-up) goes through the cash manager, consumer `pioneer_upgrade:<name>` (§2l); a denied grant retries next idle cycle.
- **Swap ordering (`_upgrade_function_module()`/`_upgrade_containers()`)**: `unmount` old → `shop.buy` new → `mount` new → `shop.sell` old — deliberately holds old + new in Inventory briefly instead of selling first, so failed purchase rolls back clean (`mount(slot_index, old_id)` restores vehicle). Failure *after* buy (mount rejects, or container mid-sequence purchase runs out of credits) left as logged, non-destructive stop state for operator, not force-rolled-back — nothing silently lost, at most deferred one cycle.
- **Battery Holder swaps always recharge to ~100% first** (`_ensure_full_charge_for_sale()`) before uninstalling any Portable Battery — `shop.sell()` refunds battery's retained charge% with 50% floor (`docs/components/shop.md`), so full charge maximizes refund. Cargo Rack swaps skip this; Portable Bins not charge-valued.
- **Density policy**: every Battery Holder/Cargo Rack bay — newly added by size upgrade (`_fill_container_bays()`) or already installed at base tier (`_top_up_container_density()`, independent of any size upgrade that cycle) — gets **Heavy** Portable Battery/Bin once unlocked, falls back to base variant only while Heavy locked.
- **Sport Nav deliberately excluded from auto ladder** — manual one-shot operator action: `request_sport_nav(vehicle_name)` sets shared `{vehicle_name: True}` archive dict (`SPORT_NAV_REQUEST_KEY = "vehicle.sport_nav_request"`, same shape/rationale as `vehicle_claims.RECALL_KEY`), surfaced as the `+ Sport Nav` button in the detail pane of `vehicles_panel.py`'s FLEET card (Ground tab, selected Pioneer; `lib/vehicles_card.py`).
  `handle_sport_nav_request_if_active()` consumes it once idle at base, if `nav_module_sport` unlocked + affordable. Basic Nav is exclusive (`mount()` returns `capability_already_mounted` beside it); Sport Navs stack only with each other. So with Basic Nav mounted it swaps 1:1 in that slot via `_swap_function_module()` (unmount, buy, mount, sell old; remount on buy fail). Without Basic Nav, an extra Sport Nav goes into the first free `universal` slot. Request flag always clears after one attempt, success or fail — stuck request (no free slot, locked research, insufficient credits) no retry loop; operator clicks again when ready.

### §2b-2 Pioneer Holder/Rack Split (`lib/pioneer_split.py` math + `PioneerUpgradeMixin._rebalance_container_split()`)

A Pioneer with a Drill, or in the hauler role, splits its container slots (every mounted Battery Holder + Cargo Rack) between the two kinds for its own sites. Other roles keep their preset. Runs in `run_auto_upgrade_cycle()` (idle gate, §2b-1; a pull hauler passes it at the top of every `PullJob.prepare()` cycle), after the Sonar/Drill swaps and before the holder/rack tier steps, and in `run_module_upgrades_mid_job()` at every recharge-and-resume base stop. Only the split changes: function modules and slot count stay.

- **Miner sites** (`_split_sites()`, read from `journal.surveyed_sites()` every cycle, so new sites count): mineral sites with hardness ≤ mounted drill whose nearest outpost is home (`outpost_mining.nearest_outpost_id()`). Per site: `fixed_wh` = 2 × distance from `get_home_slot_coords()` × empty Wh/m at `cruise_throttle` (§2a model, live active-module count); `wh_per_unit` = `mine_wh_per_unit(ore, purity)` + distance × extra Wh/m of one carried unit.
- **Hauler sites** (`_haul_split_sites()`, every cycle): every place `pull_plan.pull_sources()` can fetch from, stock or not, rated from the `HOME_BASE` outpost (not the floating base, §2f, so the split does not churn between trips): every other outpost and every Mining Drill on a surveyed site (`drill_sites.drill_positions()`), minus those `logistics_requests.drone_served_source()` leaves to drones. Salt pumps and the Shop are left out. Per source: `drive_wh` = 2 × distance × empty Wh/m; `wh_per_unit` = distance × extra Wh/m of one carried unit; `fixed_wh` = `drive_wh`, or only the empty way back (distance × empty Wh/m) when the source outpost has a charging station (`pull_from_source()` recharges there after loading). Each trip is modelled as one stop, full racks.
- **Capacities** are rated at the best tier of each kind (top tier in the Shop catalogue, else best mounted): Wh per holder = bays × portable Wh, units per rack = bays × bin units (`PORTABLE_CAPACITY`: 50/100 Wh, 25/50 u; bays `_BAY_COUNTS`).
- **Units per trip** (`units_per_trip()`) = min(rack room, `((holders × Wh − MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH) / SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER − fixed_wh) // wh_per_unit`), the same budget as `max_mineable_units()`, from a full battery.
- **Objective** (`split_cost()`, sites as `(fixed_wh, wh_per_unit[, drive_wh])`, `drive_wh` defaulting to `fixed_wh`): (sites no unit reaches, mean `drive_wh / units_per_trip` over the rest), lower wins. Drive Wh per delivered unit; dig Wh per unit is the same for every split, and drive time scales with drive Wh, so this also minimizes drive time per unit. `best_holder_count()` tries 1..slots−1 holders; ties go to more holders.
- **Hysteresis**: converts only when the best split reaches more sites, or the current one pays ≥ `SPLIT_MIN_GAIN = 1.15` × the best one's drive Wh/unit.
- **Conversion**: the surplus kind, lowest tier first, one slot at a time through `_swap_container()` (empty bays sold, old container sold, best-tier new container bought and mounted, bays filled with Heavy portable once unlocked). Holders → racks first charges to full (`_ensure_full_charge_for_sale()`). Each slot asks the cash manager (consumer `pioneer_upgrade:<name>`, §2l); a denied grant stops the pass, next idle cycle continues. Shop sells parts back at full price (batteries by charge, §2b-1), so a conversion costs only the price difference.

### §2e Stationed Mining Role (`lib/vehicle.py`, `lib/vehicle_energy.py`, `lib/vehicle_mining.py`, `lib/job_mine.py`)

Rover/Pioneer can treat **any** outpost — not just home — as base.

- **`VehicleController.__init__`'s `home_base` param** (outpost id, or `None` = production/home outpost). Resolved to **live objects once at construction**, cached as `self.home_outpost` (`get_outpost_ref(home_base)`) and `self.home_charging_station` (`find_charging_station(self.home_outpost)`), no re-walking `outpost_network.outposts()` by id per lookup. `get_home_slot_coords()` reads only these two cached fields: station position if found, else outpost's `.coords()`, else `(0, 0)` only if `outpost_network` unavailable at construction. `self.home_base` kept only for identity checks. Trade-off: charging station built at this outpost *after* construction not picked up until next script reload.
- **`unload_cargo(outpost=None)`** — defaults to `self.home_outpost` (cached), so stationed vehicle unloads into own outpost's Warehouse; or an explicit `outpost`.
- **`VehicleMiningMixin.build_local_stockpile_candidates(outpost_id)`** — Sites: `cuttable_sites()` = every surveyed raw-ore site the drill cuts (`item_tiers.can_cut()`); `select_best_mining_target()` drops those the charge on board can't reach. Reach: `reach_ores()` = ores with a site whose round trip from the outpost fits a full battery (`full_battery_reaches()`: out + 1 unit dug + on to the charging station nearest the site, cruise throttle, trip margin and reserve, against capacity), walked nearest first: an ore stops at its first site in reach, the walk stops at the first site whose outbound leg alone exceeds capacity. Published as this miner's reach (`outpost_mining.publish_reach()`, production_logistics.md §2d). Per ore in reach: max(stock headroom, NEED request). Stock headroom (`stockpile_headroom()`) = `ore_stock_target()` − local stock − peers' reserved yield − hauls bound here, but 0 for an ore not in `outpost_mining.active_ores()` or without buffer room (`ore_buffer_fits()`). NEED request = `stockpile_need(outpost_id)`: `stockpile_bands()` = that outpost's `outpost_tier_deficits()` net of peers' reserved yield, plus `remote_ore_need()`, folded by `split_need_stock()`; `NEED`+ ores there are latched active; dock-owed ore arrives through that request like any other, §2i-1. `remote_ore_need(outpost_id)`: other outposts' `NEED`-or-above ore deficits (`live=False`) for ore no miner stationed there reaches but one here does (`mineable_ores_by_outpost()`), netted highest band first by the ore held here plus peers' reserved yield here; every outpost reaching that ore sees the whole need, haulers carry the ore over. Blacklisted sites are skipped. Each candidate carries `"band"` = `top_band()`, so a dock order's ore goes ahead of a closer stock-only ore; `max_units` and the trip cap = max(stock headroom, need). Independent of home's live demand.
- **`MineJob(pioneer, OutpostStockpile(outpost_id))`** (§2b) — same step as the Rover's `MineJob` (reload resume, cargo/target mismatch detour, claim + drive + mine + return + unload + recharge, release claim right after return), target selection swapped for `build_local_stockpile_candidates()`, plus a transfer to the base slot before picking.
- **Outpost without its own Charging Station** (`self.home_charging_station is None`): no top-off below 95% at the base slot and no recharge after unload. The nearest station may sit at another outpost, and the transfer back to the base slot would spend what the top-off gained, so the cycle would loop. The miner launches on what is on board. It recharges only when every site is out of energy range and a full battery, less the station-to-slot transfer, covers the cheapest one (`OutpostStockpile.charge_would_reach()`).

### §2f Hauler Role: Floating Pull Hauler (`lib/job_pull.py` `PullJob`, planning `lib/pull_plan.py`)

Every hauler-role Pioneer (no constructor/sonar/drill mounted, §2b) runs `run_job_loop(PullJob(self))`: it fetches whatever any outpost is missing from wherever the stock sits and brings it there (full cycle in `production_logistics.md` §2i). There is no destination parameter and no push hauling. The hauler floats: `HOME_BASE` is only where it starts and its commission origin (`home_base`, lineage, `_wrong_home`).

- **Base** (`VehicleController.set_base(outpost_id)`): re-resolves `home_outpost`, `home_charging_station` and the staging slot (`assigned_slot_coords`/`home_coords`), so `is_at_base()`, `return_to_base()`, `unload_cargo()` and charging work against the new outpost; `base_id` = its id. `PullJob` moves the base to each trip's destination when it claims the pickups. A reload resets the base to `HOME_BASE`; cargo aboard goes back to its pickups' destination (`logistics_requests.pickup_dest()`), a resumed mission to its `"dest"`. Telemetry: `fleet.status` `base` (archive_ipc.md).
- **Ore to home**: home's requests are served like any outpost's, ore included (home smelting site ore requests, §2i-1), collecting from mining outposts' Warehouses, advertised drills and salt pumps.
- **Remote Bio Lab reagents**: a trip to the lab's outpost serves its buyable reagent requests (§2g), buying them at the Shop on a stop at the home outpost. Hauler drones serve them too (§2j).
- **Drone Depot pickups**: an outpost stop loads from the stores, then from the Drone Depot stockpile above `depot_holds()` (`_take_from_depots()`, production_logistics.md §2i free stock). The game lets a Pioneer inside the Depot's service area `take()` from it; every Depot take's status is logged.
- **No nearest-free dispatch**: destinations are ranked band-first (§2i), then by the trip's `haul_rank()` from the hauler's position, which already charges the empty leg to the first stop. With no demand the hauler parks at its current base.
- **Save migration**: `devtools/scripts_sync.py` re-homes a slot that still carries a push-hauler `DESTINATION_OUTPOST_ID` (cached answer or slot code) to that destination (`rehome_retired_destination()`; `outpost_home` → `None`, wildcard/empty → `HOME_BASE` kept).

### §2g Remote Bio Lab Reagent Resupply (`lib/outpost_reagents.py`, `lib/bio.py`)

A home Bio Lab buys missing reagents just in time. A remote Lab can't (no Shop delivery off-home), so `BioLabController.step()` at a non-home outpost calls `outpost_reagents.publish_reagent_requests(outpost)` (errors `swallowed()`): `logistics_requests.publish_requests(outpost_id, "bio_reagents", {item: (levels, have)}, buyable=True)`, recomputed every `REAGENT_REQUEST_REFRESH_TICKS = 600` per outpost (whole target as one `NEED` level; `have` = `logistics_requests.outpost_stock()`, Warehouses + Drone Depots). A hauler drone (`drone_haul_plan.shop_sources()`, §2j) or the Pioneer hauler homed there (`pull_plan.shop_source()`, §2i) fetches free stock first and buys the rest.

**`outpost_reagents.py`** mirrors `outpost_mining.py` seed-once-then-editable convention (`assigned_reagents_for(outpost_id)`, `reagent_stock_target_for(outpost_id, item_id)`), but per-reagent, not one flat constant (reagent prices span 1cr to 1,000cr):

```python
DEFAULT_REAGENT_STOCK_TARGETS = {
    "alkaline_buffer": 100, "cryo_solvent": 100, "protein_marker": 60,
    "chelating_agent": 20, "enzyme_solution": 10,
}  # dial these up as credit budget allows; FALLBACK_REAGENT_STOCK_TARGET = 100 covers any
   # reagent id not yet in this dict (e.g. a future game update)
```

A remote Lab loads from `lib/bio.py`'s `local_stock(item_id, outpost)` = `storage.total_stock(item_id, outpost)` (home Inventory only at home). `storage.outpost_is_home(outpost)` reads `OutpostRef.is_home` (a plain `bool` property, unlike the same-named method on the full `Outpost` component from `get_component()`; it accepts both).

### §2h Drone Energy Budgeting Detail (`lib/drone_energy.py` `DroneEnergyMixin`; charging trips in `lib/drone_charging.py` `DroneChargingMixin`)

`DroneController` base (`lib/drone.py`) with scout (`lib/drone_scout.py`), miner (`lib/drone_mining.py`) and hauler (`lib/drone_hauler.py`, §2j) roles, plus `lib/drone_service.py` (charging/refuelling/rescue) and `lib/drone_depot.py` (cargo station). Drones = **fresh hierarchy, not `VehicleController` subclass** — no `.drive`/`.nav`, no terrain/stall handling, route-based `go_to(x, y)`/`go_to_station(name)`/`go_to_drill(name)` not blocking drive loop, simpler linear power/speed model — but `DroneController` follows same mixin-composition philosophy as `lib/vehicle.py`. Role from mounted modules: Bio Scanner → scout, Bio Extractor → miner, neither but Cargo Pods (`cargo.capacity() > 0`) → hauler.

- **Linear travel model**: full throttle = **5 Wh/h** at **300 m/h**; both scale with throttle (speed linear, burn quadratic), collapsing to `Wh/meter = DRONE_WH_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE
  (=5.0/300.0 ≈ 0.01667) × throttle` — structurally like Rover flat model, different constant, **no** per-drone/cargo/module term. Standalone `drone_wh_per_meter_at_throttle(throttle)` / `drone_rescue_wh_per_meter()` module-level functions let `lib/drone_service.py` reuse same rate cross-script. `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()` solves safe-throttle bound **directly linear** (`t <= available_for_leg / (distance × rate × SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER)`). Drone's own `range_remaining()` = ground truth; formula for planning only.
- **Engine auto-detect** (`detect_engine()`, at `__init__` and again at `run()` after a possible re-equip): own `DroneRef.engine` from `fleet.drones()`, else probe `oil_tank.capacity()`/`battery.capacity()` (the wrong powertrain raises `ReferenceError`; `hasattr` is useless since both attributes always exist), else `"electric"`. Also caches `is_plated()`. Every energy figure in the mixin is in the drone's own unit via `ENGINE_PROFILES`: **electric** `per_meter = 5/300` Wh/m, 300 m/h, reserve `MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH`; **heli** `per_meter = HELI_T_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE = 5/900` t Oil/m (≈ 5.6 t/km at full throttle), 900 m/h, reserve `HELI_MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_T = 5.0` t. Shield Plating × `PLATING_BURN_MULTIPLIER = 1.5`. `get_battery()` keeps its name but reads the oil tank on a heli; `energy_unit()` gives `"Wh"`/`"t"` for logs; telemetry adds `engine`/`unit`. Heli drones skip **dry** drone_service stations (`heli_capable_services()`, shared with `lib/drone_service.py`): usable = `oil_in.level() >= SERVICE_MIN_OIL_T = 10.0` t (buffer max 100 t) **or** `flow_rate() > 0` (refilling now); none usable → wired-but-dry stations ("wired" via `connections()`, which also sees a link the pump declared, unlike `connected_to()`); none wired → all. Applies to refuel, idle parking, return floors and hauler fuel budgets. `flight_timeout_ticks()` uses the engine's speed; `fly_to_station()`/`fly_to_drill()` default timeout scales with distance when coords are known. `STRANDED_STATUSES` includes `stalled_no_oil`. **Route-call burn** (confirmed live on heli): hovering with no route costs nothing, but every `go_to()`/`go_to_station()`/`go_to_drill()` call costs a small minimal burn, even for a 0 m leg. So never re-issue a route needlessly: `fly_to()` returns early when already within precision, `fly_to_station()`/`fly_to_drill()` return early when `current_station()`/`current_drill()` already equals the target. The per-call amount isn't measured yet, so budgets don't model it (the safety margin and reserve cover it).
- **`MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH = 4.0`** (vs. `VehicleEnergyMixin`'s `8.0`) — electric drone batteries much smaller. `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER = 1.05` unchanged.
- **`LAUNCH_MIN_SOC = 0.80`** — launch hysteresis (`hold_for_launch_charge()`): before picking a new mission (scout and miner loops, after cargo/recall handling; the scout only while docked at a service, so in the field it chains POIs on its round-trip budget instead of flying home at 79%), a drone below 80% charge docks at its drone_service and stays until ≥ 80%, and above that while `status()` is `"charging"`/`"refueling"` (the station's job owns the drone until its own target level; route calls answer `"busy"`). Floor only: a target needing more still falls to the existing "none reachable → top up to 98%" branch, so far sites aren't lost. Needed because depot/service share coords, so the comfortable-return trigger is ~0 right after unloading. Reserve stays 4 Wh so the round-trip budget doesn't shrink max range.
- **Pinned home** (`resolve_home_depot()` / `get_home_depot()` / `get_home_service()`; the logic is module functions in `lib/drone_depots.py`, `DroneEnergyMixin` keeps one-line wrappers): each drone pins a home, either an **outpost pool** (any Drone Depot in that outpost) or one **hardwired** depot — priority (1) `HOME_DEPOT` script variable in `drone.py` entrypoint (outpost id = pool; depot id/display name = hardwired — mirrors pioneer's `HOME_BASE`), (2) archived pin in shared dict `drone.home_depots` (same matching), (3) first run: pool of nearest depot's outpost. Pin is persisted, so a restart mid-field doesn't re-home to whichever depot is nearest there. With a pool, `get_home_depot()` re-picks per call via `_pick_free_depot()`: already-docked depot, else free bay first (live `get_component(depot_id).bay_count()/bays_occupied()`), then `prefer_id` (the depot already queued at — avoids churn when all full), then free cargo slots, then nearest. `fly_to_station()` returns early on `waiting_bay`, and `return_and_unload()` (`lib/drone_cargo.py`) then re-picks once and transfers to a free sibling (same coords, no flight cost), so drone A never waits on depot A while depot B is empty. Unload (`return_and_unload()`) and recall use the home depot; if it's removed, `get_home_depot()` re-homes via `DroneController.resolve_home()` (which also refreshes `home_outpost`/`home_biome`). **Home service** = drone_service in the home depot's outpost (else nearest to home depot); used by `calculate_trip_energy()` (return leg), `energy_needed_to_return_comfortably()`, and `return_to_service_for_charge()` (falls back to nearest service when home is out of reach on current battery). **Nearest** service still used for hard survival floor `return_floor_wh()` (in-flight abort) and `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()`. `discover_drone_buildings()` (memoized per script for `DISCOVERY_TTL_TICKS = 20`, as is `charging_stations.all_stations()` (wrapped by `VehicleEnergyMixin.get_all_charging_stations()`) with `STATION_DISCOVERY_TTL_TICKS = 20`; entries shared, read-only) returns `{"id", "name", "coords", "outpost", "outpost_id"}` dicts — `"outpost"` field is how `resolve_home()` sets `self.home_outpost`/`self.home_biome`, since **drone has no `.outpost` property** (confirmed against `docs/models/vehicles_and_modules.md`): home depot's outpost first, fallback nearest drone_service's outpost, then network home outpost. `return_to_service_for_charge()` checks "already there" by `current_station() == service id`, not coords (depot and service often share coords).
- **Depot sizes = three typeIds**: `item_tiers.DEPOT_TYPE_TIERS = ["drone_station", "drone_station_medium", "drone_station_large"]`, paired by index with `DEPOT_KIT_TIERS` (`DEPOT_KIT_FOR_TYPE`) (`outpost.buildings()` matches one exact type, so discovery loops over all three). Script-slot/instance prefixes differ again: `drone_station_N` / `drone_station_med_N` / `drone_station_lrg_N` (decompiled simworker machine catalog; large confirmed live) — `scripts/4_controlpanel/drone/drone_station_med.py`/`drone_station_lrg.py` are thin copies of `drone_station.py` so scripts_sync matches them. Every Depot lookup (`drone_energy`, `logistics_requests.local_depots()`, `site_supply`, `production_source`, `fleet_decommission`, `ArchiveCleaner.MACHINE_STATUS_KEYS`, autoplay roles) imports that list. `discover_drone_depots()` hides Depots a fleet upgrade is retiring (§2k).
- **`DRONE_DEPOT_TYPE_ID = "drone_station"`**, not `"drone_depot"` — in-game building `typeId` follows item/error-code naming (`drone_station_kit`, ship_computer.md's `"missing_drone_station"`/`"drone_station_full"`), not doc-page slug or display name "Drone Depot". A wrong typeId makes `outpost.buildings()` silently match nothing. See `docs/components/drone_depot.md` scripting note.
- **No "biosphere region"** — per-outpost + per-biome: sample only locally processable (Essence Liquifier) at outpost where dropped off, and only if native to that outpost's biome (`nocturna.life_form_biome(item_id) == outpost.biome`). Miner drone filters `journal.biomass_coords()` candidates to samples native to home outpost biome (`is_home_biome_sample()`). Biosite whose tile has **mixed** biome sample set skipped entirely (`PortableBioExtractor.extract()` takes no species arg, can't pull only home-biome sample). Cross-outpost ferrying of foreign samples = deferred TODO.md Phase 4 item.
- **Exclusive biosite claims, not shared yield-debit reservations** — biosite extraction exclusive-WITH-COOLDOWN (`journal.is_ready(x, y)`/`next_ready_at(x, y)`, `BioExtractionResult.status == "cooling"` only after full depletion), fundamentally different from mineral mining's shareable sites (§2b). `lib/drone_claims.py`'s `claim_biosite()`/`refresh_biosite_claim()`/`release_biosite_claim()` reuse `vehicle_claims.py` claim/heartbeat/staleness shape (`fleet_claims_common.CLAIM_STALE_TICKS`) but own archive key (`biosite.claims`) — **do not** route biosite selection through `mining_reservations.py`. Miner target order: (1) `journal.is_ready(x, y)` cooldown gate (read-only, before any claim); candidates sorted by **request score** first (§2i: per home-biome sample still on the site whose life form has a network deficit in `logistics_requests.network_deficits()`, `RARITY_REQUEST_WEIGHT = {"common": 1, "uncommon": 2, "rare": 4}`; 0 everywhere without requests), then **partially-drained** (`0 < remaining_tons < tons` — cooldown only starts once a site is empty, so finishing a half-drained site beats opening a fresh one), then nearest; (2) exclusive claim attempt; (3) scout's separate bounded "confirmed-empty POI" cache (`SCOUTED_EMPTY_POI_KEY`, coord → tick scanned, capped at `SCOUTED_EMPTY_POI_MAX_ENTRIES = 2000`), kept as separate small archive-key wrapper from `biosite.claims`.
- **Miner drone biosite order** (`DroneMiningMixin.biosite_candidates()`): candidates sorted by `(-band, -request_score, not partial, -buffer_need, distance)`. `band` = the highest band (`logistics_requests.network_band_deficits()`) in which a life form the site holds is still requested by some outpost, `request_score` = `RARITY_REQUEST_WEIGHT` summed over those forms, then partly drained sites (so their cooldown can start), then the home outpost's buffer need (after biomass completion), then distance. The fine prio plays no part; `select_biosite_target()` still skips candidates the drone cannot afford.
- **Resumability**: `drone.mission[<name>]` persists in-progress target (mirrors `vehicle.mission[<name>]`). `DroneClaimsMixin.begin_mission()`/`end_mission()` are the only writers of `DroneController.mission` (`lib/mission.py`); `current_target`/`current_target_key` are read-only views; `release_biosite_claim()` of the current key (or all) ends the mission. Drone `go_to()` fire-and-forget, **cancelled by script restart** (unlike rover's persistent `drive_to()`) — on resume, `BiositeJob.execute()` (`lib/job_biosite.py`) re-validates the position and **re-issues** `go_to()`. `extract()`/`scan()` survive restart transparently — only flight leg needs re-issue. While a pre-restart `extract()` still runs, every `go_to*()` is rejected `"busy"` (logged at debug, not warn). `adopt_interrupted_extraction()` (run once from `BiositeJob.prepare()`) uses `detect_role()`'s extract probe (`role_probe_status`): `"busy"`/`"ok"` means hovering mid-harvest, so it claims the current tile as the mission even without a saved one, and the resume path finishes it. `return_and_unload()` returns False on failure and the job sleeps `poll_interval` before retrying; a full home Depot sets `host.depot_full` (`DepotFull`: retry tick, count, depot) and after `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS = 3` the job delivers to another Depot outpost that requests the cargo (`BiositeJob._deliver_elsewhere()`).
- **Idle-to-recharge fallback**: `energy_needed_to_return_comfortably()` alone only covers "in danger where parked" case. Drone can be above that floor (safe to sit) yet below what any candidate's `calculate_trip_energy()` round trip needs (`IDLE_OUT_OF_RANGE` tick, not low-battery). `DroneJob.top_up_if_unreachable()` (`lib/drone_job.py`, used by every drone job's `pick()`) calls `DroneChargingMixin.return_to_service_for_charge()` (shared, dock-if-not-already-there) when the cycle finds candidates but none reachable AND charge below 98%.
- **Bay-full queueing** (Drone Depot only): `go_to_station()` itself queues drone in `"waiting_bay"` when Depot full (game-engine behavior, drone.md) — no script-side reservation needed for wait. `fly_to_station()` (`drone_navigation.py`) polls `current_station()` (documented authoritative arrival check) up to `timeout_ticks` (default scales with distance via `flight_timeout_ticks()` when target coords are known, min 1500 ≈ 150 real seconds); caller's raw-`fly_to()` fallback on timeout does NOT enter bay queue (only `go_to_station()` does), so self-healing only via outer loop's next retry, not real dock. `return_and_unload()` (`drone_cargo.py`) skips that fallback while `status() == "waiting_bay"` (publishes `WAITING_DEPOT_BAY`, retries) — fallback would "arrive" instantly at same coords and unload undocked into nothing. **Depot slot-full** (unload `target_full`, distinct from bay-full): `unload_and_leave()` leaves berth and sets a backoff of `DroneCargoMixin.DEPOT_FULL_RETRY_TICKS = 300` (~30 s) in `host.depot_full`; `wait_for_depot_space()` hovers off the berth (`hover_wait()`, free) reporting `WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE` with the Depot id as target (lets the Depot flush surplus), flying to a drone_service only below `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC`. After `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS = 3` full-Depot failures in a row, `BiositeJob._deliver_elsewhere()` (`job_biosite.py`, `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS` there) takes cargo another Depot outpost requests (`outpost_deficits(live=False)`) there once, if `calculate_trip_energy()` allows; else it keeps waiting. A roleless (bare/unequipped) drone deliberately **stays docked** (needs berth to be equipped), so it blocks a 1-bay Depot until equipped.
- **Stranded vs no route** (`drone_navigation.py`): `STRANDED_STATUSES` (`stalled_no_battery`, `stalled_no_oil`, `scrambled`) wait for a `drone_service` rescue. `NO_ROUTE_STATUS` (`stalled_no_route`: the game dropped the flight, target station gone or off the map) keeps the drone's energy, so `fly_to()`, `fly_to_station()` and `fly_to_drill()` end their wait at once and the caller picks a new target.
- **`hover_wait(state, target)`** (`drone_navigation.py`): `leave_station()` + telemetry — the standard "nothing to do here" wait: free the bay, hover in place (hovering burns nothing).
- **Idle frees the Depot bay** (`DroneJob.idle()` → `release_depot_bay()`, every drone role): docked at a Drone Depot (not a drone_service, which charges) with empty cargo → `leave_station()` and hover, so a Depot bay never sits on an idle drone (another drone's unload, a FLEET Commission deploy, §2k-2). Cargo aboard keeps the bay.
- **`leave_station()`** (`drone_navigation.py`): releases current berth via `undock()` — keeps exact position/cargo/modules, costs no flight energy, unlike re-issuing `fly_to()` to same coords. `unload_and_leave()` (`drone_cargo.py`) calls it right after every Depot unload attempt (success or full), so miner frees bay for waiting peer instead of squatting until next mission moves it. No-op on `"not_docked"`/`"busy"` (latter protects active Drone Service Station charge/rescue job, which must keep control — never call on charging drone).
- **Recall** (`lib/drone_claims.py`): mirrors `vehicle_claims.py`'s `RECALL_KEY` shape exactly, own key `drone.recall` `{drone_name: True}`. `is_drone_recalled()`/`set_drone_recalled()` module-level read/write, toggled via the FLEET card's Drones-tab recall switch (`vehicles_panel.py`). Unlike ground-vehicle recall (returns to charging-station "base"), drone recall targets its home **Drone Depot specifically, never drone_service** — `couple()`/`uncouple()` (module re-equip) both require docking at operational Depot (drone.md), and recall's point is letting operator swap modules. `handle_recall_if_active()` abandons biosite claim/mission, flies to Depot, and — deliberately opposite of `leave_station()` — stays docked rather than releasing berth, since re-equip needs berth held. Checked by `run_job_loop()` right after `DroneJob.hold()` (the stranded check) (stranded drone needs `drone_service` rescue regardless of recall — can't self-navigate) but before mission-resume/target-selection, so active mission abandoned promptly.
- **`lib/drone_service.py`** structurally mirrors `lib/charging.py`: docked charge queue, fleet-wide stranded/scrambled detection via `fleet.drones()` (stranded predicate also includes `"scrambled"`), nearest-station coordination (`is_nearest_station_to()`). No return-home command to a low unit (either station type): moving another machine is a remote write the game blocks (`docs/AI_CHEATSHEET.md`), and a unit whose script runs budgets its own way back; a unit below its rescue target only keeps the station busy (watching). Engine-aware: docked electric → `charge()`, docked heli → `refuel()` (engine probed by `battery.percent()` raising); `no_oil` warns once with a hint whether `oil_in` is wired. For a heli, only oil-holding stations (`station_refs_for()`) compete in `is_nearest_station_to()` and set the return floor, so a dry station never rescues a heli into an empty refuel queue. Rescue floors use `fuel_of(drone_ref)` (`battery_*` or `oil_*` DroneRef fields) and `drone_rescue_energy_per_meter(engine)` (assumes plating, conservative); heli reserves `RETURN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_T = RESCUE_EXTRA_RESERVE_T = 2.5` t. Heli rescue carries the drone home to the refuel queue (game behaviour). **Oil intake** (`ensure_oil_feed()`, every `step()`): once `research_heli_drones` is done, a `fluid_routing.FluidInputRouter` keeps `oil_in` on a reachable oil tank, else an Oil Pump (own outpost first, same routing as the Oil Generator); starved = buffer below `SERVICE_MIN_OIL_T` with no inflow. The station does not park while the router is `waiting` on a blacklisted source.
- **`lib/drone_depot.py`** mostly passive — cargo moves via drone's own `cargo.load()`/`cargo.unload()` while docked. Ports **don't** drain passively: declared link moves nothing until someone calls `take()`/`send()`, and Liquifier's own controller does the `take()` (§1h). Controller jobs: (1) one-time idempotent `self.output.connect(...)` wiring to outpost's Essence Liquifier, only when unambiguous (exactly one same-outpost Liquifier — else logged, left for manual wiring); (2) `stage_life_forms()`: every life form (`nocturna.life_form_biome()` not None) sent Depot → local Warehouse, capped at `LIFEFORM_BUFFER_SLOTS = 2` Warehouse stacks per form per outpost (slot capacity read from `slots()`, fallback `game_rules.WAREHOUSE_SLOT_UNITS`). Tops up a slot already holding the form first; opens an empty slot only while `storage.STAGING_FREE_SLOTS_KEEP = 1` more stay empty (room for ore/cargo). Full stack → rest stays in the Depot for the Liquifier (after biomass completion: for a hauler, or `flush_surplus()`). Keeps the 50-unit Depot free for drones, gives the Liquifier a buffer and pull haulers a `take()` source; re-declares the Liquifier link next step since `output` now points at the Warehouse; (3) `drain_freight()`: first `feed_local_smelters()` — each same-outpost Smelter with a fresh `smelter.wants` entry (§4 archive, `production_core.smelter_wants_at()`) gets that ore straight from the Depot, the most urgent want first (entry `prio`, then the larger `fill_to`), up to its published `fill_to` − its live input count; no entry (demand met, no recipe, a Supply Dock still shipping that ore raw, output blocked) = no push, so the input runs empty and the Smelter clears its recipe. A refused send (busy, other material) leaves the ore for storage. A transfer runs at its faster endpoint's speed (Depot 1/2/4 by size, ×2 with `depot_handling_unlock`; Warehouse 1), so this skips the slow Warehouse → Smelter leg and its Warehouse lock. Then every remaining **non-life-form** stack → local storage via `storage.drain_port_to_storage(include=, allow_partial=True)` (Warehouse, or Inventory at home; trickles into partial room). Makes the Depot usable as a hauler endpoint — its 50/100/200-unit stockpile is tiny next to a Large hauler's load, so the drone unloads in rounds while this drains; the controller polls every `FREIGHT_POLL_INTERVAL = 2.0` s while freight sits there, a stage request is open or any drone is docked (else 10 s); (4) periodic telemetry publish (`drone_depot.status`); (5) `fulfil_stage()` (first each step): hauler stage requests (`depot.stage`, `lib/depot_stage.py`) → `take_item(self.input, ...)` from local Warehouses/home Inventory up to requested − already in stockpile, within free units (`input.capacity()`) and material slots; a full stockpile (no free units, or a new material with no free slot) first triggers drain/stage/`flush_surplus(for_stage=True)`. Staged items are skipped by `drain_freight()` and `stage_life_forms()`; (6) `flush_surplus()`: only when the stockpile is full (units or slots) and a drone is docked or reported `WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE` for this Depot within `WAITING_DRONE_FRESH_TICKS = 6000`. Surplus = unstaged life form whose Warehouse stash can't grow (≥ `lifeform_buffer_cap()`, or `buffer_target()` finds no slot) and ≥ `retain_amount()` + `network_deficits()`. `input.flush()` discards the **whole** stockpile, so everything else is drained first and it flushes only when nothing but surplus is left (else warns). Logs what it destroyed. The only life-form destruction left: a last resort so drones can unload; miner drones skip sites whose forms are all at buffer (§1h-1), so it rarely fires.

### §2j Floating Drone Hauler (`lib/job_haul.py` `HaulJob` + `lib/drone_hauler.py` `DroneHaulerMixin` legs + `lib/drone_haul_plan.py` module functions: drills and Depot outposts → Depots)

Drone with Cargo Pods and no bio module. **No home Depot**: each cycle picks the best job network-wide, refuels at whichever drone_service is nearest when needed, and hovers in place when idle (`hover_idle()`: stays if already docked at a service, flies to one below `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC` or after `HAUL_IDLE_CHARGE_TICKS` idle; never holds a Depot bay). Hard limits (drone.md / `DroneCargo`): `cargo.load()` only at a docked Drone Depot, a field Mining Drill or a Lead Cask; `cargo.unload()` only into a docked Depot (or Lead Cask) — **no direct Warehouse/Inventory access**. One material per Cargo Pod (100/250/500); Large chassis = 1 thruster + 5 modules. Drill pickups need no staging; outpost pickups go through Depot-side staging (`depot.stage`). Planning (Demand, Sources, Plan, stall memory below) lives in `drone_haul_plan.py`; it reads game state but never moves the drone or writes the archive. The legs (dock, load at a drill or staged Depot, deliver, hover idle) are `DroneHaulerMixin` abilities in `drone_hauler.py`; the role (`run_job_loop(HaulJob(drone))`: cargo aboard first, launch/recharge floors, urgent Storm Glass, plan, claim the route, fly the legs, deliver) is `HaulJob` in `job_haul.py`. The delivery in progress is the drone's `Mission` of kind `"haul"` (target `{"dest": outpost_id}`, no claim key; `load_mission()` restores it without a claim check).

- **Demand** (`haul_destinations()`): every outpost with a Depot, home included → `logistics_requests.outpost_tier_deficits(live=True)` (net of in-flight pickups) as dest `"tiers"` — same demand the Pioneer pull hauler (§2i) reads, so they share it. `FAIR_BANDS` capped per destination by `fair_share_tiers()` over the other sources (§2i). Home with requests but no Depot → one warning per run.
- **Sources**: `drill_sources()` — advertised drills (`drill.status`) standing on a surveyed site (`drill_sites.drill_positions()`), net of `reserved_from()`, unlocated drills skipped (warned once); `outpost_sources()` — every Depot outpost with free stock (`outpost_free_tiers(loader=LOADER_DRONE)`: Warehouses + Depot stockpiles + home Inventory, own request's level at the asking band, or its `keep`, kept back), never the destination itself; `shop_sources()` — per non-home destination with buyable deficits (`buyable_deficits()`, remote Bio Lab reagents §2g) one Shop source at home's Depots (id `shop:<dest>`, `"for_dest"`; `sources_for()` offers it to that destination only), listed last so free stock wins a tie; none without a Shop or a home Depot. At the stop `load_at_depot()` docks at the home Depot and, each round, buys what is neither aboard nor bought yet (`buy_at_shop()`: one Inventory stack at a time within `inventory_room()`, cash consumer `drone_reagents:<drone>`, operating; a denial, refused purchase or full Inventory ends that item's buy) and stages only the bought units not yet aboard, so free home stock stays with its own reservations; an item with nothing bought and nothing left to load is dropped. Bought units the drone doesn't load stay in home Inventory as free stock.
- **Plan** (`plan_haul_job()`): per destination a nearest-neighbour chain over drills and outposts (bands in `TIERS` order via `plan_take()`, then largest deficits; per-item room from a pod model: `pod_sizes()` reads `drone.modules()` in slot order, `CARGO_POD_UNITS` = 100/250/500, halved when plated; `load_pods()` fills the item's own pods, then empty ones, one material per pod, so a stop whose materials find no free or matching pod is never planned; `source_useful(..., room)` skips items without room), up to `HAUL_MAX_STOPS_PER_TRIP = 3`, chained stops only if direct ≤ `HAUL_CHAIN_MAX_DETOUR_RATIO = 0.75` × via-destination. Skipped below `logistics_requests.min_load_units()` (`min(HAUL_MIN_LOAD_UNITS = 10, reachable)`, shared with ground haulers) (deficit units the sources hold at all), or below 1 when the route carries `BLOCKER` units (`logistics_requests.blocker_units()`: nothing more arrives by waiting). Ranked by `logistics_requests.haul_rank()` (a 9-tuple, §2i) over (route m + `trip_overhead_m()`: `HAUL_TRIP_OVERHEAD_M = 300` × engine speed / electric speed, so 900 m for a heli, the overhead standing for dock and load time): per band the units serving it or a higher band per meter, `BLOCKER` first, compared lexicographically (`route_tier_units()`); best wins, and the next `HAUL_CLAIM_ALTERNATES = 4` ride along as `plan["alternates"]`. **Heli yield** (electric haulers only, `yield_to_helis()`): while a heli hauler is idle (`drone.heli_free` entry no older than `HELI_FREE_STALE_TICKS = 300`), a candidate carrying `BLOCKER` units or spanning ≥ `HELI_YIELD_MIN_M = 1500` m is dropped when the nearest idle heli's ETA (its flight to the first stop + the rest of the route, 900 m/h) is ≤ `HELI_YIELD_ETA_RATIO = 0.75` × the electric's (300 m/h); the electric takes its next-best job or idles, never waits. Per destination the yield lasts ≤ `HELI_YIELD_MAX_TICKS = 600`, then the electric takes it (`HaulMemory.heli_yields`, forgotten after `HELI_YIELD_FORGET_TICKS = 6000`), so a heli that never picks it (out of range, a better job) can't starve it. Each plan, a heli `HaulJob` sets its entry when it found no job and drops it when it found one (`_announce_free()`). One candidate per destination × first source, except a first source an earlier route to the same destination already visits (counted as covered); `shortest_order()` puts each route's stops (≤ 3, brute force, loads kept) in the shortest start → stops → destination order. One debug line per plan: routes atomic / stepped (over-cap destinations with sources/items) / covered. **Fuel budget** (`route_fuel()`): route legs at cruise throttle + nearest-service-from-destination at the speedmode floor, × `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER` + `emergency_reserve()` — "back" is any service, not a home. Job over a full tank → dropped; over what's aboard → `refuel()` at nearest service first, then re-plan. **Planning cost**: one `logistics_requests.PlanReads` per plan reads active requests, `logistics.pickups` (the `pickups_snapshot()` that `claim_pickups()` later trims against; `in_flight()`/`reserved_from()` for every id from one pass) and each Depot outpost's stock (`outpost_stock()` over every requested item, Depots included) once, shared by `outpost_tier_deficits()`, `outpost_tier_free()` and `fair_share_tiers()`, so an outpost that is both destination and source is read once; the outpost list and Depots (`depot_layout()`) are read once too. The Pioneer's `pull_plan.pull_sources()` uses one for requests and reservations. The pod sizes and the fuel rates (`fuel_rates()`) are read once per plan, `reachable` once per destination; each destination plans over `sources_for()`: the sources other than itself, each cut to the items it still misses (sources left empty dropped; same routes, but a network-wide item list on one source kept every candidate out of an atomic call: live 6-outpost network 278k → 105k opcodes per plan); each destination's distances (start, destination, every source; `distance_table()`, one atomic call up to `DISTANCE_TABLE_ATOMIC_MAX = 16` sources) and its tail to the nearest service are built once and shared by its candidates; each candidate (route + units, units per tier, meters, fuel) is one pure `haul_candidate()` call, run as a `lib/atomic.py` call (at most one tick each) when `drone_haul_plan.haul_atomic_ok(dest, dest_sources)`: `HAUL_COST_BASE = 5900` + `HAUL_COST_PER_SOURCE = 80` × sources + `HAUL_COST_PER_ITEM_TIER = 80` × items of the `HAUL_MAX_STOPS_PER_TRIP` sources holding the most × demand tiers the destination asks for ≤ `ROUTE_ATOMIC_MAX_COST` (only a stop's items are ranked, taken and loaded; fitted with 1/0.85 margin over ~16k random and adversarial routes, 1-12 sources, 1-14 items each, 1-9 tiers, 1-5 pods; candidates under the gate measured ≤ ~7,900 opcodes; the planners walk only the tiers with demand, `route_left()`; `pod_space()` tracks pod room incrementally). Per destination, `_dest_prep()` (`sources_for()`, cost terms, service tail, `reachable_units()`) is one atomic call when `DEST_PREP_COST_BASE = 1500` + `DEST_PREP_COST_PER_SOURCE = 60` × sources + `DEST_PREP_COST_PER_ITEM = 30` × items fits. `_cap_fair_tiers()` computes rival deficits once per plan (`logistics_requests.fair_rival_bands()`, `FAIR_RIVAL_CHUNK = 10` entries per atomic call) and caps each destination with a fair band in one atomic call when `FAIR_COST_BASE = 1000` + `FAIR_COST_PER_DEFICIT_ITEM = 160` × deficit items + `FAIR_COST_PER_SOURCE_ITEM = 24` × source items per fair band fits. `outpost_sources()` computes free stock only at the bands some destination misses (`outpost_tier_free(tiers=)`). The Pioneer pull planner gates on `logistics_requests.route_atomic_ok(dest_sources)`: estimated `ROUTE_COST_BASE = 4000` + `ROUTE_COST_PER_SOURCE = 240` × sources + `ROUTE_COST_PER_ITEM = 350` × items summed over all sources ≤ `ROUTE_ATOMIC_MAX_COST = 10000` CPython opcodes (each stop's `source_useful()` pass walks every item of every source left) (fitted upper bound on random home-shaped cases, 1-12 sources, 1-14 items each, 1-5 pods; candidates under the gate measured ≤ ~9,700 opcodes, a game step being 1.6-3 opcodes, so well under the 10,000-step callback cap). The numbers were fitted with two-band loops; the planners (`plan_take()`, `route_left()`, `route_tier_units()`) now loop all nine bands per item, so the estimate is optimistic for a worst case with every band present until it is refitted ([TODO.md](../../TODO.md), Priority ladder follow-ups). Larger networks plan directly. Usefulness per source: `logistics_requests.source_useful()` (same result as any `plan_take()` > 0). Shared with the Pioneer pull hauler.
- **Outpost pickup**: at claim, each outpost leg picks a Depot (`stage_depot_for()`: most of the planned items already in its stockpile, else a free one; `HaulJob.leg_depots`) and writes `depot_stage.request_stage()` so the Depot stages during the flight. `load_at_depot()` docks (`dock_at_depot()`, same bay wait as delivery), `cargo.load()` in rounds every `DEPOT_UNLOAD_RETRY_S`, shrinking the stage request to what is still missing after each round; stops when done, after `DOCK_IDLE_MAX_S = 60` s without progress or `DEPOT_LOAD_TIMEOUT_S = 120` s overall. Reservations corrected to what loaded, stage cleared, bay left.
- **Coordination**: planned units reserved per source (drill or outpost) in `logistics.pickups` (dest + source) by `claim_pickups()`, trimmed by what others reserved since planning; a claim left under its minimum tries the alternates (`HaulJob._claim_alternate()`: one claim each, only those the fuel aboard covers) before replanning next cycle; corrected to loaded amounts, released after full delivery. Mission `drone.mission[<name>] = {"kind": "haul", "dest", "target_key": None}` (no `target_key` → biosite `load_mission()` ignores it).
- **Deliver** (`deliver()`): free Depot of the destination (`_pick_free_depot()`, waits ≤ `DEPOT_BAY_WAIT_S = 60` s in `waiting_bay`), unload rounds every `DEPOT_UNLOAD_RETRY_S = 2` s while the Depot drains (§2h), give up after `DOCK_IDLE_MAX_S = 60` s without progress or `DEPOT_UNLOAD_TIMEOUT_S = 300` s overall → `hover_wait("WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE", depot)` (lets the Depot flush surplus), keep cargo/mission/reservations, retry next cycle. Cargo aboard at step start (restart, failed delivery) → `HaulJob.prepare_cargo()` / `pick_delivery_outpost()`: the haul mission's dest, else Depot outpost with most demand for the cargo, else home, else nearest Depot (destinations on stall cooldown skipped); reservations re-asserted from what's physically aboard.
- **Stalls** (in memory per drone, reset by a restart): a destination or source outpost failing (no dock, nothing loaded, Depot took no more) `STALL_MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3` times in a row is skipped for `STALL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. A destination going on cooldown with cargo aboard clears the saved mission, so the next cycle requeues the cargo to the next-best destination.
- **Refuel** (`DroneChargingMixin.refuel()`, `lib/drone_charging.py`): fly to nearest (oil-fed, for heli) service; its station script queues the charge/refuel; wait until ≥ `REFUEL_FULL_LEVEL = 0.98`, or ≥ needed once the service job ended, ≤ `REFUEL_TIMEOUT_S = 900` s. Between jobs (no cargo aboard) below `HAUL_RECHARGE_SOC = 0.30` the hauler docks at the nearest service; once docked, launch floor `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC` holds it until topped up (30% → 80% hysteresis). Idle (no haul job) `HAUL_IDLE_CHARGE_TICKS = 1200` ticks in a row below `REFUEL_FULL_LEVEL` → `hover_idle()` docks at the nearest service too (`HaulJob.idle_since`, reset by a job or cargo aboard), so it waits there charged instead of hovering. Without the trigger, always-available short outpost jobs fit the per-job budget and the hauler chains down to the emergency reserve.
- Recall uses the pinned home Depot as usual (`HOME_DEPOT` otherwise ignored for haulers). Stub-tested only.
- **Storm Glass between jobs** (§2j-1): with empty cargo, `HaulJob.pick()` tries `pick_aftermath_pickup(urgent_only=True)` before planning (sites expiring within `HAULER_GLASS_URGENT_GH = 24` h), `urgent_only=False` when no haul job; `execute()` flies it with `collect_aftermath()`. The glass stays aboard and goes out through the normal cargo-aboard delivery.

### §2j-1 Aftermath Collector (`lib/job_aftermath.py` `AftermathJob` + `lib/drone_weather.py` `DroneWeatherMixin`)

Collects storm aftermaths decoded into `weather.aftermaths` (§1m). Role `"aftermath"` = Cargo Pods + **Shield Plating** and no bio module (`detect_role()` checks plating before the hauler fallback). Plated drones take Raw Uranium, and Storm Glass only when no uranium site is claimable (`aftermath_passes()`: uranium pass first, glass pass second; plating costs 1.5× fuel and half the Cargo Pod capacity); unplated drones (haulers, §2j) take Storm Glass only, never uranium (+40 exposure per batch, scramble at 100).

- **Launch timing**: a site is a candidate once `now + travel_h >= ready_gh` (`launch_due()`; travel at `cruise_throttle`), and skipped when arrival + `EXPIRY_MARGIN_GH = 1.0` passes `expires_gh`. Order: soonest expiry, then distance. On site the drone hovers until `ready_gh + READY_MARGIN_GH = 0.1` (`WAITING_AFTERMATH`, polls every `WAIT_POLL_S = 10` s).
- **Exact coordinate**: `go_to()` snaps the drone to the exact target and `collect()` matches `x`/`y` exactly (simworker `zw()`), so `fly_to(..., precision=0.01)`.
- **Collect loop** (`_collect_at_site()`): `collect()` until cargo `space_for(item)` is 0, `limit` units came aboard, or `nothing_here`. `moving`/`busy` retried `SETTLE_RETRIES = 10` × 1 s. `nothing_here` after units came out = exhausted; with none, `NOTHING_HERE_LIMIT = 3` in a row (re-issuing `go_to()` if off-coordinate) = exhausted. Result written back into the site entry (`collected` += units, `exhausted`), so no drone flies there again.
- **Uranium gate**: `research_shielded_depot_ops` unlocked and unreserved Lead Cask room at the drone's home outpost (`cask_room()`: casks latched to `raw_uranium` or empty, capped at `uranium_want()` = `lead_cask.URANIUM_STOCK_TARGET = 100` + Raw Uranium Supply Dock orders at that outpost still owe − cask stock, minus other drones' `lead_cask.inbound` entries for that outpost), floored to whole `COLLECT_BATCH_UNITS = 5` batches (`batch_limit()`; one `collect()` moves up to 5, so less room launches nothing and no overshoot lands in the next empty cask). `cargo.unload()` at the home Depot puts hot cargo into one Lead Cask per call; `unload_cargo_at_depot()` repeats up to `UNLOAD_MAX_ROUNDS = 4` calls per item while each still moves something. `cask_missing` leaves cargo aboard like a full Depot.
- **Cask room reservation** (`lead_cask.reserve_inbound()`): after winning the site claim, the drone reserves its share atomically against the other carriers' entries; the grant, floored to whole batches (the entry narrowed to match), is the trip's `limit` (under one batch = reservation and claim released, next candidate). After the trip the entry is set to the units aboard (`set_inbound()`) and dropped once no Raw Uranium is aboard (`_sync_cask_reservation()`, also run before each target pick). Entries expire after `INBOUND_STALE_TICKS = 36000` and are pruned on the next write.
- **Intent**: after a trip the fleet card shows `hauling <item> from <event> to <home>` while cargo is aboard, then no job; a hauler's Storm Glass pickup clears it and its delivery sets its own.
- **Claims**: exclusive, in `biosite.claims` under `aftermath_<event_id>` (§2h claim helpers, same staleness). Released after each trip; a site needing several trips is claimed again by whichever drone is free. Mission `drone.mission[<name>] = {"kind": "aftermath", "target_key", "target"}`; after a reload the drone (or hauler) resumes the saved site first.
- **Job** (`run_job_loop(AftermathJob(drone))`): stranded (`DroneJob.hold()`) / recall / upgrade checks, then resume, unload cargo aboard (`drone_cargo.return_and_unload()`), charge, pick and claim (`begin_aftermath_mission()`), fly, wait, collect, release, unload. Idle: `IDLE_NO_TARGETS`, polls every `IDLE_POLL_S = 30` s. Stub-tested only.

### §2k Fleet Hardware Upgrade (`lib/fleet_upgrade.py` coordinator + `lib/drone_upgrade.py` drone side)

Background task in headless `builder_automation.py` (every `FLEET_TICK_INTERVAL`, panels.md §7), **gated by `upgrades_active()`** = operator switch on AND mining-drill phase reached (`upgrade_phase_reached()`: any mining drill on a surveyed site per `drill_sites.drill_positions()`, same condition as `4_controlpanel/.criteria`; stored once as `fleet.upgrade["phase_reached"]`, monotonic) — earlier, expanding beats upgrading. Switch off → swaps not started yet (Depot `ordered`; drone `ordered`/`requested`/`ready`/`announced`) are cancelled, a parked drone resumes work, orders withdrawn; swaps past the point of no return still finish. In-place module upkeep obeys the same gate. Upgrades Drone Depots in place and swaps miner/hauler drones for the **best unlocked tier** (Fabricator recipe unlocked, or item already in Inventory). **One swap at a time fleet-wide, Depots first**; within a kind the **lowest tier first** (small → large directly; Medium only deployed while Large is locked). Scouts never touched (chassis or modules). Operator switch + status line on the FLEET card's Drones tab (`vehicles_panel.py`) (`fleet.upgrade["enabled"]`, default on). All state in one archive dict `fleet.upgrade` (layout in `drone_upgrade.py`, plus `phase_reached`); each coordinator pass advances at most one state and writes back → restart-safe. Drone undeploy refused `MAX_UNDEPLOY_ATTEMPTS = 5` times → entry `blocked`, drone back in service (operator deletes the entry to retry).

- **Depot upgrade** (in place, game build e1986ce): `ordered` (kit → `fabricator.orders` at `UPGRADE`, wait for it in Inventory) → `computer.upgrade(kit, depot)` → done. The Depot keeps its id, name, stockpile, script, home pins and docked drones (the game only swaps the typeId and raises `bays`/stack slots); the old kit returns to Inventory. `DEPOT_UPGRADE_WAIT_STATUSES` (`not_enough_power`, `under_construction`, `inventory_full`, `item_not_in_inventory`) wait and retry; any other refusal → `blocked`. A Depot already at the target size (upgraded by hand; the game would answer `wrong_machine_type`) or gone → entry dropped. A legacy deploy/drain/undeploy swap entry is dropped with `retiring_depots`.
- **Drone swap**: `ordered` (chassis → `fabricator.orders` entry) → `requested` → drone answers at the top of its loop (`handle_upgrade_request_if_active()`, after recall): finishes cargo/claimed target first, charges to `UPGRADE_MIN_SOC = 0.98` at its drone_service, docks at its home Depot, writes `ready` + its script variables (`params`: `HOME_DEPOT` override) and holds (`UPGRADE_HOLD`) → `announced` (waits `ANNOUNCE_SAVE_WAIT_TICKS = 1800` so the announcement reaches the save file before the new slot exists — autosave ~30 s real) → `swapping` (re-verify docked at a Depot + cargo 0 via `fleet.drones()`/`get_component(id).cargo`; stop, undeploy → modules back to Inventory; `deploy(chassis, outpost)` or adopt a new drone of that kind not in the pre-swap snapshot; write `lineage[new_id] = {from, role, engine, kind, params, fitted: False}`; move `drone.home_depots` pin, drop `drone.recall`/`drone.mission`/`fleet.status`/upgrade-order entries of the old id) → `attach` (start retried until `fleet.status[new]` appears) → `fitting` → done when `lineage[new].fitted`, then `computer.rename(new, old display name)` (`old_name`, captured at `ordered`). Candidates need role `miner`/`hauler` in `fleet.status` (published from `DroneController.role`) and a heartbeat ≤ `DRONE_ALIVE_TICKS = 600` old.
- **New chassis fitting** (`fit_loadout_if_new()`, `DroneController.run()` loops it every 30 s **before** `detect_role()`, which refuses a bare drone): couples thruster (slot 0, from `lineage.engine`), then `FIT_LOADOUTS[role][kind]` from Inventory, best tier present; empty slots ordered. `fitted` once thruster + energy + (extractor for miner / Bio Scanner for scout / Cargo Pod for hauler / Shield Plating and Cargo Pod for aftermath). Replacement drones also inherit `params` for any script variable left at `None` (`inherited_params()`), so a hand-pasted default script behaves like the old drone.
- **`LOADOUTS`** (one category per module slot; energy = `battery_pack` electric / best `oil_tank_*` heli; cargo = best `cargo_pod_*`; role = `portable_bio_extractor` (miner) / `shield_plating` (aftermath)): miner small `role, energy` · medium `role, energy, cargo` · large `role, energy, cargo, cargo, cargo`; hauler small `energy, cargo` · medium `energy, cargo, cargo` · large `energy, cargo, cargo, cargo, cargo`; aftermath medium `role, energy, cargo` · large `role, energy, cargo, cargo, cargo` (no small: no slot left for a Cargo Pod). One `energy` is the minimum: **`fit_slot_plan()`** adds energy slots (beside the first, each replacing the last `cargo`, keeping one) until `drone_energy.energy_modules_needed()` reaches **`TARGET_RANGE_M = 4000`** m (`loadout_range_m()`: capacity × `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC` − reserve, full throttle, × `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER`, plated burn for aftermath; capacities in `ENERGY_MODULE_CAPACITY`). Result: 1 Battery Pack (plated too), 2 Small Oil Tanks, 1 Medium/Large. Commission specs, new-chassis fit and upkeep all use it; heli conversion couples only that many tanks. **`FIT_LOADOUTS`** = `LOADOUTS` + scout (role = `portable_bio_scanner`): small `role, energy` · medium `role, energy, energy` · large `role` + 4× `energy`; used only for commission specs and first fit, so scouts are never upgraded or topped up. Module slots (small 2, medium 3, large 5) come from `drone.modules()`.
- **In-place module upkeep** (`maintain_modules_at_depot()`, right after a successful unload while docked and empty — `drone_hauler.deliver()`, `drone_cargo.unload_and_leave()`): Cargo Pod / Oil Tank → better unlocked tier once in Inventory (else ordered under the drone's id); then energy modules beyond `TARGET_RANGE_M` go back to Inventory, smallest first (`_surplus_energy_slots()`; a stripped slot's pending tier order is dropped); empty slots filled with whatever category `LOADOUTS` still lacks. **Engine type changes only electric → heli**, two ways, both gated on `heli_ready()` (`research_heli_drones` done, Heli Thruster and some Oil Tank craftable or in Inventory, some drone_service `service_can_refuel()`): (1) the Drones tab "-> heli" button (`fleet.upgrade["heli_requests"]`, cleared on retire) makes `convert_to_heli_if_requested()`, at the start of the same upkeep, swap the thruster and every Battery Pack for the best Oil Tank in Inventory, ordering missing parts under the drone's id meanwhile; (2) the "heli on unlock" switch (`fleet.upgrade["heli_on_unlock"]`, off by default) makes `commission_engine()` answer "heli" for new commissions (`build_drone_spec(engine=)`) and chassis swaps (`fleet_upgrade`), which `fit_loadout_if_new()` then fits heli. Existing drones never convert on their own. Old thrusters and packs return to Inventory; `FLEET_PART_KEEP` parks the surplus in a Warehouse. Slot contents come from `drone.modules()` (`_read_slots()`: `{index: module_id | None}`, 0 = thruster); `couple()`/`uncouple()` update it within the same call, so nothing is stored.
- **Drone hardware stays in Inventory**: `storage.INVENTORY_ONLY_ITEM_IDS` (Depot kits, Service Station kit, chassis, thrusters, pods, tanks, battery pack, bio modules, plating) are never warehoused and are reclaimed if found in a Warehouse — `deploy()`/`couple()` take from Inventory only.
- **Script attach**: scripts can't attach scripts (`run_control.apply_variant()` needs an operator-saved variant, and variants don't carry across drone sizes — TODO.md). `devtools/scripts_sync.py` fills the new slot (§9: replacement and commissioned drones get their params without prompting); the coordinator's `run_control.start()` retry starts it even without `--auto`.

### §2k-2 Fleet Commissioning (`lib/fleet_commission.py` coordinator + `lib/pioneer_commission.py` Pioneer side + `lib/drone_commission.py` drone presets)

Operator-triggered launch of new Pioneers and drones. The FLEET card's Commission tab (`vehicles_panel.py`, `lib/commission_card.py`, §7) queues a job per click; headless `builder_automation.py` runs `FleetCommissionCoordinator.step()` every `FLEET_TICK_INTERVAL`, and every pass (`FAST_SLEEP_S = 1.0` s apart, storage swaps skipped) while `commission_fast()` holds: a head job in its kind's `FAST_STATES` (Pioneer `queued`/`deploying`/`attach`; drone also `fitting`). The card logs each order and cancel at info level (`[COMMISSION] Ordered d<N>: ...`). Autobuild queues Pioneers only (`early_buyer`, `production_logistics.md` §2m). Each job carries `prio`, the order-book band of its kit (`job_prio()`, `lib/priority.py`): `MANUAL` for a card click (also jobs without the field), `BUILD` for autobuild. Automatic fleet upgrades order through their own requesters at `UPGRADE`. No gate beyond `version_mismatch()` (the whole automation block).

- **State**: one archive dict `fleet.commission` = `{jobs: [...], lineage: {vehicle_id: ...}, target_home, drone_outpost, status, seq}` (layout in `pioneer_commission.py`). Jobs carry `kind` (`pioneer`, the default for jobs without one, or `drone`; ids `p<seq>`/`d<seq>`). Per kind the head job = first not `blocked`; one Pioneer job and one drone job are worked per pass, each advanced until a state waits (unchanged or `blocked`), at most `MAX_ADVANCES_PER_PASS = 5`, written back after every state → restart-safe; a drone whose kit is already in Inventory goes `queued` → `attach` (script started) in one pass.
- **Pioneers are deployed at their `home_base` outpost** (`computer.deploy("pioneer", home_base)`; build e1986ce places it inside that outpost, fitting happens there). The card's `home:` picker (`target_home`, None = home) sets the job's `home_base`, which also becomes the new Pioneer's `HOME_BASE` (§2f/§2g: every role works for its HOME_BASE).
- **Pioneer states**: `queued` (spec from `PIONEER_PRESETS` at the best unlocked tier of each part, fresh Shop catalogue; a locked part → `blocked`) → `buying` (the chassis kit alone, once the cash manager grants it, consumer `pioneer_commission`, §2l, planned against the whole job's cost; parts follow after the deploy, so a nearly full Inventory doesn't stall the job) → `deploying` (Pioneer id snapshot stored first, next pass adopts a new id not in it or calls `computer.deploy("pioneer")` at home; `deploy_limit`/`location_not_found`/`not_deployable`/`locked`/`wrong_biome_for_machine` → `blocked`, `no_kit` → back to `buying`; writes `lineage[new_id] = {role, job, spec, home_base, fitted: False, missing: {}}`) → `attach` (`run_control.start()` retried until `fleet.status[new_id]` appears or lineage says fitted; `devtools/scripts_sync.py` fills the new `pioneer_N` slot with `HOME_BASE` from the lineage, §9) → `fitting` (the Pioneer takes each part from Inventory as it lands; the coordinator buys `lineage.missing` in batches clipped to `inventory.space_for()`, Inventory full → waits, each batch under its own grant; after a buy it waits `FIT_SETTLE_PASSES = 2` more fitting passes (`lineage.passes` vs `job.bought_pass`) so a report from a pass that checked Inventory before the buy never buys twice) → done when `lineage.fitted` **and** the Pioneer's `fleet.status[new_id].home` (every vehicle publishes its `home_base`) is the job's home_base; a mismatch keeps the job open with "set HOME_BASE" in the status line.
- **Display name**: right after the deploy (or the adopt after a restart), `commission_name()` renames the new vehicle `<home tag>_<role>_<id number>`: `pioneer_14` mining for `outpost_3` becomes `O3_miner_14`, home is `H` (`H_hauler_5`). The id number keeps names unique. A refused `rename()` keeps the game's name (debug log only). Ids never change, so slots, claims and archive keys are unaffected. A drone swap (§2k) carries the name to the new chassis.
- **Drones are crafted** (Shop-only parts aside, see `crafting` below), and deployed at the outpost the card's `at` picker names (`drone_outpost`, None = home; only outposts with a Drone Depot are offered). That outpost becomes the drone's `HOME_DEPOT` (haulers still float between jobs, §2k). Roles `DRONE_ROLES` = hauler, miner, aftermath (scouts stay hand-built); engine `COMMISSION_ENGINE = "electric"`.
- **Drone states**: `queued` (`build_drone_spec()`: best chassis of `DRONE_CHASSIS_TIERS` the Fabricator can craft or Inventory holds, `electric_thruster`, one module per `FIT_LOADOUTS[role][chassis]` slot at its best obtainable tier (craftable, held, or sold by the Shop); nothing obtainable → `blocked`) → `crafting` (parts the Fabricator can't craft but the Shop sells go in `spec["buy"]` and are bought here first, through the cash manager, consumer `drone_commission`, §2l; the rest of the kit is a `fabricator.orders` entry at the job's band (`job_prio()`) under requester `COMMISSION_REQUESTER = "fleet_commission"`, listed in `production.STANDING_ORDER_REQUESTERS`; re-synced every pass from the crafting job, withdrawn when none. The same entry also orders the craftable kit parts of every drone job still `queued` behind the head job (`_backlog_parts()`, a spec built each pass) at `BACKLOG_PRIO = UPGRADE`, so the Fabricator works ahead while a kit is hauled and deployed) until Inventory holds all of it; parts a remote fab site built come home on site supply's home pull request (§2i-1 consumer site) and unload straight into Inventory (`storage.best_unload_target()`, §2c); the status splits `crafting (…)` (not built anywhere), `hauling home (…)` (`in_flight(home)`) and `built, awaiting haul home (…)` (`SourceCache.network_stock()` − Inventory, nothing in flight), and warns once per job after `HAUL_HOME_WARN_TICKS = REQUEST_STALE_TICKS` (6000) of the last case: home needs a Pioneer pull hauler based there or a Drone Depot for floating drone haulers → `deploying` (waits while a fleet_upgrade drone swap is `announced`/`swapping`, since both adopt "a new drone of this chassis"; drone id snapshot, then `computer.deploy(chassis, outpost)`; `drone_station_full` waits, `missing_drone_station` and the Pioneer blocking statuses → `blocked`, `no_kit` → back to `crafting`; writes `fleet.upgrade.lineage[new_id] = {from: None, job, role, engine, kind, params: {HOME_DEPOT}, fitted: False}`) → `attach` (as for Pioneers) → `fitting` (the drone couples its kit via the unchanged `fit_loadout_if_new()`, §2k; its `inherited_params()` give it `HOME_DEPOT` even with a default-filled script) → done when that lineage says fitted. fleet_upgrade's own swap adoption skips drones that already have a lineage entry.
- **`blocked`**: skipped by later passes; the card's cancel button drops it. Cancellable states `CANCELLABLE_STATES = ("queued", "buying", "crafting", "blocked")`. Bought or crafted parts stay in Inventory and are reused by the next job. A job whose deployed vehicle vanished (`attach`/`fitting`) → `blocked` (`vehicle gone`); Pioneer lineage without a live vehicle or job is pruned.
- **`PIONEER_PRESETS`** (one part category per chassis slot, mount order; tiers from `pioneer_upgrade.py` §2b-1, nav is always `nav_module`, Sport Nav stays manual): hauler `nav, battery×2, cargo×5` (a start only: §2b-2 re-splits it) · miner `nav, drill, battery×4, cargo×2` (a start only: §2b-2 re-splits it) · scout `nav, sonar, battery×6` · constructor `nav, constructor, battery×4, cargo×2` (early jobs carry 1–2 item kinds of ≤ 20 each, so range beats cargo). Bays get Heavy Portable Battery/Bin once unlocked, else the base one (same density policy as §2b-1). Role then follows from `detect_role()` (§2b).
- **Fitting (`LoadoutFittingMixin.fit_commissioned_loadout()`)**, first thing in `PioneerController.run()`, before `detect_role()`: no-op without a lineage entry or when fitted. `fit_loadout(spec)` publishes `FITTING`, then passes: mount each unmounted spec module into the first free slot (`capability_already_mounted` counts as mounted), then install the fill into every empty Battery Holder / Cargo Rack bay; after each call polls `modules()` up to `FIT_POLL_TRIES = 10` × `FIT_POLL_S = 0.5` s. A part not in Inventory → `lineage.missing` (coordinator buys it), and each pass bumps `lineage.passes`; `not_at_service_point` or a refusal → retry after `FIT_RETRY_S = 10` s. Done → `lineage.fitted = True`.
- **Rovers fit themselves too** (`RoverController.fit_rover_loadout()`, start of `run()`): each `ROVER_LOADOUT` kind (nav, sonar, drill) with no module of that kind mounted at any tier goes through the same `fit_loadout()`; parts come from `lib/early_buyer.py` (§2m) or the operator, never from the commission queue.
- **`pioneer`** (the chassis kit) is in `storage.INVENTORY_ONLY_ITEM_IDS` so the rebalance sweep never moves it out of Inventory before the deploy.

### §2k-4 Fleet Decommissioning (`lib/fleet_decommission.py` coordinator + prepare steps in `vehicle_recall.py` / `drone_claims.py`)

Operator-triggered retirement of a Pioneer, Rover or drone, never automatic. A **retire** button in the FLEET card's detail pane (Ground tab: selected Pioneer or Rover; Drones tab: selected drone not in a chassis swap, §2k) calls `request_decommission()`: entry `requested` + recall flag on. Pressing it again (label **cancel**) or switching recall off drops the entry and clears the recall. Both cards narrow the battery bar by the button width (`RETIRE_BTN_W = 64`) so the row fits.

- **State**: one archive dict `fleet.decommission` = `{machine_id: {kind: "pioneer"|"rover"|"drone", state, attempts, reason}}`. States `requested` → `ready` → gone; `blocked` after `MAX_UNDEPLOY_ATTEMPTS = 5` refused undeploys (recall cleared, machine back to work; retire again to retry).
- **Machine side** (`_prepare_decommission()`, called from `handle_recall_if_active()` once home): Pioneer at base unloads cargo (`unload_cargo()`, HOME_BASE), charges to `DECOMMISSION_MIN_SOC = 0.98` (Portable Batteries sell for their charge %, 50% floor), then strips itself (`_strip_and_sell_parts()`: each portable `uninstall()`ed, then its holder/rack `unmount()`ed, every part sold right after it lands in Inventory; the Nav Module stays mounted for `get_position()`/`is_at_base()` and is sold by the coordinator with the kit — `undeploy()` refuses with `inventory_full` when Inventory lacks a slot per returned part, e.g. 27 for a fully fitted Pioneer), then marks `ready`. A Rover does the same but skips the charge (integrated battery, no sale value). `shop.sell()` takes the lowest-indexed matching slot, so a spare of the same item may be the unit sold. Drone docked at its recall Depot unloads into it (`unload_cargo_at_depot()`), then marks `ready`. Cargo left aboard (storage full) → retried next cycle.
- **Coordinator** (`FleetDecommissionCoordinator.step()`, `builder_automation.py` every `FLEET_TICK_INTERVAL`): on `ready`, re-checks cargo 0 (drone: still docked at a Drone Depot; else back to `requested`), snapshots a vehicle's remaining parts (chassis kit from `VEHICLE_KIT_IDS` + the Nav Module + anything else the strip left), stops the script, `computer.undeploy()` (hardware → Inventory). Refused → script restarted, back to `requested`; `inventory_full` (`RETRY_STATUSES`) is not counted toward `MAX_UNDEPLOY_ATTEMPTS`.
- **After undeploy**: Pioneer/Rover parts sold at the Shop, each capped at its Inventory gain from the undeploy (spare stock of the same item is never sold). Drone chassis and modules stay in Inventory (not sellable; reused by commission/upgrade). Then the machine's archive state is dropped: `vehicle.recall`/`drone.recall`, `vehicle.mission`/`drone.mission`, `fleet.status`, `logistics.pickups`, `mining.reserved_yield`, `fabricator.orders[id]`; drone: `drone.home_depots` pin, Depot stage, `fleet.upgrade` `drones`/`lineage` entries; Pioneer: sport nav request, `fleet.commission` lineage. An entry whose machine vanished gets the same cleanup.
- `fleet_upgrade.py` never picks a drone with a decommission entry for a chassis swap.
