# Drones (§2h, §2j, §2j-1)

Part of [`AI_CHEATSHEET.md`](../AI_CHEATSHEET.md). Drone energy, roles (scout, miner, hauler), depots and stations, aftermath collector. Surface vehicles in [`vehicles_drones.md`](vehicles_drones.md); fleet lifecycle in [`fleet_lifecycle.md`](fleet_lifecycle.md).

### §2h Drone Energy Budgeting Detail (`lib/drone_energy.py` `DroneEnergyMixin`; charging trips in `lib/drone_charging.py` `DroneChargingMixin`)

`DroneController` base (`lib/drone.py`) with scout (`lib/drone_scout.py`), miner (`lib/drone_mining.py`) and hauler (`lib/drone_hauler.py`, §2j) roles, plus `lib/drone_service.py` (charging/refuelling/rescue) and `lib/drone_depot.py` (cargo station). Drones = **fresh hierarchy, not `VehicleController` subclass** — no `.drive`/`.nav`, no terrain/stall handling, route-based `go_to(x, y)`/`go_to_station(name)`/`go_to_drill(name)` not blocking drive loop, simpler linear power/speed model — but `DroneController` follows same mixin-composition philosophy as `lib/vehicle.py`. Role from mounted modules: Bio Scanner → scout, Bio Extractor → miner, neither but Cargo Pods (`cargo.capacity() > 0`) → hauler.

#### Energy model and engines

- **Linear travel model**: full throttle = **5 Wh/h** at **300 m/h**; both scale with throttle (speed linear, burn quadratic), collapsing to `Wh/meter = DRONE_WH_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE
  (=5.0/300.0 ≈ 0.01667) × throttle` — structurally like Rover flat model, different constant, **no** per-drone/cargo/module term. Standalone `drone_wh_per_meter_at_throttle(throttle)` / `drone_rescue_wh_per_meter()` module-level functions let `lib/drone_service.py` reuse same rate cross-script. `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()` solves safe-throttle bound **directly linear** (`t <= available_for_leg / (distance × rate × SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER)`). Drone's own `range_remaining()` = ground truth; formula for planning only.
- **Engine auto-detect** (`detect_engine()`, at `__init__` and again at `run()` after a possible re-equip): own `DroneRef.engine` from `fleet.drones()`, else probe `oil_tank.capacity()`/`battery.capacity()` (the wrong powertrain raises `ReferenceError`; `hasattr` is useless since both attributes always exist), else `"electric"`. Also caches `is_plated()`. Every energy figure in the mixin is in the drone's own unit via `ENGINE_PROFILES`: **electric** `per_meter = 5/300` Wh/m, 300 m/h, reserve `MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH`; **heli** `per_meter = HELI_T_PER_METER_PER_THROTTLE = 5/900` t Oil/m (≈ 5.6 t/km at full throttle), 900 m/h, reserve `HELI_MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_T = 5.0` t. Shield Plating × `PLATING_BURN_MULTIPLIER = 1.5`. `get_battery()` keeps its name but reads the oil tank on a heli; `energy_unit()` gives `"Wh"`/`"t"` for logs; telemetry adds `engine`/`unit`. Heli drones skip **dry** drone_service stations (`heli_capable_services()`, shared with `lib/drone_service.py`): usable = `oil_in.level() >= SERVICE_MIN_OIL_T = 10.0` t (buffer max 100 t) **or** `flow_rate() > 0` (refilling now); none usable → wired-but-dry stations ("wired" via `connections()`, which also sees a link the pump declared, unlike `connected_to()`); none wired → all. Applies to refuel, idle parking, return floors and hauler fuel budgets. `flight_timeout_ticks()` uses the engine's speed; `fly_to_station()`/`fly_to_drill()` default timeout scales with distance when coords are known. `STRANDED_STATUSES` includes `stalled_no_oil`. **Route-call burn** (confirmed live on heli): hovering with no route costs nothing, but every `go_to()`/`go_to_station()`/`go_to_drill()` call costs a small minimal burn, even for a 0 m leg. So never re-issue a route needlessly: `fly_to()` returns early when already within precision, `fly_to_station()`/`fly_to_drill()` return early when `current_station()`/`current_drill()` already equals the target. The per-call amount isn't measured yet, so budgets don't model it (the safety margin and reserve cover it).
- **`MIN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_WH = 4.0`** (vs. `VehicleEnergyMixin`'s `8.0`) — electric drone batteries much smaller. `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER = 1.05` unchanged.
- **`LAUNCH_MIN_SOC = 0.80`** — launch hysteresis (`hold_for_launch_charge()`): before picking a new mission (scout and miner loops, after cargo/recall handling; the scout only while docked at a service, so in the field it chains POIs on its round-trip budget instead of flying home at 79%), a drone below 80% charge docks at its drone_service and stays until ≥ 80%, and above that while `status()` is `"charging"`/`"refueling"` (the station's job owns the drone until its own target level; route calls answer `"busy"`). Floor only: a target needing more still falls to the existing "none reachable → top up to 98%" branch, so far sites aren't lost. Needed because depot/service share coords, so the comfortable-return trigger is ~0 right after unloading. Reserve stays 4 Wh so the round-trip budget doesn't shrink max range.
#### Home depot and discovery

- **Pinned home** (`resolve_home_depot()` / `get_home_depot()` / `get_home_service()`; the logic is module functions in `lib/drone_depots.py`, `DroneEnergyMixin` keeps one-line wrappers): each drone pins a home, either an **outpost pool** (any Drone Depot in that outpost) or one **hardwired** depot — priority (1) `HOME_DEPOT` script variable in `drone.py` entrypoint (outpost id = pool; depot id/display name = hardwired — mirrors pioneer's `HOME_BASE`), (2) archived pin in shared dict `drone.home_depots` (same matching), (3) first run: pool of nearest depot's outpost. Pin is persisted, so a restart mid-field doesn't re-home to whichever depot is nearest there. With a pool, `get_home_depot()` re-picks per call via `_pick_free_depot()`: already-docked depot, else free bay first (live `get_component(depot_id).bay_count()/bays_occupied()`), then `prefer_id` (the depot already queued at — avoids churn when all full), then free cargo slots, then nearest. `fly_to_station()` returns early on `waiting_bay`, and `return_and_unload()` (`lib/drone_cargo.py`) then re-picks once and transfers to a free sibling (same coords, no flight cost), so drone A never waits on depot A while depot B is empty. Unload (`return_and_unload()`) and recall use the home depot; if it's removed, `get_home_depot()` re-homes via `DroneController.resolve_home()` (which also refreshes `home_outpost`/`home_biome`). **Home service** = drone_service in the home depot's outpost (else nearest to home depot); used by `calculate_trip_energy()` (return leg), `energy_needed_to_return_comfortably()`, and `return_to_service_for_charge()` (falls back to nearest service when home is out of reach on current battery). **Nearest** service still used for hard survival floor `return_floor_wh()` (in-flight abort) and `max_safe_throttle_for_leg()`. `discover_drone_buildings()` (memoized per script for `DISCOVERY_TTL_TICKS = 20`, as is `charging_stations.all_stations()` (wrapped by `VehicleEnergyMixin.get_all_charging_stations()`) with `STATION_DISCOVERY_TTL_TICKS = 20`; entries shared, read-only) returns `{"id", "name", "coords", "outpost", "outpost_id"}` dicts — `"outpost"` field is how `resolve_home()` sets `self.home_outpost`/`self.home_biome`, since **drone has no `.outpost` property** (confirmed against `docs/models/vehicles_and_modules.md`): home depot's outpost first, fallback nearest drone_service's outpost, then network home outpost. `return_to_service_for_charge()` checks "already there" by `current_station() == service id`, not coords (depot and service often share coords).
- **Depot sizes = three typeIds**: `item_tiers.DEPOT_TYPE_TIERS = ["drone_station", "drone_station_medium", "drone_station_large"]`, paired by index with `DEPOT_KIT_TIERS` (`DEPOT_KIT_FOR_TYPE`) (`outpost.buildings()` matches one exact type, so discovery loops over all three). Script-slot/instance prefixes differ again: `drone_station_N` / `drone_station_med_N` / `drone_station_lrg_N` (decompiled simworker machine catalog; large confirmed live) — `scripts/4_controlpanel/drone/drone_station_med.py`/`drone_station_lrg.py` are thin copies of `drone_station.py` so scripts_sync matches them. Every Depot lookup (`drone_energy`, `logistics_requests.local_depots()`, `site_supply`, `production_source`, `fleet_decommission`, `ArchiveCleaner.MACHINE_STATUS_KEYS`, autoplay roles) imports that list. `discover_drone_depots()` hides Depots a fleet upgrade is retiring (§2k).
- **`DRONE_DEPOT_TYPE_ID = "drone_station"`**, not `"drone_depot"` — in-game building `typeId` follows item/error-code naming (`drone_station_kit`, ship_computer.md's `"missing_drone_station"`/`"drone_station_full"`), not doc-page slug or display name "Drone Depot". A wrong typeId makes `outpost.buildings()` silently match nothing. See `docs/components/drone_depot.md` scripting note.
#### Biosite mining

- **No "biosphere region"** — per-outpost + per-biome: sample only locally processable (Essence Liquifier) at outpost where dropped off, and only if native to that outpost's biome (`nocturna.life_form_biome(item_id) == outpost.biome`). Miner drone filters `journal.biomass_coords()` candidates to samples native to home outpost biome (`is_home_biome_sample()`). Biosite whose tile has **mixed** biome sample set skipped entirely (`PortableBioExtractor.extract()` takes no species arg, can't pull only home-biome sample). Cross-outpost ferrying of foreign samples = deferred TODO.md Phase 4 item.
- **Exclusive biosite claims, not shared yield-debit reservations** — biosite extraction exclusive-WITH-COOLDOWN (`journal.is_ready(x, y)`/`next_ready_at(x, y)`, `BioExtractionResult.status == "cooling"` only after full depletion), fundamentally different from mineral mining's shareable sites (§2b). `lib/drone_claims.py`'s `claim_biosite()`/`refresh_biosite_claim()`/`release_biosite_claim()` reuse `vehicle_claims.py` claim/heartbeat/staleness shape (`fleet_claims_common.CLAIM_STALE_TICKS`) but own archive key (`biosite.claims`) — **do not** route biosite selection through `mining_reservations.py`. Miner target order: (1) `journal.is_ready(x, y)` cooldown gate (read-only, before any claim); candidates sorted by **request score** first (§2i: per home-biome sample still on the site whose life form has a network deficit in `logistics_requests.network_deficits()`, `RARITY_REQUEST_WEIGHT = {"common": 1, "uncommon": 2, "rare": 4}`; 0 everywhere without requests), then **partially-drained** (`0 < remaining_tons < tons` — cooldown only starts once a site is empty, so finishing a half-drained site beats opening a fresh one), then nearest; (2) exclusive claim attempt; (3) scout's separate bounded "confirmed-empty POI" cache (`SCOUTED_EMPTY_POI_KEY`, coord → tick scanned, capped at `SCOUTED_EMPTY_POI_MAX_ENTRIES = 2000`), kept as separate small archive-key wrapper from `biosite.claims`.
- **Miner drone biosite order** (`DroneMiningMixin.biosite_candidates()`): candidates sorted by `(-band, -request_score, not partial, -buffer_need, distance)`. `band` = the highest band (`logistics_requests.network_band_deficits()`) in which a life form the site holds is still requested by some outpost, `request_score` = `RARITY_REQUEST_WEIGHT` summed over those forms, then partly drained sites (so their cooldown can start), then the home outpost's buffer need (after biomass completion), then distance. The fine prio plays no part; `select_biosite_target()` still skips candidates the drone cannot afford.
- **Resumability**: `drone.mission[<name>]` persists in-progress target (mirrors `vehicle.mission[<name>]`). `DroneClaimsMixin.begin_mission()`/`end_mission()` are the only writers of `DroneController.mission` (`lib/mission.py`); `current_target`/`current_target_key` are read-only views; `release_biosite_claim()` of the current key (or all) ends the mission. Drone `go_to()` fire-and-forget, **cancelled by script restart** (unlike rover's persistent `drive_to()`) — on resume, `BiositeJob.execute()` (`lib/job_biosite.py`) re-validates the position and **re-issues** `go_to()`. `extract()`/`scan()` survive restart transparently — only flight leg needs re-issue. While a pre-restart `extract()` still runs, every `go_to*()` is rejected `"busy"` (logged at debug, not warn). `adopt_interrupted_extraction()` (run once from `BiositeJob.prepare()`) uses `detect_role()`'s extract probe (`role_probe_status`): `"busy"`/`"ok"` means hovering mid-harvest, so it claims the current tile as the mission even without a saved one, and the resume path finishes it. `return_and_unload()` returns False on failure and the job sleeps `poll_interval` before retrying; a full home Depot sets `host.depot_full` (`DepotFull`: retry tick, count, depot) and after `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS = 3` the job delivers to another Depot outpost that requests the cargo (`BiositeJob._deliver_elsewhere()`).
- **Idle-to-recharge fallback**: `energy_needed_to_return_comfortably()` alone only covers "in danger where parked" case. Drone can be above that floor (safe to sit) yet below what any candidate's `calculate_trip_energy()` round trip needs (`IDLE_OUT_OF_RANGE` tick, not low-battery). `DroneJob.top_up_if_unreachable()` (`lib/drone_job.py`, used by every drone job's `pick()`) calls `DroneChargingMixin.return_to_service_for_charge()` (shared, dock-if-not-already-there) when the cycle finds candidates but none reachable AND charge below 98%.
#### Docking, bays and recall

- **Bay-full queueing** (Drone Depot only): `go_to_station()` itself queues drone in `"waiting_bay"` when Depot full (game-engine behavior, drone.md) — no script-side reservation needed for wait. `fly_to_station()` (`drone_navigation.py`) polls `current_station()` (documented authoritative arrival check) up to `timeout_ticks` (default scales with distance via `flight_timeout_ticks()` when target coords are known, min 1500 ≈ 150 real seconds); caller's raw-`fly_to()` fallback on timeout does NOT enter bay queue (only `go_to_station()` does), so self-healing only via outer loop's next retry, not real dock. `return_and_unload()` (`drone_cargo.py`) skips that fallback while `status() == "waiting_bay"` (publishes `WAITING_DEPOT_BAY`, retries) — fallback would "arrive" instantly at same coords and unload undocked into nothing. **Depot slot-full** (unload `target_full`, distinct from bay-full): `unload_and_leave()` leaves berth and sets a backoff of `DroneCargoMixin.DEPOT_FULL_RETRY_TICKS = 300` (~30 s) in `host.depot_full`; `wait_for_depot_space()` hovers off the berth (`hover_wait()`, free) reporting `WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE` with the Depot id as target (lets the Depot flush surplus), flying to a drone_service only below `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC`. After `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS = 3` full-Depot failures in a row, `BiositeJob._deliver_elsewhere()` (`job_biosite.py`, `DEPOT_FULL_STALL_ATTEMPTS` there) takes cargo another Depot outpost requests (`outpost_deficits(live=False)`) there once, if `calculate_trip_energy()` allows; else it keeps waiting. A roleless (bare/unequipped) drone deliberately **stays docked** (needs berth to be equipped), so it blocks a 1-bay Depot until equipped.
- **Stranded vs no route** (`drone_navigation.py`): `STRANDED_STATUSES` (`stalled_no_battery`, `stalled_no_oil`, `scrambled`) wait for a `drone_service` rescue. `NO_ROUTE_STATUS` (`stalled_no_route`: the game dropped the flight, target station gone or off the map) keeps the drone's energy, so `fly_to()`, `fly_to_station()` and `fly_to_drill()` end their wait at once and the caller picks a new target.
- **`hover_wait(state, target)`** (`drone_navigation.py`): `leave_station()` + telemetry — the standard "nothing to do here" wait: free the bay, hover in place (hovering burns nothing).
- **Idle frees the Depot bay** (`DroneJob.idle()` → `release_depot_bay()`, every drone role): docked at a Drone Depot (not a drone_service, which charges) with empty cargo → `leave_station()` and hover, so a Depot bay never sits on an idle drone (another drone's unload, a FLEET Commission deploy, §2k-2). Cargo aboard keeps the bay.
- **`leave_station()`** (`drone_navigation.py`): releases current berth via `undock()` — keeps exact position/cargo/modules, costs no flight energy, unlike re-issuing `fly_to()` to same coords. `unload_and_leave()` (`drone_cargo.py`) calls it right after every Depot unload attempt (success or full), so miner frees bay for waiting peer instead of squatting until next mission moves it. No-op on `"not_docked"`/`"busy"` (latter protects active Drone Service Station charge/rescue job, which must keep control — never call on charging drone).
- **Recall** (`lib/drone_claims.py`): mirrors `vehicle_claims.py`'s `RECALL_KEY` shape exactly, own key `drone.recall` `{drone_name: True}`. `is_drone_recalled()`/`set_drone_recalled()` module-level read/write, toggled via the FLEET card's Drones-tab recall switch (`vehicles_panel.py`). Unlike ground-vehicle recall (returns to charging-station "base"), drone recall targets its home **Drone Depot specifically, never drone_service** — `couple()`/`uncouple()` (module re-equip) both require docking at operational Depot (drone.md), and recall's point is letting operator swap modules. `handle_recall_if_active()` abandons biosite claim/mission, flies to Depot, and — deliberately opposite of `leave_station()` — stays docked rather than releasing berth, since re-equip needs berth held. Checked by `run_job_loop()` right after `DroneJob.hold()` (the stranded check) (stranded drone needs `drone_service` rescue regardless of recall — can't self-navigate) but before mission-resume/target-selection, so active mission abandoned promptly.
#### Station controllers

- **`lib/drone_service.py`** structurally mirrors `lib/charging.py`: docked charge queue, fleet-wide stranded/scrambled detection via `fleet.drones()` (stranded predicate also includes `"scrambled"`), nearest-station coordination (`is_nearest_station_to()`). No return-home command to a low unit (either station type): moving another machine is a remote write the game blocks (`docs/AI_CHEATSHEET.md`), and a unit whose script runs budgets its own way back; a unit below its rescue target only keeps the station busy (watching). Engine-aware: docked electric → `charge()`, docked heli → `refuel()` (engine probed by `battery.percent()` raising); `no_oil` warns once with a hint whether `oil_in` is wired. For a heli, only oil-holding stations (`station_refs_for()`) compete in `is_nearest_station_to()` and set the return floor, so a dry station never rescues a heli into an empty refuel queue. Rescue floors use `fuel_of(drone_ref)` (`battery_*` or `oil_*` DroneRef fields) and `drone_rescue_energy_per_meter(engine)` (assumes plating, conservative); heli reserves `RETURN_EMERGENCY_RESERVE_T = RESCUE_EXTRA_RESERVE_T = 2.5` t. Heli rescue carries the drone home to the refuel queue (game behaviour). **Oil intake** (`ensure_oil_feed()`, every `step()`): once `research_heli_drones` is done, a `fluid_routing.FluidInputRouter` keeps `oil_in` on a reachable oil tank, else an Oil Pump (own outpost first, same routing as the Oil Generator); starved = buffer below `SERVICE_MIN_OIL_T` with no inflow. The station does not park while the router is `waiting` on a blacklisted source.
- **`lib/drone_depot.py`** mostly passive — cargo moves via drone's own `cargo.load()`/`cargo.unload()` while docked. Ports **don't** drain passively: declared link moves nothing until someone calls `take()`/`send()`, and Liquifier's own controller does the `take()` (§1h). Controller jobs: (1) one-time idempotent `self.output.connect(...)` wiring to outpost's Essence Liquifier, only when unambiguous (exactly one same-outpost Liquifier — else logged, left for manual wiring); (2) `stage_life_forms()`: every life form (`nocturna.life_form_biome()` not None) sent Depot → local Warehouse, capped at `LIFEFORM_BUFFER_SLOTS = 2` Warehouse stacks per form per outpost (slot capacity read from `slots()`, fallback `game_rules.WAREHOUSE_SLOT_UNITS`). Tops up a slot already holding the form first; opens an empty slot only while `storage.STAGING_FREE_SLOTS_KEEP = 1` more stay empty (room for ore/cargo). Full stack → rest stays in the Depot for the Liquifier (after biomass completion: for a hauler, or `flush_surplus()`). Keeps the 50-unit Depot free for drones, gives the Liquifier a buffer and pull haulers a `take()` source; re-declares the Liquifier link next step since `output` now points at the Warehouse; (3) `drain_freight()`: first `feed_local_smelters()` — each same-outpost Smelter with a fresh `smelter.wants` entry (§4 archive, `production_core.smelter_wants_at()`) gets that ore straight from the Depot, the most urgent want first (entry `prio`, then the larger `fill_to`), up to its published `fill_to` − its live input count; no entry (demand met, no recipe, a Supply Dock still shipping that ore raw, output blocked) = no push, so the input runs empty and the Smelter clears its recipe. A refused send (busy, other material) leaves the ore for storage. A transfer runs at its faster endpoint's speed (Depot 1/2/4 by size, ×2 with `depot_handling_unlock`; Warehouse 1), so this skips the slow Warehouse → Smelter leg and its Warehouse lock. Then every remaining **non-life-form** stack → local storage via `storage.drain_port_to_storage(include=, allow_partial=True)` (Warehouse, or Inventory at home; trickles into partial room). Makes the Depot usable as a hauler endpoint — its 50/100/200-unit stockpile is tiny next to a Large hauler's load, so the drone unloads in rounds while this drains; the controller polls every `FREIGHT_POLL_INTERVAL = 2.0` s while freight sits there, a stage request is open or any drone is docked (else 10 s); (4) periodic telemetry publish (`drone_depot.status`); (5) `fulfil_stage()` (first each step): hauler stage requests (`depot.stage`, `lib/depot_stage.py`) → `take_item(self.input, ...)` from local Warehouses/home Inventory up to requested − already in stockpile, within free units (`input.capacity()`) and material slots; a full stockpile (no free units, or a new material with no free slot) first triggers drain/stage/`flush_surplus(for_stage=True)`. Staged items are skipped by `drain_freight()` and `stage_life_forms()`; (6) `flush_surplus()`: only when the stockpile is full (units or slots) and a drone is docked or reported `WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE` for this Depot within `WAITING_DRONE_FRESH_TICKS = 6000`. Surplus = unstaged life form whose Warehouse stash can't grow (≥ `lifeform_buffer_cap()`, or `buffer_target()` finds no slot) and ≥ `retain_amount()` + `network_deficits()`. `input.flush()` discards the **whole** stockpile, so everything else is drained first and it flushes only when nothing but surplus is left (else warns). Logs what it destroyed. The only life-form destruction left: a last resort so drones can unload; miner drones skip sites whose forms are all at buffer (§1h-1), so it rarely fires.

### §2j Floating Drone Hauler (`lib/job_haul.py` `HaulJob` + `lib/drone_hauler.py` `DroneHaulerMixin` legs + `lib/drone_haul_plan.py` module functions: drills and Depot outposts → Depots)

Drone with Cargo Pods and no bio module. **No home Depot**: each cycle picks the best job network-wide, refuels at whichever drone_service is nearest when needed, and hovers in place when idle (`hover_idle()`: stays if already docked at a service, flies to one below `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC` or after `HAUL_IDLE_CHARGE_TICKS` idle; never holds a Depot bay). Hard limits (drone.md / `DroneCargo`): `cargo.load()` only at a docked Drone Depot, a field Mining Drill or a Lead Cask; `cargo.unload()` only into a docked Depot (or Lead Cask) — **no direct Warehouse/Inventory access**. One material per Cargo Pod (100/250/500); Large chassis = 1 thruster + 5 modules. Drill pickups need no staging; outpost pickups go through Depot-side staging (`depot.stage`). Planning (Demand, Sources, Plan, stall memory below) lives in `drone_haul_plan.py`; it reads game state but never moves the drone or writes the archive. The legs (dock, load at a drill or staged Depot, deliver, hover idle) are `DroneHaulerMixin` abilities in `drone_hauler.py`; the role (`run_job_loop(HaulJob(drone))`: cargo aboard first, launch/recharge floors, urgent Storm Glass, plan, claim the route, fly the legs, deliver) is `HaulJob` in `job_haul.py`. The delivery in progress is the drone's `Mission` of kind `"haul"` (target `{"dest": outpost_id}`, no claim key; `load_mission()` restores it without a claim check).

- **Demand** (`haul_destinations()`): every outpost with a Depot, home included → `logistics_requests.outpost_tier_deficits(live=True)` (net of in-flight pickups) as dest `"tiers"` — same demand the Pioneer pull hauler (§2i) reads, so they share it. `FAIR_BANDS` capped per destination by `fair_share_tiers()` over the other sources (§2i). Home with requests but no Depot → one warning per run.
- **Sources**: `drill_sources()` — advertised drills (`drill.status`) standing on a surveyed site (`drill_sites.drill_positions()`), net of `reserved_from()`, unlocated drills skipped (warned once); `outpost_sources()` — every Depot outpost with free stock (`outpost_free_tiers(loader=LOADER_DRONE)`: Warehouses + Depot stockpiles + home Inventory, own request's level at the asking band, or its `keep`, kept back), never the destination itself; `shop_sources()` — per non-home destination with buyable deficits (`buyable_deficits()`, remote Bio Lab reagents §2g) one Shop source at home's Depots (id `shop:<dest>`, `"for_dest"`; `sources_for()` offers it to that destination only), listed last so free stock wins a tie; none without a Shop or a home Depot. At the stop `load_at_depot()` docks at the home Depot and, each round, buys what is neither aboard nor bought yet (`buy_at_shop()`: one Inventory stack at a time within `inventory_room()`, cash consumer `drone_reagents:<drone>`, operating; a denial, refused purchase or full Inventory ends that item's buy) and stages only the bought units not yet aboard, so free home stock stays with its own reservations; an item with nothing bought and nothing left to load is dropped. Bought units the drone doesn't load stay in home Inventory as free stock.
- **Plan** (`plan_haul_job()`): per destination a nearest-neighbour chain over drills and outposts (bands in `TIERS` order via `plan_take()`, then largest deficits; per-item room from a pod model: `pod_sizes()` reads `drone.modules()` in slot order, `CARGO_POD_UNITS` = 100/250/500, halved when plated; `load_pods()` fills the item's own pods, then empty ones, one material per pod, so a stop whose materials find no free or matching pod is never planned; `source_useful(..., room)` skips items without room), up to `HAUL_MAX_STOPS_PER_TRIP = 3`, chained stops only if direct ≤ `HAUL_CHAIN_MAX_DETOUR_RATIO = 0.75` × via-destination. Skipped below `logistics_requests.min_load_units()` (`min(HAUL_MIN_LOAD_UNITS = 10, reachable)`, shared with ground haulers) (deficit units the sources hold at all), or below 1 when the route carries `BLOCKER` units (`logistics_requests.blocker_units()`: nothing more arrives by waiting). Ranked by `logistics_requests.haul_rank()` (a 9-tuple, §2i) over (route m + `trip_overhead_m()`: `HAUL_TRIP_OVERHEAD_M = 300` × engine speed / electric speed, so 900 m for a heli, the overhead standing for dock and load time): per band the units serving it or a higher band per meter, `BLOCKER` first, compared lexicographically (`route_tier_units()`); best wins, and the next `HAUL_CLAIM_ALTERNATES = 4` ride along as `plan["alternates"]`. **Heli yield** (electric haulers only, `yield_to_helis()`): while a heli hauler is idle (`drone.heli_free` entry no older than `HELI_FREE_STALE_TICKS = 300`), a candidate carrying `BLOCKER` units or spanning ≥ `HELI_YIELD_MIN_M = 1500` m is dropped when the nearest idle heli's ETA (its flight to the first stop + the rest of the route, 900 m/h) is ≤ `HELI_YIELD_ETA_RATIO = 0.75` × the electric's (300 m/h); the electric takes its next-best job or idles, never waits. Per destination the yield lasts ≤ `HELI_YIELD_MAX_TICKS = 600`, then the electric takes it (`HaulMemory.heli_yields`, forgotten after `HELI_YIELD_FORGET_TICKS = 6000`), so a heli that never picks it (out of range, a better job) can't starve it. Each plan, a heli `HaulJob` sets its entry when it found no job and drops it when it found one (`_announce_free()`). One candidate per destination × first source, except a first source an earlier route to the same destination already visits (counted as covered); `shortest_order()` puts each route's stops (≤ 3, brute force, loads kept) in the shortest start → stops → destination order. One debug line per plan: routes atomic / stepped (over-cap destinations with sources/items) / covered. **Fuel budget** (`route_fuel()`): route legs at cruise throttle + nearest-service-from-destination at the speedmode floor, × `SAFETY_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER` + `emergency_reserve()` — "back" is any service, not a home. Job over a full tank → dropped; over what's aboard → `refuel()` at nearest service first, then re-plan. **Planning cost**: one `logistics_requests.PlanReads` per plan reads active requests, `logistics.pickups` (the `pickups_snapshot()` that `claim_pickups()` later trims against; `in_flight()`/`reserved_from()` for every id from one pass) and each Depot outpost's stock (`outpost_stock()` over every requested item, Depots included) once, shared by `outpost_tier_deficits()`, `outpost_tier_free()` and `fair_share_tiers()`, so an outpost that is both destination and source is read once; the outpost list and Depots (`depot_layout()`) are read once too. The Pioneer's `pull_plan.pull_sources()` uses one for requests and reservations. The pod sizes and the fuel rates (`fuel_rates()`) are read once per plan, `reachable` once per destination; each destination plans over `sources_for()`: the sources other than itself, each cut to the items it still misses (sources left empty dropped; same routes, but a network-wide item list on one source kept every candidate out of an atomic call: live 6-outpost network 278k → 105k opcodes per plan); each destination's distances (start, destination, every source; `distance_table()`, one atomic call up to `DISTANCE_TABLE_ATOMIC_MAX = 16` sources) and its tail to the nearest service are built once and shared by its candidates; each candidate (route + units, units per tier, meters, fuel) is one pure `haul_candidate()` call, run as a `lib/atomic.py` call (at most one tick each) when `drone_haul_plan.haul_atomic_ok(dest, dest_sources)`: `HAUL_COST_BASE = 5900` + `HAUL_COST_PER_SOURCE = 80` × sources + `HAUL_COST_PER_ITEM_TIER = 80` × items of the `HAUL_MAX_STOPS_PER_TRIP` sources holding the most × demand tiers the destination asks for ≤ `ROUTE_ATOMIC_MAX_COST` (only a stop's items are ranked, taken and loaded; fitted with 1/0.85 margin over ~16k random and adversarial routes, 1-12 sources, 1-14 items each, 1-9 tiers, 1-5 pods; candidates under the gate measured ≤ ~7,900 opcodes; the planners walk only the tiers with demand, `route_left()`; `pod_space()` tracks pod room incrementally). Per destination, `_dest_prep()` (`sources_for()`, cost terms, service tail, `reachable_units()`) is one atomic call when `DEST_PREP_COST_BASE = 1500` + `DEST_PREP_COST_PER_SOURCE = 60` × sources + `DEST_PREP_COST_PER_ITEM = 30` × items fits. `_cap_fair_tiers()` computes rival deficits once per plan (`logistics_requests.fair_rival_bands()`, `FAIR_RIVAL_CHUNK = 10` entries per atomic call) and caps each destination with a fair band in one atomic call when `FAIR_COST_BASE = 1000` + `FAIR_COST_PER_DEFICIT_ITEM = 160` × deficit items + `FAIR_COST_PER_SOURCE_ITEM = 24` × source items per fair band fits. `outpost_sources()` computes free stock only at the bands some destination misses (`outpost_tier_free(tiers=)`). The Pioneer pull planner gates on `logistics_requests.route_atomic_ok(dest_sources)`: estimated `ROUTE_COST_BASE = 4000` + `ROUTE_COST_PER_SOURCE = 240` × sources + `ROUTE_COST_PER_ITEM = 350` × items summed over all sources ≤ `ROUTE_ATOMIC_MAX_COST = 10000` CPython opcodes (each stop's `source_useful()` pass walks every item of every source left) (fitted upper bound on random home-shaped cases, 1-12 sources, 1-14 items each, 1-5 pods; candidates under the gate measured ≤ ~9,700 opcodes, a game step being 1.6-3 opcodes, so well under the 10,000-step callback cap). The numbers were fitted with two-band loops; the planners (`plan_take()`, `route_left()`, `route_tier_units()`) now loop all nine bands per item, so the estimate is optimistic for a worst case with every band present until it is refitted ([TODO.md](../../TODO.md), Priority ladder follow-ups). Larger networks plan directly. Usefulness per source: `logistics_requests.source_useful()` (same result as any `plan_take()` > 0). Shared with the Pioneer pull hauler.
- **Tier snapshot** (`lib/logistics_snapshot.py`, archive `logistics.tier_snapshot`, archive_ipc.md §4): every outpost's `outpost_tier_deficits(live=True)`, `outpost_tier_free(loader=LOADER_DRONE)` and its in-flight / reserved units, built by the orchestrator and read by `TierView` (`deficits()`, `free()`, `rivals()` at `FAIR_BANDS`) instead of the live reads. A reader corrects an entry for pickups gained since the build: a destination loses the new in-flight units from its top band down, a source loses the new reserved units from every band; units that left the in-flight set stay counted. Age = reader tick - record tick: FRESH ≤ `LOGISTICS_FRESH_TICKS = 3000`, STALE ≤ `LOGISTICS_MAX_STALE_TICKS = REQUEST_STALE_TICKS` (6000), else MISSING (`view()` is None unless FRESH, or STALE with `allow_stale`). Build cadence `LOGISTICS_SNAPSHOT_TICKS = 600` (round start to round start), one publisher step `LOGISTICS_SLICE_TICKS = 100` ticks. Publisher: `SnapshotPublisher` / `PUBLISHER`, stepped by `orchestrator_automation.publish_logistics_if_due()` before the demand record; a round = one `PlanReads` at its start, one `build_entry()` task per `outpost_network.outposts()` entry (items = every item in `reads.requests`), at least one task per step, published stamped with the round start tick; a task error is logged (`swallowed()`) and leaves that outpost out. - **Outpost pickup**: at claim, each outpost leg picks a Depot (`stage_depot_for()`: most of the planned items already in its stockpile, else a free one; `HaulJob.leg_depots`) and writes `depot_stage.request_stage()` so the Depot stages during the flight. `load_at_depot()` docks (`dock_at_depot()`, same bay wait as delivery), `cargo.load()` in rounds every `DEPOT_UNLOAD_RETRY_S`, shrinking the stage request to what is still missing after each round; stops when done, after `DOCK_IDLE_MAX_S = 60` s without progress or `DEPOT_LOAD_TIMEOUT_S = 120` s overall. Reservations corrected to what loaded, stage cleared, bay left.
- **Coordination**: planned units reserved per source (drill or outpost) in `logistics.pickups` (dest + source) by `claim_pickups()`, trimmed by what others reserved since planning; a claim left under its minimum tries the alternates (`HaulJob._claim_alternate()`: one claim each, only those the fuel aboard covers) before replanning next cycle; corrected to loaded amounts, released after full delivery. Mission `drone.mission[<name>] = {"kind": "haul", "dest", "target_key": None}` (no `target_key` → biosite `load_mission()` ignores it).
- **Deliver** (`deliver()`): free Depot of the destination (`_pick_free_depot()`, waits ≤ `DEPOT_BAY_WAIT_S = 60` s in `waiting_bay`), unload rounds every `DEPOT_UNLOAD_RETRY_S = 2` s while the Depot drains (§2h), give up after `DOCK_IDLE_MAX_S = 60` s without progress or `DEPOT_UNLOAD_TIMEOUT_S = 300` s overall → `hover_wait("WAITING_DEPOT_SPACE", depot)` (lets the Depot flush surplus), keep cargo/mission/reservations, retry next cycle. Cargo aboard at step start (restart, failed delivery) → `HaulJob.prepare_cargo()` / `pick_delivery_outpost()`: the haul mission's dest, else Depot outpost with most demand for the cargo, else home, else nearest Depot (destinations on stall cooldown skipped); reservations re-asserted from what's physically aboard.
- **Stalls** (in memory per drone, reset by a restart): a destination or source outpost failing (no dock, nothing loaded, Depot took no more) `STALL_MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3` times in a row is skipped for `STALL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. A destination going on cooldown with cargo aboard clears the saved mission, so the next cycle requeues the cargo to the next-best destination.
- **Refuel** (`DroneChargingMixin.refuel()`, `lib/drone_charging.py`): fly to nearest (oil-fed, for heli) service; its station script queues the charge/refuel; wait until ≥ `REFUEL_FULL_LEVEL = 0.98`, or ≥ needed once the service job ended, ≤ `REFUEL_TIMEOUT_S = 900` s. Between jobs (no cargo aboard) below `HAUL_RECHARGE_SOC = 0.30` the hauler docks at the nearest service; once docked, launch floor `LAUNCH_MIN_SOC` holds it until topped up (30% → 80% hysteresis). Idle (no haul job) `HAUL_IDLE_CHARGE_TICKS = 1200` ticks in a row below `REFUEL_FULL_LEVEL` → `hover_idle()` docks at the nearest service too (`HaulJob.idle_since`, reset by a job or cargo aboard), so it waits there charged instead of hovering. Without the trigger, always-available short outpost jobs fit the per-job budget and the hauler chains down to the emergency reserve.

The glass stays aboard and goes out through the normal cargo-aboard delivery.

### §2j-1 Aftermath Collector (`lib/job_aftermath.py` `AftermathJob` + `lib/drone_weather.py` `DroneWeatherMixin`)

Collects storm aftermaths decoded into `weather.aftermaths` (§1m). Role `"aftermath"` = Cargo Pods + **Shield Plating** and no bio module (`detect_role()` checks plating before the hauler fallback). Plated drones take Raw Uranium, and Storm Glass only when no uranium site is claimable (`aftermath_passes()`: uranium pass first, glass pass second; plating costs 1.5× fuel and half the Cargo Pod capacity); unplated drones (haulers, §2j) take Storm Glass only, never uranium (+40 exposure per batch, scramble at 100).

- **Launch timing**: a site is a candidate once `now + travel_h >= ready_gh` (`launch_due()`; travel at `cruise_throttle`), and skipped when arrival + `EXPIRY_MARGIN_GH = 1.0` passes `expires_gh`. Order: soonest expiry, then distance. On site the drone hovers until `ready_gh + READY_MARGIN_GH = 0.1` (`WAITING_AFTERMATH`, polls every `WAIT_POLL_S = 10` s).
- **Exact coordinate**: `go_to()` snaps the drone to the exact target and `collect()` matches `x`/`y` exactly (simworker `zw()`), so `fly_to(..., precision=0.01)`.
- **Collect loop** (`_collect_at_site()`): `collect()` until cargo `space_for(item)` is 0, `limit` units came aboard, or `nothing_here`. `moving`/`busy` retried `SETTLE_RETRIES = 10` × 1 s. `nothing_here` after units came out = exhausted; with none, `NOTHING_HERE_LIMIT = 3` in a row (re-issuing `go_to()` if off-coordinate) = exhausted. Result written back into the site entry (`collected` += units, `exhausted`), so no drone flies there again.
- **Uranium gate**: `research_shielded_depot_ops` unlocked and unreserved Lead Cask room at the drone's home outpost (`cask_room()`: casks latched to `raw_uranium` or empty, capped at `uranium_want()` = `lead_cask.URANIUM_STOCK_TARGET = 100` + Raw Uranium Supply Dock orders at that outpost still owe − cask stock, minus other drones' `lead_cask.inbound` entries for that outpost), floored to whole `COLLECT_BATCH_UNITS = 5` batches (`batch_limit()`; one `collect()` moves up to 5, so less room launches nothing and no overshoot lands in the next empty cask). `cargo.unload()` at the home Depot puts hot cargo into one Lead Cask per call; `unload_cargo_at_depot()` repeats up to `UNLOAD_MAX_ROUNDS = 4` calls per item while each still moves something. `cask_missing` leaves cargo aboard like a full Depot.
- **Cask room reservation** (`lead_cask.reserve_inbound()`): after winning the site claim, the drone reserves its share atomically against the other carriers' entries; the grant, floored to whole batches (the entry narrowed to match), is the trip's `limit` (under one batch = reservation and claim released, next candidate). After the trip the entry is set to the units aboard (`set_inbound()`) and dropped once no Raw Uranium is aboard (`_sync_cask_reservation()`, also run before each target pick). Entries expire after `INBOUND_STALE_TICKS = 36000` and are pruned on the next write.
- **Intent**: after a trip the fleet card shows `hauling <item> from <event> to <home>` while cargo is aboard, then no job; a hauler's Storm Glass pickup clears it and its delivery sets its own.
- **Claims**: exclusive, in `biosite.claims` under `aftermath_<event_id>` (§2h claim helpers, same staleness). Released after each trip; a site needing several trips is claimed again by whichever drone is free. Mission `drone.mission[<name>] = {"kind": "aftermath", "target_key", "target"}`; after a reload the drone (or hauler) resumes the saved site first.
- **Job** (`run_job_loop(AftermathJob(drone))`): stranded (`DroneJob.hold()`) / recall / upgrade checks, then resume, unload cargo aboard (`drone_cargo.return_and_unload()`), charge, pick and claim (`begin_aftermath_mission()`), fly, wait, collect, release, unload. Idle: `IDLE_NO_TARGETS`, polls every `IDLE_POLL_S = 30` s. Stub-tested only.
