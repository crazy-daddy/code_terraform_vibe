# Pull Logistics, Reverse Hauler & Site Supply (§2i, §2i-1)

Part of [`AI_CHEATSHEET.md`](../AI_CHEATSHEET.md). Storage and mining stock targets live in [`storage_mining.md`](storage_mining.md).

### §2i Pull Logistics + Reverse Hauler (`lib/logistics_requests.py`, `lib/pull_plan.py`, `lib/job_pull.py` `PullJob`)

Generic "bring X to outpost Y" demand. Requester publishes wants. The outposts' top deficit bands also set how many Pioneers the hauler role wants (`fleet.role_demand`, fleet_lifecycle.md §2f-1). Supply ("free stock"): outposts are read live (`outpost_free_tiers()`, no per-outpost script), field Mining Drills advertise via `drill.status` (§1j). Served by the Pioneer reverse hauler (below) and the floating drone hauler (§2j).

- **Requests** `logistics.requests` (§4): an entry is `{"levels": ladder, "have", "by", "tick", "buy"?, "keep"?}`; `levels` = the request's priority ladder (§2a-p), the units the requester wants on hand and the band each slice of them is wanted at (a requester that needs 30 to keep working and would like 2000 on hand writes `[[NEED, 30], [STOCK, 2000]]`; `priority.from_levels(target, need)` builds that from a plain pair). `set_requests(outpost_id, requester, {item: (levels, have)})` or `(levels, have, keep)` replaces that requester's entries; entries older than `REQUEST_STALE_TICKS = 6000` ignored/pruned (dead requester can't pin stock; must exceed the slowest requester's publish gap — the field Harvester's care tours). Requesters publish through `publish_requests(outpost_id, requester, wants, tick, requests=None, buyable=False, skip_foreign=True, have_of=None)`: writes only when levels/keep/buy differ from that requester's published entries or they are `REPUBLISH_TICKS = REQUEST_STALE_TICKS // 2` (3000) old (`have` not compared, so it lags up to that); empty wants withdraws. `skip_foreign` leaves an item another requester owns at that outpost to it (one owner per (outpost, item), first writer wins). Requesters that take an item anyway (`skip_foreign=False`): site supply + eviction, home salt (`pump_salt`), Seed Maker/supply; all others yield. `keep` (third wants field, `request_keep()`) is compared too. `have_of(item_id)` fills `have` on a write only (Plant Terraformer: its stock read is a live walk). `outpost_deficits(outpost, live=True)` = `outpost_tier_deficits()` summed over the bands: ladder − local stock (`outpost_stock()`: Warehouses + Drone Depots + home Inventory + Crop Automator outputs for `forage`) − in-flight pickups. `network_band_deficits()` (`{band: {item: units}}`) and `network_deficits()` (flat) sum the published `have`-based deficits of every requesting outpost (cheap, for drones).
- **Bands in the planners** (`TIERS = priority.BANDS`, nine bands, highest first; the code calls a band a tier): `tier_level(entry, band)` = the ladder's units at that band or higher; `tier_deficits(entry, have, flying)` → `{band: units missing}` (`band_deficits()`: each band's level minus stock and in-flight, after the higher bands' share); `tier_keep(entry, band)` = what the outpost holds back from another outpost's demand at that band (its own level there, or its `keep` reserve when higher); `tier_free(entry, units, cap)` → `{band: units}` another outpost may take at each band. `outpost_tier_deficits()`/`outpost_tier_free()` → `{band: {item: units}}`; the route planners walk `TIERS` too. `outpost_deficits_tiered()` → `(need, stock)` adapter over `split_need_stock()` (bands at `NEED` or above vs the rest; miners): in-flight = pickups bound there + mining yield reserved for there (`mining.reserved_yield`). Planners serve bands in `TIERS` order everywhere: `haul_rank(tier_units, meters, overhead_m)` = a 9-tuple, per band the units serving it or a higher band, per (route m + overhead), `tier_units` from `route_tier_units(deficits, route)`, compared lexicographically by `rank_beats()`: `BLOCKER` throughput decides, then `MANUAL`'s, … , all units only break a tie, so any trip carrying a higher band beats a bigger load of lower ones and a band's fine prio plays no part (a trip is scored by what it delivers per metre). `blocker_units(tier_units)` = the route's units in the `BLOCKER` band: with any, the hauler's minimum load is waived. `FAIR_BANDS` are capped by `fair_share_tiers(dest, deficits, sources)` → `fair_tier_caps(dest, band, deficit, supply)` = supply split in proportion to every requesting outpost's deficit at that band (others from published `have`), rounding remainder to the largest; higher bands are served in full. Per-source split via `plan_take(source, item, left, cap)` → `{band: units}` (`left` = `route_left(deficits)`; each band only from what `source_tier(source, band)` frees after the higher bands' takes).
- **Pickups** `logistics.pickups` (§4): `reserve_pickup(..., source_id=)` per planned/loaded item and source (amount 0 drops the entry; `aboard=True` after a load marks the units as cargo, summed by `aboard_units()` for `SourceCache.network_stock()`; planned-only entries still sit at the source and don't count), `release_pickups(vehicle)` after unloading; stale after `PICKUP_STALE_TICKS = 36000`. A hauler plans only with empty cargo and takes its snapshot with `planning_snapshot(vehicle)`, which first releases the vehicle's own leftovers from an abandoned plan (script restart, a replan that dropped a leg). Otherwise they count as in flight and hide the deficit from every hauler until stale. Debits both ends: `in_flight(dest)` against the requester's deficit, `reserved_from(source)` against that source's free stock (so two haulers never plan the same units there).
- **Free stock** (`outpost_free_tiers()`): outpost = `stock_scan` `HELD` (Warehouses, Bins, Drone Depot stockpiles, + Inventory and Crop Automator Forage if home) − `reserved_from()`, capped by what the `loader` can load there: `LOADER_DRONE` (drone haulers) everything held, `LOADER_VEHICLE` (default: Pioneer haulers, site supply's "free elsewhere") `LOCAL` + each Depot's stockpile above `depot_holds()` = its hauler drone stage requests (`depot_stage.staged_for()`) + same-outpost Smelter wants (`smelter_wants_at()` `fill_to`, an upper bound). Other haulers' pickups count against those held-back Depot units first (a drone's stage request is its own pickup), so a staged claim is not deducted twice. Then minus what it keeps for itself at the asking band (`tier_free()`): its own request's ladder level at that band or higher (or its `keep` reserve when higher, `request_keep()`), so a request's `STOCK` slice is held back only from `STOCK`-band demand. A planner source carries these as `"tiers"` (`outpost_tier_free()`) and `"available"` = the most any band frees (`most_free()`); drills, pumps and the Shop carry `"available"` only (nothing held back, every band). So a home salt buffer of 2000 still hands everything above its need (30) to an outpost whose Terraformer is short. `outpost_free_tiers()` → `(free for NEED-band demand, free for STOCK-band demand)`; `outpost_free_stock()` = the `STOCK` one, `LOADER_VEHICLE`. Loading: a Pioneer takes from the stores first (`take_item()`), then the Depots (`take_from_depots(spare_holds=True)`, every Depot's status logged, nothing moved = warn). Drill = advertised `items` − `reserved_from()` (every band).
- **Reverse hauler**: standard Pioneer template, hauler role (no role module) → `PullJob` (`lib/job_pull.py`), the only Pioneer hauler mode (§2f); it floats: the base (`set_base()`) moves to each trip's destination, where it parks and delivers. Per cycle: cargo aboard → deliver to its pickups' destination first (`pickup_dest()`); else `pull_plan.pull_destinations(tick, reads)` = every network outpost with a live deficit (`pull_deficits_tiered(dest)` = request `{band: {item: units}}`, home included, no extra ore demand), grouped by top band (`top_band()`), highest group first, one shared `PlanReads`. Per destination of a group `pull_plan.plan_pull_route(host, dest, ...)` (sources: `route_sources()`; ranking: `best_pull_route(host, dest, ..., origin=host position)` → `(route, reachable, rank)`); the best `rank` at or above the minimum load wins, a group with none falls through to the next; `claim_pickups(dest=...)`, then `set_base(dest)`, mission target `"dest"`. Per destination: `FAIR_BANDS` capped by `fair_share_tiers()`, then every source (other outposts + advertised drills + salt pumps) whose free stock covers a deficit tried as first stop, then nearest-neighbour chain from there (`plan_pull_chain()`), bands in order then largest deficits (`tier_rank()`), up to `PULL_MAX_STOPS_PER_TRIP = 3` stops / cargo capacity (so several Water Pumps chain into one salt run); best `haul_rank()` over (round-trip m + `PULL_TRIP_OVERHEAD_M = 300`) wins (highest band's rate first), so a nearby source with small top-ups doesn't shadow far sources holding the real deficit. Stops after the first only chained if direct leg ≤ `PULL_CHAIN_MAX_DETOUR_RATIO = 0.75` × (prev → dest → next), so it never drives past the destination; skipped stops wait for the next trip. Pickup legs pass `drive_with_recharge(min_throttle=PULL_MIN_LEG_THROTTLE = 0.3)`: a leg a full battery could only drive below 30% throttle (no closer charging station) is refused instead of crawling at the 10% floor; a refused first stop drops out of planning for `PULL_UNVIABLE_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 18000` (`drop_unviable_sources()` over the job's `PullJob.unviable`). Each first stop's chain and its measures are one pure call (`pull_candidate()`, chain checks returned as one debug text), run as a `lib/atomic.py` call when `logistics_requests.route_atomic_ok()` (below the drone hauler's **Plan**, `docs/cheatsheet/vehicles_drones.md`). Trip skipped if planned < `logistics_requests.min_load_units()` (`min(HAUL_MIN_LOAD_UNITS = 10, reachable)`, shared with drones), reachable = deficit units some source actually holds (deficits nobody holds, or left to drones, don't hold back a small real delivery); 1 when the route carries `BLOCKER` units (`blocker_units()`). Legs via `drive_with_recharge()` (already refuses a leg that can't reach a charging station afterwards); recharge at stops that have a station; unreachable stop → head to the base (the destination) with what's aboard (its and later stops' pickup reservations dropped).
- **Outpost flow** (`lib/outpost_flow.py`, `logistics.outpost_flow` §4): `record(tick, sent=, received=)` adds units per outpost, old values decayed with half-life `FLOW_HALF_LIFE_TICKS = 36000`; `read(tick)` → `{outpost_id: (sent, received)}`. `sent`: units loaded per trip source (`PullJob.execute()`, drone `HaulJob` pickups); a drill or pump source counts for the nearest founded outpost, a Shop purchase for none. `received`: units unloaded at the destination (`PullJob._deliver_pull_cargo()`, `DroneHaulerMixin.deliver()`). Read by Pioneer hauler dispersal (vehicles_drones.md §2f).
- **Drone yield switch** (`logistics.drone_yield`, FLEET card `vehicles_panel.py` "leave to drones", default off): `pull_plan.pull_sources()` drops every source `drone_served_source(source, dest)` flags — only while the switch is on, the trip's destination has a Drone Depot (drones deliver only to Depots) and `drone_haulers_present()` (a drone `role == "hauler"` entry — one with `engine` — in `fleet.status` younger than `HAULER_FRESH_TICKS = 6000` and not off duty (`fleet_status.is_off_duty()`: recalled, upgrade hold, retiring); else every source stays with the ground hauler). Flagged: field Mining Drills, and outposts with their own Drone Depot. Water Pump salt never (drones can't load it). Each skip logged at `debug()`.
- **Shop source** (`pull_plan.shop_source()`, `VehicleCargoMixin._buy_and_take()`): only for a destination that is not home, and only for deficits whose request is flagged `buy` (`set_requests(buyable=True)`, read via `buyable_deficits()`; today only remote Bio Lab reagents, §2g). A virtual source at the home outpost's coords (id `SHOP_SOURCE_ID:<home id>` = `"shop:<outpost>"`, so haulers for different outposts never trim each other in `claim_pickups()`), "available" = the whole buyable deficit, appended after the real sources so free stock at home wins a tie. At the stop it buys one Inventory stack at a time and `take_item()`s it before the next; a cash manager denial (consumer `pioneer_reagents:<name>`, operating, §2l) or a refused purchase (credits, Shop stock) ends that item. Hauler drones get their own Shop source per destination (§2j, `drone_haul_plan.shop_sources()`, same id, so `claim_pickups()` trims a drone and a Pioneer buying for one outpost against each other).
- **Plays nice with other haulers**: planned units are reserved per source (`logistics.pickups`) and count as in flight against the destination's deficits, so a drone already bringing 800 ore → pull hauler sees 800 less demand, and vice versa. Demand-bounded: drills are only emptied as far as the destination's requests (smelting sites: `ore_stock_target()` buffer) reach.
- **Drill location** (`lib/drill_sites.py`): `drill_positions()` walks `journal.surveyed_sites("nocturna")`; each `MiningSite` with a `drill_id()` gives that drill the site's `x`/`y` (cached `DRILL_CACHE_TICKS = 3000`, like pumps). A drill on no surveyed site is skipped (warned once per hauler run). On arrival (`DRILL_ARRIVAL_PRECISION_M = 2.0`) `vehicle.input.connect(drill_id)`; refusal → warning, nothing loaded that stop. Loading via `take_from_drill()`; no recharge at drill stops.
- **Source retention**: `retain_amount(item, outpost_id)` = own request's ladder total (`request_target()`) if this outpost requests it, else, while any other outpost requests it at all (even when topped up — continuous consumers drain again soon), `max(LIFEFORM_STASH_CAP_T = 25, largest remote total)`, else 0. Used by Drone Depot staging (§2h) and Essence Liquifier feed (§1h). Pioneer haulers take from Warehouses first, then Depot stock above `depot_holds()` (§2i free stock); Depot buffer staging (§2h) puts life forms in Warehouses.
- **Water Pump salt** (`lib/pump_salt.py`): pumps are field structures on water wells, found via `journal.surveyed_sites("nocturna")` WaterWells with `has_pump()` → `pump_id()` + well `x`/`y` (cached `PUMP_CACHE_TICKS = 3000`). Salt read live from the pump's `output` (`PickupOutputSlot`). Added as `"kind": "pump"` sources only when `salt` is wanted, home pumps included; connect/take reuse `drill_sites.connect_to_drill()`/`take_from_drill()`, arrival precision `PUMP_ARRIVAL_PRECISION_M = 2.0`. Drones can't take it (`not_at_source`).
- **Drones**: request score in miner target order (§2h). Biosites are permanent (35 fixed, 7 per biome, 1–3 forms each, refill at same coordinate after rarity cooldown — docs/guide/biosphere_biomass_tier.md), so a requested rare form is found deterministically via `journal.biomass_coords()`.

### §2i-1 Factory Outpost Site Supply (`lib/site_supply.py`)

`publish_site_requests(tick)` runs once per storage tick in headless `orchestrator_automation.py`, one `logistics.requests` entry set per outpost under requester `SITE_SUPPLY_REQUESTER = "site_supply"`; pull haulers homed there serve it (§2i). Roles come from the buildings at the outpost, no setting.

- **Fab site** (≥ 1 Fabricator): gross = Σ local Fabricators' active recipe Smelter-output inputs × `crafts_remaining` − stockpile (`production.fab_site_gross_need()` → ladders at the recipe's prio, the highest level of its site target still short, `get_fabricator_active_recipe()` → `(recipe, crafts_remaining, prio)`). `D = gross − local ingots − local ore a local Smelter refines (1:1) − in-flight ingots and ore` (ladder totals). `S` = free ingots at every other outpost (`outpost_free_tiers()` NEED-band free stock). Ingot request level = local + in-flight + `min(D, S)` at the recipe's prio (holds local ingots back from other sites' demand of that band and below), or + all of `D` when no local Smelter can refine that ore. Rest `D − S` goes to the ore request at the same prio. Preference local ingots > local ore > remote ingots > remote ore, except remote ingots go first (up to `S`, before local ore) when `production.ship_units()` says ship (§2a-0-6 rule: big surplus or slow/impossible local smelt); in-flight counts both sides, so no hysteresis.
  - **Raw inputs** (`raw_input_wants()`): every active-recipe input no Smelter or Fabricator makes (e.g. Forage, which only the home Crop Automators hold) still to stage (`fab_site_gross_need(..., None, ...)`) is requested at level = local + in-flight + `min(short, free at other outposts)` at the recipe's prio; none free and none in flight, no request.
  - **Ship plan** (`ship_wants()`): each item of `production.get_site_ship_plan()` (§2a-0-6 ship-before-craft) is requested at level = local + in-flight + to ship at the plan's prio (`{item: [units, prio]}`); an item this requester already asked for stays requested while units are in flight. Levels of one item merge by max (`want_raise()`), so a higher level from another builder wins.
- **Ingot buffer** (`ingot_wants()`, fab sites): every item of `production.fab_site_ingot_targets()` (§2a-0-2) is requested with its buffer target at `STOCK` and its need level at `NEED`, published even with nothing to pull: the request is what makes `outpost_free_tiers()` keep the buffer from other sites' `STOCK`-band demand and the need level from their `NEED`-band demand. Local Smelters refill it as part of their one demand map (§2a-0-2); haulers bring it where none can.
- **Smelting site** (≥ 1 Smelter, home included): one ore request per ore with an unlocked Smelter recipe: `ore_stock_target(ore)` at `STOCK` (only for an active ore with buffer room, §2d), plus, when a local Fabricator needs that output, level = local + in-flight ore + ore short from `D − S` at the recipe's prio, plus the dock ore level below at the owing order's prio; `want_raise()` keeps the highest level of each builder, so the ore request never drops below the ore's consumer request levels (a dock order there).
- **Stockpiles** (`site_stock_targets()` / `stock_wants()`, any outpost, home included): `SITE_STOCK_TARGETS = {"fabricator": {"forage": 1000}, "refiner": {"tar": WAREHOUSE_STOCK_TARGET}, "fuel_assembler": {"lead_plate": 200}}` per building type present, requested at `STOCK`; an item in `SITE_STOCK_RESEARCH = {"forage": "research_advanced_biopolymers"}` only once that research is unlocked (Forage feeds only Reinforced Biopolymer and Bio Enrichment Compound); the need level `SITE_STOCK_NEED = {"refiner": {"tar": 150}}` (`site_stock_needs()`) at `NEED`; levels the site already planned at a higher prio (ship plan) stay. Requested whether or not any is free elsewhere (the request nets against local stock). `SITE_STOCK_CRAFTED = ("lead_plate", "tar")` items a Fabricator can build are also ordered (`order_site_stock()`): one `fabricator.orders` entry (requester `SITE_STOCK_REQUESTER = "site_stock"`, §2a-1 item 4) sized to the summed targets of all outposts, each item's need levels at `NEED` and the rest up to the target at `STOCK` (tar: 150 per Refiner outpost, crafted by `craft_tar`, 5 t Oil → 2 Tar, when the Lubricant/Plastic/Rubber byproduct falls short). A site order (`production.SITE_ORDER_REQUESTERS`): it raises the Fabricator target but makes home no consumer (`fabricator_root_targets()`), so home doesn't pull the stockpiles back.
- **Construction stock** (`construction_stock_targets()` → `(targets, needs)`, the Constructor's home `production.construction_site_id()` only): archive `site_supply.construction_stock` (`CONSTRUCTION_STOCK_KEY`, `{item: {"target": n, "need": n}}`, read by `construction_stock_levels()`, need capped at target), seeded from `EARLY_CONSTRUCTION_STOCK` (`gas_pipe_segment`, `liquid_pipe_segment`, `power_line_segment` target 25 / need 5; `gas_pipe_bridge`, `liquid_pipe_bridge` target 2 / need 0) and replaced by `LATE_CONSTRUCTION_STOCK` (segments 100 / 10, bridges 5 / 0) once the mining-drill phase is reached (`drone_upgrade.upgrade_phase_reached()`), only while the stored value still equals the early one; no power line bridge, the power network is one grid. Editable in the archive; an edit stops the switch. Each level fits one plan-ahead chunk plus its reserve (autoplay `supply_tiers.plan_ahead_limits()`, [autoplay.md](autoplay.md)), plus, per extractor kind, one kit (`construction_plan.EXTRACTOR_KITS`) per untapped surveyed fluid site (`untapped_kits()`: no `pump_id()`/`cap_id()`; exotic by `medium()`, none once Wildlife is complete), at most `CONSTRUCTION_KIT_CAP = 5`. A fresh `site_supply.construction_rush` record (`construction_rush()`, archive_ipc.md §4) raises its item's need level to the recorded units, capped at the target. Requested like the stockpiles above (target at `STOCK`, need level at `NEED`, `plan_site(..., extra_stock, extra_need)`); the items a Fabricator can build are also a `fabricator.orders` entry (requester `CONSTRUCTION_STOCK_REQUESTER = "construction_stock"`), need levels at `NEED`, the rest up to the target at `STOCK`. Those Fabricator-built items also carry `keep` = their target (`mark_reserve()`): the reserve is not free for another outpost's demand (`outpost_free_tiers()`), so a Supply Dock order elsewhere neither pulls it nor counts it (§2a-1); units above the target stay free. Stock no Fabricator builds gets no `keep` (a dock order for it would wait forever). Covers work that waits for stock before it is planned (the autoplay planner's plan-ahead jobs, [autoplay.md](autoplay_outposts.md) §11g).
- **Home**: planned like any other outpost (ingot, ore, stockpile and consumer requests); its Inventory counts as home stock.
- **Consumer site** (`consumer_wants()`, any outpost, machines or not): per root consumed there (§2a-0-6 consumers, `{item: {site: ladder}}`), level by level at the consumer's prio, highest first (`ladder_deficits()`): local + in-flight + what is free elsewhere at that level's band, free = `free_elsewhere_tiers()` (`outpost_tier_free()` summed: stock above the holder's own request level at that band or above/`keep`, minus other haulers' reservations; the rule haulers take by, so a holder's 605 dock demand doesn't keep units from an 800 pull), each outpost read once per pass (one `PlanReads` in `publish_site_requests()`); kept while units are in flight. A want inherits its root's prio (an order's score, a blueprint's band, a fleet part's `UPGRADE`). **Settled items → `BLOCKER`**: a root whose top level is `UPGRADE` or above (a blueprint's own item, `blueprint_required_ladders()`, or an orders-book entry, `order_ladders()`) that no Fabricator will add more of (`settled_items()`: nothing in `get_fabricator_pipeline()`, `root_remaining()` = 0) has its levels of band `UPGRADE` and above lifted to `BLOCKER` (`ladder_promote()`), so haulers skip their minimum load for it; while more are being built it waits for the batch. Manual transit orders (`manual_transit_wants()`, units built off home) are always `BLOCKER` so completed units haul home immediately. Lower bands (Terraformer Fertilizer, Yield Amplifier doses, stock) wait for a load; there is no exception list. Levels of one item merge by max (`want_raise()`): an ingot request of the same item wins when higher.
- **Dock ore** (`dock_ore_levels()`): a consumer site pulls only units that exist, so a Supply Dock order for a Smelter output with too few units anywhere would never raise an ore request. Per Smelter output still owed (`dock_remaining_requirements()`), units = owed − `SourceCache.network_stock()` − Smelter output not yet in storage (`pipeline_units()`) − its ore already loaded into Smelters (`staged_units()`), charged as its ore at the owing order's prio (`production_docks.dock_owed_prios()`: the most urgent order owing the item) to the first smelting site by id that refines it and whose stationed miners reach it (`mineable_ores_by_outpost()`, §2d); `plan_site()` folds it into that site's ore want, so its stationed miners (`vehicle_mining.stockpile_need()`: `NEED` band and above) and haulers see the same request. Raw ore an order owes itself: owed − network stock, charged the same way to the first site by id whose miners reach it, a site whose dock owes it first. **Dock raw ore** (`dock_raw_wants()`): raw ore a dock at this outpost still owes (`dock_remaining_requirements(site_id)`, e.g. `spire_intake_3`'s titanium) is a site request at level = owed at the owing order's prio, at any outpost with a dock, Smelter or not. Pioneers mining for their home rank it by band once their drill cuts the ore; haulers bring spare units.
- **Role switch drain / eviction** (`evict_stranded()`, requester `EVICT_REQUESTER = "site_evict"`): removing a site's Smelters withdraws its ore request, so its ore is free stock for any request. Ore at an outpost with no Smelter (home included) that it neither mines (an active ore its stationed miners reach: `mineable_ores_by_outpost()` ∩ `active_ores()`, so a mining outpost keeps its buffer), requests nor consumes (a Supply Dock order there, `fabricator_root_targets()` consumers) is stranded; first-seen ticks in archive `site_supply.stranded` (`{outpost_id: {ore: tick}}`, pruned to what is stranded now), checked against this pass's planned requests (`planned_requests()`). After `EVICT_AFTER_TICKS = REQUEST_STALE_TICKS` (6000) it goes to its destinations (below). Withdrawn once gone. **Stranded goods** (same tracking, key and timing): ingots and intermediates — `item_catalog` category in `EVICT_GOODS_CATEGORIES = ("refined", "crafted")` that some Fabricator recipe takes as input (`evictable_goods()`), minus `storage.must_stay_in_inventory()` items and `CONSTRUCTION_ITEM_IDS` (pipe/power line segments and bridges; moved only by blueprint consumer demand) and `EVICT_HOLD_ITEM_IDS = ("tar",)` (also the Refiner's reagent; a Refiner outpost requests it through `SITE_STOCK_TARGETS`) — held in an outpost's Warehouses/Drone Depots (`held_item_ids()`) where there is no Fabricator (home included), not requested there and not a consumer root there (`stranded_goods()`). No `item_catalog` → no goods evicted. **Stragglers** (`stragglers()`, same tracking, key and timing): at most `STRAGGLER_MAX_UNITS = 50` of a finished good (category in `EVICT_GOODS_CATEGORIES`, Constructor items included, minus `must_stay_in_inventory()` and `EVICT_HOLD_ITEM_IDS`; deployables already in storage count too: category in `STRAGGLER_KIT_CATEGORIES = ("construction_kit",)` or an id in `storage.INVENTORY_ONLY_ITEM_IDS`, e.g. the tail of a kit or cargo pod order; `STRAGGLER_ANY_SIZE_ITEM_IDS = ("tar",)` count at any size and despite `EVICT_HOLD_ITEM_IDS`: byproduct tar waits for a Refiner in storage, not in a fab site's slot) at an outpost other than home or a storage outpost that it neither requests nor consumes, and that its Fabricators neither stage (`fab_site_gross_need()`) nor hold in their site targets (`get_site_fabricator_targets()`; a root built there for another site counts only while `get_fabricator_pipeline(cache, site_id)` shows a local Fabricator making it). Requested like stranded goods, but at `BLOCKER` (haulers skip their minimum load); when the destination already requests the item (construction stock at the Constructor's home), its levels of band `UPGRADE` and above are lifted to `BLOCKER` instead. Stranded ore/goods keep their own rule where both match. **Stranded reagents** (`stranded_reagents()`, same tracking, key and timing): lab reagents (`outpost_reagents.LAB_REAGENT_ITEM_IDS`) at an outpost with no Bio Lab (`lab_sites()`, `BIO_LAB_TYPE_ID`) that it neither requests nor consumes. They go home only, and only when home has a Bio Lab or no outpost has one. With only remote Labs they stay: a remote Lab's pull hauler takes free stock before the Shop (`pull_plan.route_sources()`). `sell_stranded_reagents()` (orchestrator storage sweep) sells home's reagents once no Bio Lab has existed for `EVICT_AFTER_TICKS`: Warehouse stock moves to Inventory (`inventory_room()`), then `shop.sell()`; reagents home requests (other than `EVICT_REQUESTER`) stay. Reagents sell at their Shop price, so nothing is lost. **Destinations** (`evict_candidates()`), tried in order until the units are placed: users first (ore: home if a home Smelter refines it, then every smelting site that does, by id (`smelting_sites()`); goods: home if home has a Fabricator (then home is never a source), then every fab site; reagents: home only, see above; Constructor stragglers (`CONSTRUCTION_ITEM_IDS`): `construction_site_id()` only), then the **storage outposts** (`storage_outposts()`: not home, a Warehouse or Storage Bin, a Drone Depot (`item_tiers.DEPOT_TYPE_TIERS`, the drone haulers' only destination) and no `storage.PENALIZED_TYPES` building), nearest first, never from one storage outpost to another. Home is never a fallback: an item with no candidate is not stranded and stays where it is. **Room** (`evict_room()`, home uncapped): `storage.slot_room()` over `storage.slot_layout()` (each Warehouse slot, each Storage Bin as one slot) = room in the slots already holding the item, or its planned request target rounded up to whole slots when that is more (a 1800 ore target may fill its 2000 slot), else one empty slot while `EVICT_FREE_SLOTS_KEEP = 1` others stay empty (`EVICT_STAGING_SLOTS_KEEP = 2` where a miner drone is homed, so its Depot can still open a life-form slot, `lifeform_staging_sites()`); a storage outpost is the dump for leftovers and takes `storage.open_room()` instead: every slot to capacity, empty ones included (`Warehouse.space_for()`), with §2k-5 adding stores before it runs out; minus units in flight there. Units that find no room stay. Home destination → home requests them (requester `EVICT_REQUESTER`), level = home have + in-flight + free there (`NEED` band; stragglers `BLOCKER`), skipped for an item another requester already asks home for; any other destination → `add_evicted()` raises its site-supply level by local + in-flight + the units sent (`STOCK` band, stragglers `BLOCKER`).
- Withdrawn (empty `publish_requests()`) once an outpost has no Smelter/Fabricator demand; unchanged requests follow `publish_requests()`'s republish rule.
- `production.get_smelter_demands()` nets against `SourceCache.network_have()` (`network_stock()`: home Inventory + every outpost's Warehouses and Drone Depots + loaded hauler cargo, `aboard_units()`; plus producer holdings, §Pipeline netting), so ingots a remote Smelter made aren't refined again at home.
