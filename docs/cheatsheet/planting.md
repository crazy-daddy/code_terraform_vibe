# Planting (§1k)

Part of [`AI_CHEATSHEET.md`](../AI_CHEATSHEET.md). Bio pipeline, essence and seed discovery sweep (§1e–§1i) live in [`bio_seeds_planting.md`](bio_seeds_planting.md).

### §1k Planting: Seed Supply + Field Layout + Planting Harvester (stage B)

#### Seed supply

- **Phase change**: `4_controlpanel/bio/seed_maker.py` runs `SeedSupplyController` (`seed_supply.py`) once `len(recipes()) >= SEED_SPECIES_TOTAL`, else the stage-A sweep.
- **Seed supply** (`SeedSupplyController(SeedMakerController)`, reuses chamber/stock/output helpers): makes a seed whose 3 blend forms are all in local stock: **garden first** (`plant.seed_demand["priority"]`), then the largest deficit; a non-garden seed leaves the last unit of any form a garden seed with a deficit needs. Deficit = `plant.seed_demand["now"]` − seeds at home (`total_stock()`: Inventory + home Warehouses). Finished seeds drain to a local Warehouse, Inventory only as fallback (`drain_port_storage_first()`). No fresh demand (older than `SEED_DEMAND_STALE_TICKS = 6000`) → `SEED_BUFFER_PER_SPECIES = 3` seeds per species. Life-form requests (§2i, requester `seed_maker`) = **base stock** (1 t per layout cell whose blend uses the form, from `plant.seed_demand["rotation"]`, i.e. one full replant) + open deficit, capped at `SEED_SUPPLY_STASH_T = 120` per form (room for a bulk replant). **Garden first for drones**: while any form of a `priority` seed is below its target (priority base stock + deficit), the non-garden seeds' base stock is left out of the requests (their open deficit still counts), so drones fetch garden forms before the fill's bulk. Forms outside the layout's blends get no request. Publishes `plant.recipes` for the Harvester (it has no `recipes()`). Restart: a chamber holding a known blend is combined, anything else is ejected.

#### Field and layouts

- **Field**: fixed 8 × 24 grid at home only (A1–H24). Field machines and crops use **no building slots**. Rules read only the 4 orthogonal neighbours: spacer (no other plant beside), cluster (≥2 of own species → 2×2 minimum), companion, antagonist; light/water/salt treatments; shade = must not be lit.
- **Layouts**: three phases (`field_layout.py`, chosen by `harvester_planting.load_layout()`; `LAYOUT_VERSION = 5`, `STARTER_VERSION = 3`; a stored layout with an older version of its mode is rebuilt once):
  - **starter** (`STARTER_LAYOUT`, until Field Automation is researched = `crop_automator_kit` in `deployables()`): 8 rows × 6 columns, 31 plants, 13 species (×13 diversity), hand care only, fixed at `STARTER_ANCHOR = "A1"` (`anchor_layout(..., anchor)`; falls back to the nearest-the-base search only if a plant would land on the base pad or a provider). The block is columns 1–6 of the crowncap full layout (`CROWNCAP_GARDEN` + two Crowncap columns) without its Sunspur and Pondmoss (lit spacer, 2×2 needing 4 waterings/day: too much hand care this early), so every starter cell is a full-layout cell with the same species and the switch to full keeps the whole starter, nothing uprooted (tested). A plant counts toward diversity while its conditions are met, mature or not (an uncared one never counts), so the `STARTER_KEEP` species (SU SH DW LT PF TV SP = the starter species below `SLOW_GROWTH_H = 48` h; `starter_kept()`, stored as `garden`) are planted once, cared for and never harvested. The slow species (`field_layout.slow_species(rules)`: growth_time ≥ `SLOW_GROWTH_H`; in the starter CC GV SB SM BT GB) are harvested **only while a seed of that species is at home** (`stock_count()`: Inventory + home Warehouses), so it is replanted at once: during stage A (§1i) the sweep makes no replanting seeds, and a harvested plant without a seed is lost for good. A mature slow crop without a seed is held (`starter_hold()` → `FieldPass.held`, added to `kept_garden()`): not harvested, still hand-cared, and `harvest_current()` re-checks live (`harvest_blocked()`), since a seed planted on the way may have used the last one. Plant cells ≈ +1 heat per hop, empty gap cells +7, against ~3/h cooling, so the path cells are paved first (Paving below). No machine cells: machines unlocked before automation only save hand-care time. A stored layout without `mode` (older saves) counts as starter.
  - **full** (after Field Automation; fixed 8 × 24, base pad `FULL_LAYOUT_BASE = "E13"`, always the middle): diversity garden with all 15 species in columns 1–`garden_cols(fill)` (`CROWNCAP_GARDEN_COLS = 4` / `GARDEN_COLS = 5` grandbloom), plus a fill, worked by Crop Automators (8 crowncap / 12 grandbloom). Only machines that give a kept plant a service it needs are reserved (`_needed_machines()`). `full_layout(chunks, fill)`: chunk 1 = the garden plus the automators covering it (none crowncap / 3 grandbloom); each further chunk adds the next automator left to right with the fill cells in its area. `validate()` clean and 15 species for every chunk count of both fills (from chunk 2 on for crowncap); `provider_violations()` clean for grandbloom (the crowncap garden is hand-cared). `LAYOUT_VERSION = 5`. Fill (`FIELD_FILL = "crowncap"`, operator override Data Archive key `plant.field_fill`):
    - **phase 2, `crowncap`** (most of the game: Grandbloom only pays at Mk IV machines, >2,500 t/h water): `CROWNCAP_GARDEN` (columns 1–4: the 14 non-fill species, 20 plant cells, **no machines and no Crop Automator**; the Harvester plants each cell once, cares for it by hand and never harvests it. A Dispenser burns 48 salt/day vs 1 salt/day per salt cell by hand, and a hand light treatment lights only its own cell, so lit species may border the fill. Offline search: 3 columns can't satisfy all neighbour rules, 4 can with no spacer beside column 5; picked for the shortest care tour, 14 hops over the 13 cells needing light/water/salt) + solid Crowncap everywhere else except the base pad, lamp-lit cells (shade) and cells beside a spacer; clusters cut by a chunk edge are pruned (`_prune_clusters()`). 8 Crop Automators (`CROWNCAP_AUTOMATORS` = C/F 7, 12, 17, 22) cover columns 5–24 exactly; none reaches the garden. 1.25 Forage per cell-hour with no machines, power or water; about 2× the life forms per Forage (a seed per 60 Forage vs 150). Chunk 1: garden only (0 Forage/h); chunk 2: 1 automator, 44 plants, ~450 Forage/h; chunk 9 (full): 171 plants (151 harvested), ~2,830/h.
    - **phase 3, `grandbloom`**: `FULL_LAYOUT` as drawn, a Grandbloom checkerboard (each touches only machines; Grow Lamp rows alternate with Sprinkler rows). Pays from Mk II lamps + sprinklers (×3; ×12 cap at Mk IV). Chunk 1: 28 plants, 24 machines, ~560/h; chunk 10: 94 plants, 90 machines, ~2,600/h at Mk I. Switched by the operator (`plant.field_fill = "grandbloom"`), which rebuilds the full layout once.
  - Chunks: **crowncap fill = whole field** (`full_chunk_count()`, 9) right away, since it needs no machines, power or water. **grandbloom** is sized from demand: `chunks_for_demand(terraformer_demand(plant.terraformer), rules, fill)`, `forage_per_hour()` = Σ base/growth × diversity at Mk I; Terraformer use `TERRAFORMER_BATCH = {1: 1200, 2: 6600}` per `TERRAFORMER_CYCLE_H = 3`. The switch to full happens once; a full layout then only grows (same fill), re-checked every `LAYOUT_RECHECK_TICKS = 3000`. Full mode: no paving (the Harvester drives over machines); cells a deployed automator serves are its work; the Harvester plants only garden cells no automator serves, never a fill cell, even before its automator is deployed (one Harvester cannot keep up with the whole fill, and a hand-planted fill cell pulled it across the field and back); seed demand (`seed_layout()`) covers the Harvester cells + automated cells. The Harvester harvests everything else mature. **Garden kept** (full layout only, `field_layout.kept_crop()`): a garden cell holding its own layout species is planted once and never harvested, by the Harvester or a Crop Automator. A mature crop still counts toward the species multiplier, and a replant risks a species whose seed is missing; the fill carries the Forage. A wrong species in a garden cell is harvested as usual. `forage_per_hour(cells, rules, garden)` counts garden cells toward diversity only.
  - **Rebuild**: every crop not on a layout cell of its own species is stale (`field_layout.stale()`: wrong species on a layout cell, on a reserved cell, or off the layout, e.g. the old starter zone; `stale_targets()`). Stale crops are cleared in one **sweep**: mature → harvest, growing/stalled → uproot (seed back to Inventory), also when the layout no longer uses the species (the seed comes back, the trip is paid while the Harvester is in the zone; waiting for maturity costs one more crossing per crop). Exception: a mature stale crop on a layout cell a Crop Automator serves is its job. Not replanted.
- **Starter expansion** (`expand_layout()`, off: `LAYOUT_EXPANSION_ENABLED = False`): greedy single-plant additions of the best `base_yield / growth_time / blend_cost` species, where `blend_cost` = Σ `RARITY_COST = {common: 1, uncommon: 2, rare: 5}` over the blend (static `LIFE_FORM_RARITY` from docs). Only the new cell and its neighbours are re-validated. Caps: `MAX_DAILY_CARE = 20` treatments/day, `MAX_FIELD_PLANTS = 40`.

#### Harvester loop

- **Harvester loop** (`FieldKeeperController`), one task per step, cheapest route (heat) first: **apply the Yield Amplifier** when due (see below) → **remove a stray field machine** (full layout: deployed on a cell the layout doesn't reserve for its kind, e.g. after a layout rebuild; see Field machines below) → **build** (full layout, only while `missing_machines()` is non-empty — the build phase; once every reserved machine is in, the machines carry the field and leftover own cells go through care → harvest → plant; phase flips are logged. First every Crop Automator whose kit is at home, the nearest (route cost) each step, so all go out in one round over the fill; then deploy a field machine whose kit is at home, or plant an own cell, picked by `field_layout.work_order()` rank instead of heat: the garden row by row in a snake. Build ranks first because each machine removes care work for good) → **running sweep**: the nearest stale crop (rebuild, above) within route cost `SWEEP_REACH_COST = 26` (3 empty hops) from the current cell, or farther but still nearer than any layout work (a plantable cell, a mature layout crop; `sweep_next()`, so a sparse old field is still cleared in one visit); otherwise the sweep ends. Ahead of care, even a lapsing treatment: a lapse only stalls growth, while leaving the old zone mid-sweep costs two crossings at +7 heat per empty hop → **care tour** (before harvest: a lapsed treatment stalls growth, a mature crop just waits; a mature kept cell — full-layout garden, starter keeper — is still cared for, or it drops out of the diversity count) → **starter: pave a path cell first** (`pave_first()`, at any heat; strict only while the material is near or nothing is plantable, `choose_pave_first()`; below) → the non-urgent kinds, in rank order: harvest a mature layout crop (then replant on the spot; starter slow crops only with their seed at home, above) → start a sweep (a stale crop) → plant open layout cell with its seed at home (clears a loose item there first, as below) → **pave** a path cell (below) → clear loose items off the path that are not kept for paving. **Locality** (`choose_local()`): among these the highest-ranked kind with a target within route cost `LOCAL_ROUTE_COST = 17` (2 empty hops, or ~6 over plant/item cells) wins over a farther target of a higher rank; none local: the highest-ranked kind with any target, except that a far stale crop starts a sweep only when nothing else is due (it loses nothing by waiting; the trip costs +7 heat per empty hop). Both use the step's single-source route map (`nearest_within()`, a cost-capped continuation of `cheapest()`). Above these everything stays strict (amplify, strays, build, running sweep, care, `pave_first`). Loose items only at heat ≤ `ITEM_SWEEP_MAX_HEAT = 40` (`clear_here()`: collect, `store()`, `shop.sell(item, 1)`; a seed of a layout species (`keep_item_ids()`) and an item the Shop refuses stay in Inventory; deploys clear their cell the same way). Nothing due: wait in place (no trip back to base: costs heat, nothing needs the base). Care tour: once any treatment drops below `CARE_REFRESH_H = 4.0` h, every treatment below `CARE_BATCH_H = 20.0` h is renewed in one tour, so renewals line up (at 12 h, salt (24 h) and light/water (~16 h) never did: 2-3 partial tours a day across the 8-row garden). Any visit also renews everything below `CARE_BATCH_H` on that cell. **On the way**: every Harvester-own cell (`harvester_layout()`) a route passes through (not the target, nothing held) is harvested if mature and planted if open with its seed at home (`FieldKeeperController.work_on_pass()`, the hook `move_to()` calls): the next pass costs +1 heat instead of +7. `plant_here()` decrements the per-step `stock_memo` seed count (a harvest drops the Forage entry), so a route with two cells of one species and one seed plants once. Provider-covered = flag set with 0 manual time. Water refilled when < 1 t aboard. Failed plant → cell skipped `PLANT_FAIL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. Inventory holds 20 Forage per slot (10 without `high_density_storage_unlock`), so one crop (Crowncap 60 × 13 = 780) can fill it: after a `partial` or `inventory_full` harvest the Harvester waits on the cell (`wait_for_forage_room()`, `PARTIAL_WAIT_STEP_H = 0.25` h steps, at most `PARTIAL_WAIT_H = 1.0` h) until Inventory has room for the crop's remaining `cell.forage` (the Plant Terraformers pull it within ~30 min), then harvests the rest on the spot. Moving it to a Warehouse itself was slower: a `rebalance_inventory_to_warehouses()` pass took ~4.6 game hours of script time headless. Still `inventory_full` on harvest or uproot (`inventory_full()`) → harvest and sweep pause, and at most once per `REBALANCE_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000` the Field Keeper runs `storage.rebalance_inventory_to_warehouses()` itself; with room for Forage afterwards (`inventory_room`) the pause is `INVENTORY_FULL_RELIEVED_RETRY_TICKS = 300`, else `INVENTORY_FULL_RETRY_TICKS = 3000`. Never sells to make room. Direct call, not a request flag: the orchestrator already runs the same sweep every `STORAGE_TICK_INTERVAL` (100 ticks), so a flag would change nothing; the direct call lets the retry right after see the result. Each move is one atomic transfer, so a concurrent orchestrator pass only meets a fuller or emptier source (the Harvester already moves Inventory items when it stages seeds). Harvester actions retried `ACTION_RETRIES = 3` on busy/moving.

#### Heat and paving

- **Heat** (`harvester_heat.py`): move onto item +1, empty +7, empty collect +9, passive cooling ~3/world-hour (`COOL_PER_HOUR_DEFAULT`, from the vakermit harvester; also during actions). That's ~72 heat/day = only ~13 net empty-cell hops/day without resting, so heat is the Harvester's real budget. Routes: single-source Dijkstra over all 192 cells (Dial buckets, costs ×`COST_SCALE = 2`, <1 ms offline), per hop `move_cost(status) + HOP_TIME_WEIGHT = 1.5`, so detours over plant/item cells (+1) beat crossing empty cells (+7); one map serves target choice (`cheapest()`) and the route. Plant statuses (`growing`/`stalled`/`mature`) seeded at +1 from the first live readings. Rests only right before a hop/action would cross `max_heat − HEAT_SAFETY (3)`, and only as long as that needs (`real_seconds_per_hour()`); overheated action → rest to cap − `ACTION_HEAT_RESERVE = 9`. Move cost per destination status (seed `{"item": 1, "empty": 7, "unknown": 7, "growing"/"stalled"/"mature": 1}`, others (provider, base) `DEFAULT_MOVE_COST = 7` until measured) and cooling rate are measured every hop/rest (EMA `CALIBRATION_ALPHA = 0.3`) and saved in `plant.status[<id>]["heat"]`. First live readings: plant cells (`mature`, `stalled`) ≈ +1. Field actions heat up too: each action in `ACTION_HOURS` (plant/harvest 0.5 h, treatments/collect/refill 0.25 h, ...) is budgeted before the call (`ACTION_COST_DEFAULT = 3.0` until measured) and its cost learned from the heat rise corrected for cooling during the action (`heat.actions`). Near the cap it therefore rests right before every step: that's the intended cooling-limited steady state, not a stall. **Step budget**: the script gets a limited number of steps per tick, so planning is cached: neighbour table built once, one statuses dict per step shared by target choice and route (heat map cached by identity), `stock_count()` memoised per step (each `total_stock()` walks every Warehouse), rules re-read every `PUBLISH_INTERVAL_TICKS`, layout loaded once per run. Debug logs per decision `planned in <world time> (<ticks>: <phase> <ticks>, ...)` (`mark()` phases cells/layout/publish/machines/build/care/decide, `plan_note()`): planning runs on the step budget and can take a sizeable part of a world hour. Also debug: each move as a `Move A -> B` block (route, then arrival with world time and heat), each Harvester action (`act()`: result, world time, heat), and the start of an idle streak (`log_idle()`: next hand care in h, harvest pause), whose length the next decision line reports. Per-hop heat is `trace`.
- **Paving** (`harvester_paving.py`): a dropped item (seeds included) makes a cell an item cell, so moving onto it costs +1 heat instead of +7. Plant cells calibrate at about +1 too, so the expensive cells are the plant-free gaps the rules force. `field_layout.path_cells()` picks a short path of gap cells joining every plant patch (greedy Steiner tree over 4-connected patches; base pad and reserved machine cells excluded) — **4 gap cells in the starter block** (A2, A4, B3, C2; its spacers are separate patches). Off in the full layout. Each gets one item, once. **Starter: paving ranks after the care tour and before harvest, sweep and planting, with no heat gate** (`pave_first()`), while there is material, but only while the material (the nearest spare item, or for a seed the nearest unpaved cell) is within `LOCAL_ROUTE_COST` or nothing is plantable (`choose_pave_first()`). Otherwise planting goes first and the pave waits among the non-urgent kinds (any heat) until the Harvester is near it (carry-along) or nothing else is due. A spare item beyond route cost `PAVE_ITEM_MAX_COST = 43` (5 empty hops, ~35 heat one way, ~12 h of cooling, with the carry to the path about a day's heat budget) is left when a paving seed is at home: the cell is paved with the seed (3 t life forms) instead. Material: a loose item, or a paving seed beyond the open layout cells of that species in the Harvester's cells (paving never takes a seed a layout cell waits for). Each hop still rests just in time (Heat above). Why no gate: unpaved, the gap cells cost +7 per hop and the hand-cared starter keeps heat near the cap, so a ≤ 40 gate never opens (DESIGN_HISTORY: garden-aligned starter). A pave step that paves nothing turns `pave_first()` off for `PAVE_FAIL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. Otherwise (tail of the loop) paving runs when heat ≤ `ITEM_SWEEP_MAX_HEAT`. Material: first a loose item carried over from elsewhere on the field, else (`PAVE_WITH_SEEDS = True`) a seed of the species with the cheapest blend (`blend_cost`), loaded from Inventory and `drop()`ped (3 t life forms per cell). Paving seeds are added to `plant.seed_demand["now"]`, at most `PAVE_SEED_BATCH = 5` at a time. **Loose items** (`split_loose()`): of the items off the path, the cheapest up to Scanner value `PAVE_MAX_VALUE = 250`, one per unpaved path cell (≤ 4), are kept as paving material (`FieldPass.spare_items`); every other one is cleared and sold (`clear_items`, loop above). An item on a path cell worth more than `PAVE_MAX_VALUE` (not a seed) is cleared and sold too; its cell is then unpaved and gets a cheap item. Cheapest = `rank_loose()`: the Scanner's value (`SCANNER_ID = "scanner_1"`, `get_scanned()`), at equal value a non-seed first, items without a scan last, ties by sector. The Scanner is read only while a path cell is unpaved or holds an item, and the set of item sectors changed. Full layout or paved path: every loose item is cleared. The early Harvester leaves `PAVE_KEEP_ITEMS` items nearest the path on the field for this, never one worth more than its own `PAVE_MAX_VALUE` (same value, checked by `tests/test_loose_items.py`) ([vehicles_drones.md §2](vehicles_drones.md)). `PAVING_ENABLED` switches paving off.

#### Field Keeper caching and Harvester machines

- **Field Keeper loop and per-step caching** (`field_keeper.py`): `run()` sleeps `LOOP_SLEEP_SECONDS = 0.2` between steps, `IDLE_POLL_SECONDS = 3.0` when nothing is due. Each step is one `FieldPass` (`harvester_pass.py`; `prepare_pass()` → `choose()` → `run_choice()`): it reads the cells once and shares the pass's cells, statuses and planted cells through `cell_statuses()`/`planted_cells()` (`harvester_hub.py`), and holds the step's deployed-machine read (`step_machines()`, hub) and plan-note phases; `care_snapshot()` reads the planted cells once per step (`planted_rows()`) and scans them in one pure pass (`row_scan()` in `harvester_care.py`, shared with the status entry) for `care_targets()`/`next_care_hours()`. The publish phase's pure scans (care/status scan, seed demand, care targets) live in `harvester_pure.py` and run through `lib/atomic.py` `run_atomic()` in chunks (`ROW_CHUNK = 32`, `SOON_CHUNK = 96`, `CARE_CHUNK = 48`, `ITEM_CHUNK = 96`; worst chunk ~3,900 operations, asserted ≤ `ATOMIC_STEP_BUDGET = 4000` in `tests/test_harvester_pure.py`); a reloaded recipe set equal to the current one keeps its object so the memos keyed on it stay valid. Pure geometry in `field_layout.py` is memoised (`sector_to_rc`, `neighbours`, `automator_area` as tuples, capped at `_CACHE_LIMIT = 1024` entries; `_full_parts`, `full_chunk_count`, `full_layout` (returns copies), `work_order`), and `active_layout`, `harvester_layout`, `seed_layout`, `build_rank`, `automated_cells`, `missing_machines` are memoised on their inputs — their results are shared, read-only. An unchanged layout on the `LAYOUT_RECHECK_TICKS` re-read keeps its object so those memos hold. Routing (`harvester_heat.py`): `heat_search()` holds a resumable Dijkstra (Dial buckets) over flat lists indexed by grid position (`_grid()`, built once), unreachable = `INFINITE_COST`, cached per (start, statuses dict). `expand_until()` expands only as far as needed: `cheapest()` stops after the bucket holding the first target reached, `route()` until its target is final, continuing the same search. Plan note phases: `cells`, `rules` / `reload` (only on their refresh ticks), `active`, `layout`, `publish`, `machines`, `build`, `care`, `decide`.
- **Field machines, Harvester side** (`harvester_machines.py` `HarvesterMachinesMixin`): deployed machines from `home.harvesting_machines()` (type + position, once per step; fallback `Cell.status == "provider"`). **Kit order**: standing Fabricator order `fabricator.orders["field_keeper"]` (UPGRADE band) for the Grow Lamp / Sprinkler / Dispenser kits still missing on reserved cells (only kinds in `deployables()`, re-read every `DEPLOYABLES_REFRESH_TICKS = 3000`), a stock target capped at `KIT_STOCK_CAP = 10` per kit so Warehouses don't fill with kits. Full layout: its reserved cells. Starter: **pre-order** for `full_layout(desired chunks)` once the Plants Sensor reads ≥ `PREORDER_MIN_KM2 = 550000` (Field Automation at 620,000). **Crop Automator kits** are Shop items (catalogue price, fallback `AUTOMATOR_PRICE_FALLBACK = 30000`): full layout only, one bought per publish while fewer are at home than missing (so the build phase deploys them all in one round), once the cash manager grants it (consumer `crop_automator`, planned = wanted × price, §2l); the missing count is `plant.status[...]["automators_wanted"]`. **Deploy** (full layout only): kit at home → drive there, collect a loose item, clear a crop on the cell (`clear_crop_here()`: harvest if mature, else uproot; not cleared → cooldown), `stage()` the kit, `deploy()`. Failed cell skipped `DEPLOY_FAIL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. A new machine has no script until scripts_sync fills its slot. **Strays** (`stray_machines()`, full layout: a deployed field machine the layout doesn't reserve for its kind): `undeploy()` refuses stored items and `eject()` is self-only, so the machine empties itself: a stray provider stays off and ejects its input (Dispenser salt) to Inventory; a stray automator queues no jobs, clears its queue and ejects its input; Forage in an automator's output drains through `storage.take_item()`. While a stray holds items (`count()` reads, remote) the Harvester leaves it, restarting a stopped script with `run_control.start()` so it can empty itself, re-checked every `DEPLOY_FAIL_COOLDOWN_TICKS`. Once empty: drive there, `run_control.stop()` its script, `undeploy()` (kit back to Inventory). No Crop Automator kit is bought while a stray automator is still out (its kit comes back).

#### Crop Automator and field machines

- **Crop Automator** (`crop_automator.py` `CropAutomatorController`, thin `harvesting/crop_automator.py`), every `POLL_INTERVAL_S = 10`, full layout only: consumes job results (the 50-result inbox pauses the machine when full); owns the layout cells in its 5 × 5 area that no nearer deployed automator reaches (`field_layout.automator_owner()`: Chebyshev, then Manhattan, then sector id: every cell exactly one owner); queues `harvest()` for its mature crops (status `mature` or growth ≥ 1.0) the output has room for, except kept garden crops (a garden automator only plants) and `plant()` for its open cells once the machines beside the cell give every care service the species needs (automators only apply Fertilizer/Growth Accelerant); seeds in the input are netted against queued plant jobs (`queued_jobs_info()` → committed seeds per id), and only when none are spare are `SEED_PULL_BATCH = 10` loaded at once (`take_item()`, Inventory then home Warehouses); a pull that hits the input's 8-material slot cap (`storage.hit_slot_cap()`, §2a-0-2 Material slots) ejects seeds no owned cell plants to Inventory (`eject_unused_seeds()`; Fertilizer and other non-seed inputs stay); keeps the queue ≤ `QUEUE_LIMIT = 45`; failed cell skipped `JOB_FAIL_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 3000`. **Blocked head job** (`unblock_queue()`, FIFO executor would freeze): `no_seed` → load one seed, else `cancel_job()` + cell cooldown; `output_full` → wait (debug log only; rare, see output room); `QUIET_BLOCKERS = ("no_power", "not_placed", "results_full")` are normal states, debug only; anything else warns. **Output room** (`harvest_budget()`, `trim_harvests()`): a harvest that finds the output short keeps what fits and discards the rest (`partial`), so harvests are queued only while free space (`OUTPUT_CAP = 50000` − output Forage) covers one learned yield each; queued harvests past that budget are `cancel_job()`ed newest first (a `working` one finishes), the cell stays mature. Yield = `max(this harvest, estimate × HARVEST_YIELD_DECAY = 0.95)` over kept + discarded, start `HARVEST_YIELD_DEFAULT = 1000` (Crowncap ≈ 900 amplified), published as `harvest_yield`. Mature crops waiting for room are no work: the automator parks. **Wake on pull**: `take_item()` wakes a parked automator only when the pull leaves free ≥ `storage.crop_automator_wake_free()` = max(`CROP_AUTOMATOR_WAKE_FREE_MIN = 1000`, its `harvest_yield`). **Forage stays in the output** (holds 50,000; no drain to Warehouses) so Warehouse auto-loaders stay free; a clogged automator is accepted over clogged Warehouses. **Drain priority** (`storage.crop_automator_forage()`, used by `take_item()` via `_holder_candidates()`): Inventory → Warehouses → clogged automators (`status() == "output_full"` or output ≥ `CROP_AUTOMATOR_CLOG_FRACTION = 0.9` × `CROP_AUTOMATOR_OUTPUT_CAP = 50000`) → other automators (Forage in Inventory/Warehouses only takes slots, so it drains first); within the automator groups garden ones first (`garden` flag = automator column ≤ `garden_cols(fill)`, from `plant.automators` telemetry; the crowncap layout has none, so only a stray left in the garden after a rebuild carries it, and it drains first), then most Forage. Automators are found by `harvesting_machines("crop_automator")` type id, never by id spelling. Known limits: each Forage `take_item()`/`outpost_stock()` walks every automator (`get_component()` + `count()` + `status()`), fine at 10 s polls, cache it if a hot loop starts calling it; the `garden` flag is only as fresh as that automator's last publish (a shed automator's is missing, so it ranks as fill). `logistics_requests.outpost_stock()`/`outpost_free_stock()` count automator Forage at home (`crop_automator_forage_total()`). **Seed demand fallback** (`refresh_seed_demand_if_stale()`): if `plant.seed_demand` is ≥ `SEED_DEMAND_FALLBACK_TICKS = 3000` ticks old (Harvester offline), the first deployed automator republishes it for the **automated fill cells only** (no garden cells; `rotation` = those cells per seed, `now` = `seed_buffer()` per seed, `priority` = `priority_seeds()`), via `archive.transaction` that keeps a Harvester write landing in between. No jobs while in `power.shedded`. Loose items on its cells are swept by the Harvester. Telemetry `plant.automators`. Draw 60 W.
- **Crop Automator pacing**: `step()` returns busy (results consumed, queue non-empty, a blocked job, or a job submitted); `run()` sleeps `POLL_INTERVAL_S` while busy, `IDLE_POLL_SECONDS = 30.0` otherwise. `_field_view()` reuses the deployed-machine map, automators and this instance's owned cells for `DEPLOYED_CACHE_TICKS = 600` (~60 s; refreshed early when the layout's cell/reserved counts change, never caches an unreadable scan); `_cached_ready()` memoizes `services_ready` per (sector, species) until that refresh.
- **Field machines, machine side** (`field_provider.py` `FieldProviderController`, thin `harvesting/grow_lamp.py`, `sprinkler.py`, `dispenser.py`), every `POLL_INTERVAL_S = 10`: enabled only while a layout crop beside it needs its service (`plant.layout` + `plant.recipes`; no layout stored → just runs) and it isn't in `power.shedded`. A Sprinkler drinks Water even with no crops, hence the off switch. Sprinkler `water_in` via a `FluidInputRouter` (Fabricator values, home sources first). Dispenser tops its 50-unit input up to `DISPENSER_FILL = 8` salt once below `DISPENSER_REFILL_BELOW = 3` (`storage.take_item()`: Inventory, then home Warehouses), small so the Harvester's hand-care salt isn't drained. Mk I draws: lamp/sprinkler 5 W (sprinkler 2 t/h Water). Telemetry `plant.providers` every `PUBLISH_INTERVAL_TICKS = 600`. The Harvester stops treating a covered cell by hand (flag set with 0 manual time).

#### Seed demand, Yield Amplifier and salt

- **Seed demand** `plant.seed_demand` (every `PUBLISH_INTERVAL_TICKS = 600`): `now` = open layout cells + layout crops at growth ≥ `SEED_PREFETCH_GROWTH = 0.75` or mature + `seed_supply.seed_buffer(rotation)` spare seeds = rotation ÷ `SEED_BUFFER_CELLS_PER = 6`, clamped to [`SEED_BUFFER_PER_SPECIES = 3`, `SEED_BUFFER_MAX = 30`] (one helper, shared with the automator fallback); `rotation` = cells per seed in `seed_layout()` (full layout: the Harvester's own cells + automated cells, minus the kept garden: an open garden cell adds 1 to `now`, but no rotation, buffer or prefetch); `priority` (full layout only) = `field_layout.priority_seeds()`: every garden species except the fill species, sorted — the Seed Maker makes these first; `replace` (starter only, `{}` in the full layout) = `{seed_id: n}` per slow species (`SLOW_GROWTH_H`): mature (status `mature` or growth ≥ 1.0) layout crops outside the stored garden minus that seed's home stock, entries > 0 only (`replace_demand()`). The stage-A Seed Maker makes these known blends before its next discovery trial, so held crops can be harvested. Held crops stay in `now`/`rotation` (only the stored garden is left out there).
- **Yield Amplifier** (`harvester_amplify.py` `HarvesterAmplifyMixin`): one `yield_amplifier` = +200% base Forage for every growing crop, 24 h; the timer drains even when nothing grows, and a dose applied with time left adds 24 h. **Apply** (first task of every Harvester step, no move): `amplifier_remaining()` is 0, a dose is at home (staged into Inventory: `amplify()` reads Inventory only), ≥ `AMPLIFY_MIN_GROWING_FRACTION = 0.5` of the planted cells are `growing`, and no majority of deployed Crop Automators is clogged (`storage.crop_automator_forage()`; consumers drain clogged automators first among automators). Any failed apply skips it for `AMPLIFY_RETRY_TICKS = 3000`, or `AMPLIFY_BUSY_RETRY_TICKS = 300` when staging failed only because every Warehouse holding a dose answered `"busy"`. **Order**: while a Fabricator lists the recipe (re-read every `RECIPE_REFRESH_TICKS = 3000`), requester `"field_amplifier"` holds a `fabricator.orders` entry of `AMPLIFIER_NEED = 3` at the NEED band and `AMPLIFIER_STOCK = 10` at the STOCK band (idle time); a recurring requester, so site supply hauls the doses home with full loads. Status entry: `amplifier_order`, `amplifier_h`.
- **Salt**: species needing salt stay unplanted while home salt (Inventory + Warehouses) < `SALT_MIN_STOCK = 3`. Home's salt request covers the field and the Plant Terraformers (see **Salt budget** below); the Harvester draws from that stock. **Staging**: seeds and salt live in home Warehouses like any bulk item (no Inventory clutter). `load_seed()`/`dispense_salt()`/`amplify()` read Inventory only, so `FieldKeeperController.stage(item_id)` pulls one unit from a Warehouse into Inventory right before each use (`transfer_to("inventory", ...)`, works from anywhere). Warehouses that answered `"busy"` (Auto Feeder cycle) within `storage.TAKE_BUSY_COOLDOWN_TICKS` are tried last (`storage.mark_busy()`/`recently_busy()`, shared with `take_item()`); `stage_busy` reports a failure caused only by busy holders; a Warehouse still answering `"busy"` after `STAGE_STUCK_WARN_TICKS = 6000` (~10 min) gets one warning until it answers otherwise. A leftover unit is swept back out by `rebalance_inventory_to_warehouses()`.
- **Salt budget** (game facts, same in every game): all Water Pumps together make at most **50 salt/h** (1,200/day), the only salt source; it reaches home only by Pioneer haul (drones can't load pump salt, see `lib/pump_salt.py`), ~20 salt per trip early on. Consumers: Harvester hand care = 1 salt per salt-plant cell per 24 h (~0.042/h per cell); a Dispenser burns **48 salt/day** whatever it covers (0.5/h per cell even with all 4 neighbours salt plants, 12x hand care), so salt species are hand-cared by the Harvester wherever it has the time; Plant Terraformers need **0.002 salt/Forage** from the third band on. Terraformer bands (km² per Forage, cumulative extra input): 0-500k 20; 500k-1.25m 5 (+Water); 1.25m-2.25m 1.67 (+Salt); 2.25m-3.5m 1 (+Fertilizer); 3.5m-5m 0.33 (+Growth Accelerant). So 1.25m-5m takes ~6.4M Forage and ~12,800 salt, i.e. at least ~256 h of the whole salt cap; every salt/h the field burns lowers the Forage/h the Terraformers can use (cap ≈ (50 − field salt/h) / 0.002 = 25,000 Forage/h with no field salt). Salt stored before 1.25m is not lost. Salt has no use outside home's Plants, so one **home salt request** covers it all (`pump_salt.publish_home_salt_request()`, requester `SALT_REQUESTER_ID = "salt_reserve"`, published by the orchestrator Automation's storage pass so it outlives the Harvester/Terraformer scripts; via `publish_requests(..., skip_foreign=False)`, so it overrides a Terraformer's salt entry at home): NEED level `SALT_FIELD_UNITS` = `storage.WAREHOUSE_STOCK_TARGET` (one Warehouse slot less a 200 margin against overspill, the field's stock); target = that + `salt_to_finish(Plants km²)` (Forage left in the salt bands `SALT_BANDS` (phase 3 on of `plant_terraformer_common.PLANTS_BANDS`) / `SALT_FORAGE_PER_ITEM = 500`, plus one item per `plant_terraformer_common.TERRAFORMER_MK2_BATCH = 6600` Forage for per-batch rounding: 13,663 below 1.25m, 0 at 5m; Plants km² from the Plants Sensor, none = 0), capped at home salt + Warehouse room above `SALT_KEEP_FREE = 4000`, then raised by `slot_target()` to the next whole-slot count less the same margin (`game_rules.WAREHOUSE_SLOT_UNITS`; e.g. 15,463 -> 15,800), never below the NEED level (ladder `[[NEED, SALT_FIELD_UNITS], [STOCK, target]]`). A normal request (§2i): served by pull haulers from pumps and any outpost holding salt, and kept back from other outposts' buffers by `outpost_free_tiers()`.

#### Plant Terraformer and completion

- **Plant Terraformer** (`plant_terraformer.py` `PlantTerraformerController` + `PlantTerraformerWaterMixin` (`plant_terraformer_water.py`) + `PlantTerraformerDemandMixin` (`plant_terraformer_demand.py`, demand advert + Fabricator orders), keys/ladder in `plant_terraformer_common.py`, thin `bio/plant_terraformer.py`): Inventory is location-bound for ordinary I/O, so Harvester Forage (home Inventory) feeds a Terraformer at home, or must be hauled. Holders fit one full batch (Mk I 1,200 / Mk II 6,600 Forage) + Salt + 10 of each support item; all consumed at cycle start, so the next batch is preloaded while one runs. Materials follow `required_inputs()`/`batch_requirements()`: Forage/Salt/Growth Accelerant via `storage.take_item()` (Inventory + local Warehouses), Salt up to the full batch's need (Mk II 14), Fertilizer best tier first until unopened potency covers `fertilizer_potency` (Fertilizer tiers / Accelerant capped `SUPPORT_HOLDER_CAP = 10`), Water via a `FluidInputRouter` on `water_in` once the phase needs it (Fabricator's constants). **Run policy**: a cycle is 3 h and draws power (Mk I 180 W, Mk II 900 W) whenever enabled, blocked or not, and commits the largest batch every loaded material supports (half-full water tank = half batch). `supported_batch()` = min over Forage onboard, `water_in.level()` × forage/water, Salt × `SALT_FORAGE_PER_ITEM = 500`, unopened Fertilizer potency, Accelerant items (each scaled by `batch_requirements()`). An idle machine is enabled only when that reaches `start_threshold()` = `START_BATCH_FRACTION = 1.0` of the full batch (a partial batch spends the same whole Salt/Accelerant items and power) (floor `MIN_START_FORAGE = 100`, or the full batch if smaller), else disabled -- also while an input like uncraftable Fertilizer is missing; the limiting material is logged on change and published as `blocker` (+ `supported_batch`). An enabled machine starts a batch the moment the last one ends, or, idle, as soon as its holders support one Forage (mid-transfer too), and Salt/Accelerant are whole items per batch, so a partial batch wastes them: an idle machine is disabled before loading (support items first, then Forage); while a batch runs, Forage preloads first (once onboard + local stock of every input, `reachable_support()`, reaches the threshold) and the support items only after the onboard Forage reaches it, keeping the holders unstartable until then. `complete`/`needs_mk2` → disabled, requests cleared, `needs_mk2` warned once. Restart: in-flight batch found from `get_progress()`/`is_running()` or the last published `in_flight`, re-enabled. **Demand advert** (requester `plant_terraformer`, §2i): every needed material is a **local stock target** (request ladder `[[NEED, target]]`) (next batch staged in the outpost's Warehouses/Drone Depots, holders not counted) -- the shape `outpost_deficits()` (Pioneer pull hauler, floating drone hauler) and `network_deficits()` (miner drones) already read. Forage: one full batch, only away from home (home = field output = source). Salt/Growth Accelerant: `SUPPORT_REQUEST_BATCHES = 10` batches. Fertilizer: as the tier the Fabricator crafts (`craft_fertilizer_item()`), "have" in that tier's potency units over all tiers. "have" = `logistics_requests.outpost_stock()`, read only on a write (`publish_requests(have_of=...)`, §2i). An item another requester already owns at the outpost (e.g. the Harvester's salt) is left alone. Loader (`_take()`): away from home, local Drone Depot (drone freight) first, then Warehouses (every item, Salt/Fertilizer too, not just Forage); at home, `take_item()` (Forage in Crop Automator drain priority, above: Inventory → Warehouses → clogged automators → other automators, garden first) → Drone Depot. Depot takes go through `logistics_requests.take_from_depots(port, item_id, amount, outpost)` (also the Seed Maker's depot fallback). Home Forage `local_stock()` includes the automator outputs. **Source side**: an item the outpost doesn't request itself (home Forage) is only consumed above the other outposts' summed shortfall (target minus published `have`, `remote_retain()`), so a home Terraformer leaves the batch a hauler comes for; a requesting outpost never holds back. **Fabricator orders** (`fabricator_orders()`, §2a-1): Fertilizer is crafted as the first unlocked of `FERTILIZER_CRAFT_PREFERENCE = ("fertilizer_mk2", "fertilizer")`. Per 100 potency incl. sub-crafts: Mk I 0.90 Fabricator-h / 5 Tar / 5 Glass; Mk II 0.65 h / 3.3 Tar / 5 Glass / 0.8 Iron; Mk III 3.15 h (Neutron Capacitor 1.2 h + Cells + Control Units) / 4 Tar / 11 Glass + Neutronium, RE Core, Steam, so Mk III is never ordered (the loader still uses it). Every Mk II Terraformer writes the same two orders for the fleet (`fleet_size()` = fresh, non-stopped tier-2 `plant.terraformer` entries + itself): **need** = `NEED_BATCHES = 10` batches per machine at the NEED band of a `fabricator.orders` entry (requester `plant_terraformer`), **backlog** = `BACKLOG_BATCHES = 100` batches per machine at the STOCK band of the same entry (network stock, idle Fabricator time only). Both net against `SourceCache.network_stock()` (Drone Depots included), so units at a remote fab site count and the local request hauls them in. Both capped by what the rest of the ladder needs (`remaining_forage()` over `PLANTS_BANDS` from `CRAFTED_SUPPORT_FIRST_PHASE` = Fertilizer 4, Growth Accelerant 5); Growth Accelerant uses the same orders once required. 2 Mk II machines: need 20 / backlog 200 `growth_accelerant`, need 18 / backlog 180 `fertilizer_mk2`. The whole ladder needs ~23,000 potency (~770 Mk II). Cleared on `complete`; a `needs_mk2` Mk I machine leaves them alone. Poll `POLL_INTERVAL_S = 10`. Telemetry `plant.terraformer` (§4).
- **Plants finish line** (`plant_terraformer.forage_to_go()`): each machine publishes `batch_km2` = `km2_rate()` × `CYCLE_H = 3.0` of its running batch (kept while `km2_rate()` reads 0, e.g. paused; reseeded from telemetry on restart). Forage still to load fleet-wide = `remaining_forage()` − (own + every other in-flight batch's `batch_km2` × (1 − progress)) / current phase's km² per Forage; other batches count only while their entry is younger than `COMMIT_FRESH_TICKS = 600` (a stopped script pauses its batch). At 0 the running batches reach 5,000,000 km²: no loading, requests and Fabricator orders cleared, an idle machine stays off, a running one finishes its batch. Below a full batch: the next batch's Forage is cut to what is left (`cap_batch()`; support items stay at full-batch amounts) and support requests to `request_batches()` = ceil(left / full batch), ≤ `SUPPORT_REQUEST_BATCHES`; Fabricator orders cap their Forage left the same way. An unreadable `remaining()` never counts as the finish line. Two idle machines loading in the same poll can still both start; the overshoot is at most one batch each.
- **Plants completion** (`plants_retire.py` `PlantsRetirement`, orchestrator Automation storage pass): at `complete` each Terraformer script `eject()`s its holders to `best_unload_target()` (home falls back to Inventory, remote to a local Drone Depot), then its script ends. Once any `plant.terraformer` entry reads `complete`, the pass walks every deployed `plant_terraformer`: not yet `complete` → left alone (card: "finishing"); holders not empty → `run_control.start()` if its script is stopped (card: "emptying"); empty → `computer.undeploy()` (kit + tier pack to Inventory, not sold), telemetry entry pruned. Undeploy goes through `building_ops.undeploy()` (autoplay.md, building executor): transient refusals are retried next pass, other refusals warn once per status. Entries of Terraformers no longer deployed are pruned (on an empty discovery only those older than `STATUS_STALE_TICKS`). Ejected Forage/support items sit in Warehouses until haulers and consolidation clear them.
